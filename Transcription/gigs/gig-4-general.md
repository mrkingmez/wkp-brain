## Description (by my estimate this is over 1,200 characters; trim once you confirm the real limit)

Whatever you recorded, an interview, a meeting, a lecture, a voicemail, a family story, a video you need the words from, I turn it into text.

What you get:
- A transcript of the whole recording with time stamps.
- Speaker labels (Speaker 1, Speaker 2, or the names you give me).
- A proofread pass against the audio, so names and terms are right.

How I work: speech-recognition software makes the first draft and I read it against the recording and correct it.

What this gig covers: English speech, clear to moderately noisy recordings, one to four speakers, MP3, WAV, MP4, MOV and links.

What it does not cover:
- Heavy accents or very fast speech where I cannot make out the words. I mark those spots with a time stamp instead of guessing.
- Very poor audio (loud music, wind, phone calls with dropouts). Send a 2-minute sample and I will tell you before you pay.
- More than 4 speakers or heavy crosstalk.
- Languages other than English.
- Certified, legal-court or medical transcription.
- Church sermons, podcasts and video subtitle files: see my other gigs, which add outlines, chapters and caption files.
