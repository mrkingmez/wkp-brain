# Etsy Store — WarriorKingDesigns

## What This Is
Print-on-demand store (puzzles + canvas wall art) on Printify. All artwork is AI-generated by Zac. ~57 of ~70 live listings are considered genuinely good; the rest is filler from earlier posting. Zero ad budget — pure organic/SEO play.

## Current Reality (as of 7/24 interview)
- No sales in months. Lifetime: 4 reviews (two 5-star, one 3-star unexplained, one 1-star).
- The 1-star is from a January puzzle order — international (Czech Republic) delivery issue caused by DHL, not a quality problem. Zac already left a public response clarifying this.
- Traffic last 7 days: 28 views, 14 visits (up 300% YoY, still low absolute volume). 8 of 14 visits came from Etsy's own marketing/SEO — tags are working to get found. 4 from on-app browsing.
- 1 abandoned cart, 4 favorites in the same window. No completed sale to point to yet.
- Store started as a t-shirt shop, later rebranded to puzzles + wall art. Some old reviews are from the t-shirt era and not representative of current catalog.

## Diagnosis (Zac's own read, confirmed in interview)
Not a quality problem — POD quality is table stakes, everyone's baseline is similar ("teardrop in an ocean"). The real levers are: (1) too few reviews to build trust, (2) catalog spread thin across too many themes instead of doubling down on what's proven, (3) pricing already at the floor — can't undercut dropshippers who have lower COGS, so pricing is not the lever to pull.

## Content Lines & What's Actually Working
- **Military** — top performer for PUZZLES. Views and favorites concentrate here. Lean in hard.
- **Landscapes** — top performer for WALL ART / canvas specifically. Keep for canvas, pull back on landscape puzzles.
- **Christian** — third line, still developing, no strong signal yet either way.
- **Fantasy/Sci-Fi** — mentioned in projects.md as in development, not yet a proven category.

## Active Plan (locked in 7/24)
1. **Reviews**: include a polite ask-for-review note in every shipped order going forward (not incentivized — Etsy prohibits that). Goal: bury the 1-star under volume of good reviews.
2. **Focus**: prioritize new military puzzle designs and landscape wall art/canvas designs over other themes. Don't abandon the full range, but stop spreading effort evenly.
3. **Cadence**: ~60 designs already finished and ready to post. Monday = 3 new physical listings (puzzle/canvas, military + landscape focus). Wednesday = 2 new digital printable listings.
4. **Pricing**: hold at current levels. Margin is already thin because Zac's COGS are higher than dropship-only competitors; cutting price further would go negative.
