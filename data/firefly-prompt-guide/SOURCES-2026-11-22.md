## Google (Veo 3.1, Veo 3.1 Fast, Veo 3.1 Lite)

**G1. Vertex AI "Prompt guide" for Veo and Gemini Omni Flash.** https://docs.cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/video/video-gen-prompt-guide . RETRIEVED (fetcher), no page date shown.
- "Breaking your idea down into key components is the most effective way to guide" the model. "You don't need to use all elements in every prompt."
- Subject: "Specificity helps avoid generic outputs." Action: "Action brings the subject to life, describes movements, interactions, and subtle expressions."
- Temporal elements: "Temporal elements affect the flow of time in a video, which you can use to highlight changes even in short clips."
- Camera angles, moves and lens effects are listed (POV, static, dolly, handheld and so on). Caveats: "Some advanced camera angles are not officially supported." "Some advanced camera lenses are not officially supported."
- Negative prompts: do not use "no" or "don't". "Describe what you don't want to see." Its example is the nouns "wall, frame".
- Audio: "Clearly specify if you want audio." "We recommend that you use separate sentences in your prompt to describe the audio." Types: sound effects, ambient noise, dialogue.
- NOT on this page (the fetcher said so): reference images, length tips, timestamps, first and last frames. Those are on other pages below.

**G2. Google Cloud blog, "Ultimate prompting guide for Veo 3.1", published October 15, 2025.** https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/ultimate-prompting-guide-for-veo-3-1 . RETRIEVED (fetcher).
- Formula: "[Cinematography] + [Subject] + [Action] + [Context] + [Style & Ambiance]".
- Timestamps: "By assigning actions to timed segments, you can efficiently create a full scene with multiple distinct shots". Format shown: `[00:00-00:02] Medium shot ... [00:02-00:04] Reverse shot ...` with `SFX:` inside a segment.
- First and Last Frame: "Generate a natural video transition between a provided start image and end image, complete with audio." "In your prompt, describe the transition and the audio you want."
- Ingredients to Video: "Provide reference images of a scene, character, object, or style to maintain a consistent aesthetic across multiple shots." Example prompt names the role of each image ("Using the provided images for the detective, the woman, and the office setting...").
- Audio: dialogue in quotation marks, "SFX: ...", "Ambient noise: ...".
- Negatives: "specify 'a desolate landscape with no buildings or roads' instead of 'no man-made structures'." No other negative advice.

**G3. Vertex AI model page, Veo 3.1 Generate / Fast / Lite.** https://docs.cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/models/veo/3-1-generate . RETRIEVED (fetcher).

## Runway (Gen-4.5)

**R1. Runway Help Center, "Text to Video Prompting Guide".** https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/42460036199443 . RAW (downloaded by curl and read), "currently optimized for the newest Gen-4.5 model", no date.
- "Effective prompts describe what appears in the frame and how those elements move through the scene using direct, clear language."
- "Structure and order are far less important than clearly conveying an idea and reducing ambiguity." Starter structure: "[Camera] shot of [a subject/object] [action] in [environment]. [Supporting component descriptions]".
- "Overly long prompts can create conflicting requests and may limit the creative freedom that produces surprising, dynamic shots."
- "Is the beginning of my prompt prioritized? No. The order in which elements are introduced in a prompt do not matter."
- Timestamp prompting: "it effectively guides the general sequence and timing" but "may not be perfectly precise"; "pairing timestamp prompts with a natural language prompt for the most control"; "consider how long an action would reasonably take to complete".
- "If a component isn't present or executed in an initial generation, try iterating your prompt to reinforce it through natural language."

**R2. Runway Academy prompt guide.** https://academy.runwayml.com/prompt-guide . RETRIEVED (fetcher).
- "Describe what you want to see rather than what you don't want. Most models respond better to positive instructions."
- "Be specific and concrete. Instead of abstract concepts, use clear, observable descriptions."
- "Make sure your prompt elements don't contradict each other."
- "Starting simple lets you add one element at a time and see what each change does."
- Image to video: "Effective image to video text prompts focus almost exclusively on motion." "ensure that the input image is high quality and free of visual artifacts."
- Unwanted cuts: add "Continuous, seamless shot". Less camera motion: "The camera must start and end on the exact same frame to create a perfect loop."

**R3. Runway "Image to Video Prompting Guide" and the Gen-4.5 help pages.** NOT RETRIEVED (403 and a Cloudflare challenge). SNIPPET only.

## ByteDance / BytePlus (Seedance 2.5)

**B1. "Dreamina Seedance 2.5 series prompt guide", BytePlus ModelArk, last updated November 19, 2026.** https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/ModelArk/doubao-seedance-2-5-prompt-guide . RAW (the page body is embedded as JSON; I extracted 104,238 characters of text and read it). This is the maker's own guide.
- "Treat Seedance 2.5 as a visual content producer, and write structured prompts with a visual storytelling mindset."
- Structure: "One-Sentence Summary: Subject + Location + Event + Genre/Style + Camera movement..." then a "Detailed Plot Description" as a shot sequence or timeline, then "Additional Notes: Add any visual details that should remain consistent throughout".
- "Use positive descriptions whenever possible. Negative constraints are supported for subtitles and audio control, such as 'no subtitles' and 'no BGM.'"
- Timestamps: "Use 1-second intervals as the basic unit". "If too little plot is specified within a given time range, the model may improvise more freely." "If too much content is packed into a given time range, the result may contain excessive cuts or omit parts of the plot." "It is not recommended to use timestamps to control high-frequency actions". "Pay attention to timeline continuity and avoid gaps such as '0-3s... 5-6s...'."
- First and last frames: "Prioritize setting the image role through parameters as first_frame or last_frame. Note that this method locks the output video's aspect ratio". Or in text: "Image 1 is the first frame." "Image 3 is the first frame, and Image 5 is the last frame."
- Keyframes: state "Use Images X to X in order as keyframes."
- "Specify the role of each reference asset clearly". Upload order = numbering (Image 1, Video 1, Audio 1).
- Camera: shot sizes, movements and angles "can be written directly", including "first-person perspective" and "handheld shake". Niche terms: write "term + descriptive explanation".
- "Avoid inconsistencies in the prompt: Make sure the prompt does not contain contradictions or unreasonable camera movement and motion design." (storyboard section)
- Actions: "Give priority to general descriptions". Text: "Text in the video may be misspelled."
- Limits: up to 30 seconds, 30 images / 10 videos / 10 audio clips for Seedance 2.5 (10 / 5 / 5 for Flash). Any aspect ratio between 0.4 and 2.5 by input asset.
- NOT RETRIEVED: the separate Seedance 2.0 guide (navigation text only).
