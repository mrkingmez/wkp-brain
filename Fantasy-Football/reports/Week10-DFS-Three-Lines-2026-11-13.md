## What this is, in 5 lines

1. Three DraftKings classic lineups, because you have $3 and three entries reserved. 7-game slate, 14 teams, $50,000 cap.
2. Structure follows your friend's process: chalk pool (5 or more players from it), QB gate, QB stack, no more than 2 bring-backs, nobody forced in.
3. His file gives projection, ownership and implied totals. Our simulator (20,000 draws of Week 10) gives ceilings and how players move together. Defense rank against QBs is ours, from nflverse.
4. Every QB shown, with the gate result. One thing to know first: no QB under 10 percent owned passes the gate, so "low-owned" for a QB is set at under 15 percent (my assumption, details below).
5. This is the early set. Refresh at 11:30 AM ET Sunday when inactives and the new file land, compare with your friend's three at 12:00, decide at 12:15, enter before 1:00.

**Rule R1, when our QB and the friend's QB disagree.**

1. If his QB fails a gate item I can verify (under $5,000, not a confirmed starter, implied under 21, opposing defense in our top 8), keep ours. He may know something the gate does not, so I flag it to you but I do not copy it.
2. If his QB passes the gate and is not one of our three, swap him into lineup C only. Run `python dfs_build.py --qb-c "NAME"`. Keep A and B as they are. Reason: the QB is where the money is won or lost, you have three entries, and splitting across both views is the cheapest hedge.
3. If both QBs pass and you must choose one for C, take the one with the higher sim 90th percentile.
4. If his QB is unconfirmed as a starter at 12:15, it loses, always.
5. If he and we use the same QB, nothing to resolve.
6. This is a 50/50 style call when both pass and are close. It is yours. I present the case for both and you decide.
