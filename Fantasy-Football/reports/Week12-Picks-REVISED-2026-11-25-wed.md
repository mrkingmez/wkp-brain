## What we learned (short)

1. **The card is against the spread, not straight up.** Checked on 800 past NFL picks: 27 yellow picks were games the picked team lost outright but still covered. So "pick the better team" does not win by itself. The pick has to beat the number.
2. **Our method WAS the problem, just not the way it looked.** The earlier cards picked the card underdog in 18 of 20 games ("NO EDGE, pick the underdog"). Underdogs cover about as often as favorites (dogs 48.2%, favorites 49.4%), so that default has no edge. It also loses outright 66 percent of the time, which is why it feels bad and why one bad QB or blowout sinks a card.
3. **The one signal that held up: line value.** When your card line was 1 point or more WORSE than the market for your pick, you hit 37.3% (63 of 169). When it was 1 point or more BETTER, 55.1% (87 of 158). Same-line picks 58.3% (98 of 168). So the frozen card line only helps when it beats the market.
4. **Hit rate is a coin flip overall:** 48.9% ATS on 800 NFL picks across 2022, 2024, 2025 and 2026 (break-even on a 20 pick card is about 52). 2026 NFL so far is 80 of 147 (54.4%), Week 11 was 6 of 13.
5. **New rule used here:** take the side with real line value (1 point or better than market) or, when the line is the same, take the better team with the healthier quarterback. Underdog only when the line value or an injury supports it. Result: 11 favorites, 9 underdogs, 9 picks changed.

Honest limits: 800 picks gives about plus or minus 3.5 points of noise on any overall rate, and the splits (n of 23 to 220) are weaker still. Only two findings are strong enough to act on: the line-value split (an 18-point gap on about 160 each) and the fact that underdog-by-default is not an edge. 2022 looks broken (37%, also the card-game baseline looks off), so I kept it in the totals but do not lean on it. Excluding 2022, the rate is 51.5% (336 of 652). College was not checked for line value.

Does the frozen-card-line-versus-market approach work? Only in the direction of the table above: it works when the market has moved away from your card line, and it is a trap when the market moved toward the other side you picked against. It says nothing about which side is the better team.

## Standing rule proposed (for Zac to approve)

Pick order for every ATS card: (1) line value of 1 point or more versus the current market wins the pick; (2) with no line value, take the better team (market win chance, healthier QB, injuries) and the favorite is fine; (3) take an underdog only on line value, a hook, or a clear injury or QB edge on the other side; (4) never default to the underdog; (5) rerun after Wednesday QB news and 6 PM ET on lock day; (6) log every card and grade it the same week in the ledger, color files included.
