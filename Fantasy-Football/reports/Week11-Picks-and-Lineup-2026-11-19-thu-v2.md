## Close calls (card)

- **Why so many underdogs, honestly.** Against the spread is close to a coin flip on every game. This week the only tested edge (a frozen card line against a moving market) points to the underdog on 7 games and the favorite on 2 (Houston, Carolina). On the 11 games with no line value at all, each pick below has a reason, and most of those reasons also point to the dog: dogs of 11.5 have covered 52 percent at that closing number, low totals in rain shrink big spreads, and the play-by-play sim sides with the dog. The play-by-play sim is a weak signal: it squeezes favorites toward even, and its backtest (269 games) found no information beyond the market (56 percent on 84 games when it took the dog by 3 or more points, inside noise).
- **Game 12, Tampa Bay +6.5 or Detroit -6.5.** The play-by-play sim has Detroit by 8.1 if Jalon Daniels starts and Detroit by 6.4 if Mayfield starts. Bowles decides Friday, after the card locks. Detroit News 11/19 says Mayfield is trending to play, and the market moved to Tampa Bay today (7 to 6.5, total 44 to 45.5). Case for Detroit: if Mayfield sits, the fair line is past 7. Case for Tampa Bay: home favorites of 4 to 6.5 covered only 45 percent from 2016 to 2025 (390 games, closing lines), and the market is leaning Mayfield plays. **Coin flip. Take Tampa Bay.**
- **Game 4, Colorado +3.5 or Cincinnati -3.5 (college).** All three ratings (SP+, FPI, Elo) have Cincinnati by about 6.6, 3 points past the card. The market sits at Cincinnati -3, so the card's 3.5 is a half point of value for Colorado. Ratings are a sanity check only (Elo added nothing over the market in the 2025 test, 762 games). **Coin flip, genuinely split. Taking Colorado on the market; Cincinnati is the pick if you trust the ratings more than the books.**
- **Game 10, New Orleans +11.5.** Saints C Erik McCoy and RT Taliese Fuaga are listed doubtful (SI) and Shough took 8 sacks in two games. That argues for Chicago. Against it: rain likely and gusts to 24 in Chicago, Caleb Williams threw 4 interceptions last game, and 11.5-point dogs cover about 52 percent. **Coin flip, New Orleans.**
- **Game 16, Jets +3.5.** Geno Smith (ankle, limited Wednesday) says he plans to start; Glenn has not confirmed. If rookie Cade Klubnik starts, the hook still helps but the game gets worse for the Jets. **Lean Jets on the hook.**
- **Favorites you could take instead, if you want a less dog-heavy card:** Detroit -6.5 (if you think Mayfield sits), Cincinnati -3.5 (college ratings), BYU -5.5 (ratings have BYU by 6.6), Buffalo -16.5 (Miami starts backup Kyle McCord and has three DTs on IR; but the market already priced Willis being out).
