# Format - Hasn't Seen It
 
## What this is
Matt watches a film he has genuinely never seen. Zac has.
First-reaction format.
 
## The rule that protects it
Matt must NOT be briefed beforehand. No plot summary, no 'watch for
this'. The value is a real first reaction. Once he is prepped, the
format is dead and the audience can tell.
 
## Structure
1. Zac sets up why this one, without spoiling
2. Reactions during / after
3. Zac fills in context Matt missed
4. Matt's verdict, cold
 
## Zac's job
Do not lead the witness. Do not react ahead of him.
The audience is watching Matt, not you.
