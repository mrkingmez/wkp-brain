# Format - Recorded Review

## What this is
Matt and Zac review a film or show. Transcript comes from Premiere.
NEVER transcribe these.

## Structure
There isn't a fixed one. Two guys talking, and it goes where it goes.
Do not impose a running order and do not expect one.
What is reliably present: a hook somewhere near the top, and a
verdict somewhere near the end. Everything between varies.

## Voices
Matt: Winter Wolf. Sharp. Verdict first, then reasoning.
Zac: KingZ. Warm. Frames and connects.
Tone benchmark: Spider-Noir. Gritty, direct, not sanitized.

## Scores
Scores are allowed. The scale varies by episode and that is fine.
Use whatever scale was actually given. Never invent one, never
convert between scales, never add a score that was not said.

## Rules
No em dashes. Handles only, never real names.

## Output
Full upload package: SEO title, GEO description with chapters, tags,
hashtags, thumbnail concept, 5-7 shorts candidates.
