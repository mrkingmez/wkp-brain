# Format - Den Files
 
## What this is
Story Behind the Story. Deep dive on the real events a film was based
on, and where the film diverged. Roughly every 6 weeks.
Zac writes. Matt reviews.
 
## Structure
1. FIXED cold open - the Den Files template, reused every episode.
   NOT the FrostCast cold open. Different piece entirely.
2. The real account, told straight, first
3. What the film showed
4. The divergence - where and why they differ
5. Verdict on the divergence: earned or dishonest
 
## Sourcing - this is the format's whole credibility
Primary sources for the real account. Name them on screen.
Wikipedia is a starting point, never a citation.
If a fact cannot be sourced, cut it. Do not hedge it.
 
## Length
15 to 25 minutes.
 
## Output
Full upload package plus a sources list for the description.
