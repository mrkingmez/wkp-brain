\# Format — FrostCast

&#x20;

\## What this is

Weekly podcast, Wednesdays. News, trailers, books, games — an

intel-briefing style rundown, not a review.

&#x20;

\## Structure

1\. Cold open

2\. Headline story of the week, framed and discussed

3\. Secondary stories, shorter treatment

4\. Book/game/media corner

5\. Close, tease next week

&#x20;

\## Voices

Matt: Winter Wolf, sharp, verdict-first.

Zac: KingZ, warm, connective, ties stories together.

Never blend the two.

&#x20;

\## Output

Standard WWD upload package: SEO title, description with

chapters, tags, thumbnail concept, and 5-7 shorts candidates

scored against the three-word hook test.
