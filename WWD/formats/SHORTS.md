# Format - Shorts and Reels
 
## Selection criteria (Opus Clip v2.1)
1. THREE-WORD HOOK TEST. The first three words must earn the next
   three seconds. If they do not, the clip is dead. This is the
   single highest-weighted criterion.
2. On-screen hook text, separate from the spoken words
3. A mid-clip retention beat - something lands around halfway
4. Ending plus loop - the end should invite a replay
 
## Scoring
Score every candidate 1-10 against the four criteria above.
Ship 5-7 per source video. Below 6, cut it - a weak Short costs
channel reach, it does not just underperform on its own.
 
## Per clip, deliver
- Filename
- On-screen hook text
- Caption and hashtags per platform (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook)
- Score with a one-line reason
 
## Posting schedule
YouTube first. Instagram +45 min. Facebook +90 min.
One clip per day.
