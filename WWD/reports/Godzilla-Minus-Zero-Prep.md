## Release facts
- Japanese title: Gojira Mainasu Zero. Writer, director and VFX: Takashi Yamazaki. Direct sequel to Godzilla Minus One (2023). Set in 1949.
- Studio and distribution: Toho. North America by GKIDS (Toho subsidiary). VFX by Shirogumi. Cinematographer Kozo Shibasaki, composer Naoki Sato, editor Ryuji Miyajima.
- Dates: NYFF premiere Sep 26, 2026. Japan Nov 3. North America Nov 6 wide, IMAX. IMAX early-access showings Nov 3 in some US/UK/Mexico theaters (Wikipedia).
- Runtime: 2h 15m (Rotten Tomatoes).
- Rating: R, first R-rated Godzilla film in the US, for "some violent content/bloody images" (Rotten Tomatoes and Wikipedia agree on R).
- First Japanese production filmed for IMAX (Wikipedia). Shot entirely in Japan per the official press release, though The Hollywood Reporter claimed New Zealand and Norway.
- Budget: not stated. Minus One cost under US$15M, and Yamazaki said this one is higher (Wikipedia). No number for this film.

## Plot beats (SPOILERS, internal use, from Wikipedia)
1. 1949. Shikishima and Noriko raise adopted daughter Akiko. Noda's bureau prepares for future threats.
2. A dragon-like creature hits Matsumoto City, an alien organism that arrived in a meteorite. Scientists name it Ghidorah. Murakami is a Hiroshima survivor.
3. Air attack on dormant Ghidorah with a fuel-air bomb and lightning-rod harpoons. It seems dead, then evolves into three-headed King Ghidorah with a gravity beam.
4. Godzilla resurfaces. Murakami secretly wants to lure him to New York and use a "Resonance Wave" device to avenge his family.
5. Ginza trap with liquid nitrogen and fuel. Shikishima pilots a Kawanishi H8K seaplane. The monsters fight. Noriko's G-Cell mutation is tied to Godzilla.
6. Noda dies in the trap. Ghidorah's severed head absorbs G-Cells and gets a heat ray. Godzilla wins.
7. Ending: US military told to stand down and preserve Godzilla. Murakami is taken into US custody. Shikishima goes into hiding with Noriko and takes Akiko.
Public-copy rule: tease the Ghidorah matchup, the sequel hook and the Ginza setting only. Never the deaths, the ending, or who wins.

## Critical angles from the reviews (Wikipedia summaries, paraphrased)
- Praise: effects judged better than Minus One, scope, disaster plus horror intensity (The Guardian), seamless CGI (THR).
- Debate: is it better than Minus One? Critics split.
- Reservations: human story less tight than Minus One (Inverse), later passages sillier and contrived (TheWrap), ending weaker (SCMP).
