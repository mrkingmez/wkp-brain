## 1. The villain

**Motive.** He wants the talking to stop. He believes every argument should end with one official last word, so he seals what keeps people arguing. He seals objects only, never people (originality: sealing mouths echoes The Matrix and the Buffy "Hush" episode, so that is cut). Nothing is ever spoiled, and no envelope is ever opened on the page.

**Look.** Tall, narrow, slow. Long oilskin courier's coat, flat brimmed cap, satchel of sealed black envelopes, gloves that drip hot red wax. His head is a cast-iron and brass wax-seal stamp with one engraved mark on its face, a single full stop. No screen, no glow, no eyes. Where he touches, red wax pools and sets. He never speaks: his voice is the thud of the stamp and a wax-shaped caption box.

**Changes made after originality:**
1. Envelope stamp is now FINAL WORD, not ENDING ENCLOSED. On a movie channel "ending" reads as a movie's ending and brings the spoiler read back. FINAL WORD matches his motive.
2. The seal face carries the full stop mark, the caption box is a wax blob shape (not a jagged ragged box), and he is staged on the stairwell landing at a rusted mailbox below the Den door, not a rainy rooftop. That moves him away from The Question and Rorschach.
3. "Mouths" cut from the motive.

**Kept:** he never wins, nothing opens, wax creeping up the mic and the "talk louder" payoff.

**Name.** Originality found no villain called The Last Word. Clearance notes it is a common title (an MSNBC news show, a 2017 film, a short story), and Marvel and DC each have a minor villain called The Word. Fine inside his caption box. A trademark search is needed before it becomes a series name or logo. Fallback names if Zac or Matt want one: THE POSTMASTER, MR. FINAL.

## 7. Character bible for the art team (one page, visual description only)

Palette: black and blood red base. Cold blue is Winter Wolf's eyes. Red glow is KingZ. Violet and cold white is Oracle. Wax red is the villain. Torch amber lives inside the Den only. The city outside is red, black and rain gray. Match the EP094 page for the hosts.

**KingZ.** Masked face, red eyes. Crown cap. Hoodie with KINGZ in dripping red lettering. Always holding something (mug, game console, case). Open posture: arms wide, leaning toward the reader, pointing. Takes the most room in a panel.

**Winter Wolf.** A wolf. Black hooded jacket, WINTER WOLF across the back, fur collar, blue eyes, the only cold blue in the frame besides Oracle. Still posture: arms crossed, a clawed hand flat on things, leaning back. Small gestures.

**Oracle (look open).**
- Option 1: a glowing, faceless circle carved into the Den table. Pale cold-white light on deep violet. The carved lines brighten when she speaks. No body, no face.
- Option 2: a hanging lantern of old frosted glass over the table, violet flame inside that flickers when she speaks. No face, no figure.
Balloons: round, tailless, thin clean serif, pale text on violet. Never a face, never a silhouette that suggests a real person.

**The Den.** A hideout in a brick stairwell hut on a rooftop above a rainy city. Heavy door with a torch-lit gap under it, a rusted mailbox on the landing below. Inside: one long wooden table, mugs, mics, scattered notes, torches on brick, a WINTER WOLF'S DEN banner, rain on the windows. Warm amber inside, cold red and gray outside.

**The Last Word.** Tall, narrow, slow. Long oilskin courier's coat, flat brimmed cap, satchel of sealed black envelopes, gloves dripping hot red wax. Head is a cast-iron and brass wax-seal stamp, worn, with a single engraved full stop on its face. No screen, no glow, no eyes. Red wax pools and sets wherever he touches. Captions from him are wax-shaped boxes. Never drawn winning, never opening an envelope. No real postal colors, patches or logos.

Never draw: any other company's characters or logos, real newspaper mastheads, Oracle's real name, a recognizable console shape, a screen-headed figure, crested seals.
