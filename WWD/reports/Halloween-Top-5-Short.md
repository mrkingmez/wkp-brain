## How to put it together (Premiere, step by step)
1. Open Premiere. **File > New > Sequence**. In the Sequence Presets pick **Digital SLR > 1080p > DSLR 1080p30**, then **Sequence Settings**, set **Frame Size** to **1080 horizontal, 1920 vertical**, **OK**.
2. **Ctrl+I**, pick `Halloween Top 5 - VO Ready.mp4`, **Open**. Drag it onto the timeline.
3. Record your voice in a quiet room (phone voice memo works). Say the lines in the table above. Import the file and drop it on **A2**.
4. Slide the voice so each line starts at its time. The captions on screen are the guide. If a line runs long, trim a breath, do not speed it up.
5. Leave the video's own sound on **A1**. Click your voice clip, open **Essential Sound** (Window menu), tag it **Dialogue**, click **Auto-Match**. Click the video clip's audio, tag it **Music**, turn on **Ducking** so the sound drops under your voice.
6. Play it with headphones. If a hit covers a word, lower **A1** by 3 dB at that spot.
7. **Ctrl+M** to export. Format **H.264**, preset **Match Source - High bitrate**, **Export**.

If you want it quicker: skip steps 5 and 6, just lower the video's audio to about -12 dB and drop the voice on top.
