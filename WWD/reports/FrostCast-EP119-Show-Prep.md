## 1. Godzilla is coming!! Rogues gallery, expectations

**What happened**
Godzilla Minus Zero, the sequel to Godzilla Minus One, opens in Japan on Tuesday 11/3 through Toho and goes wide in North America on Friday 11/6 through GKIDS. Takashi Yamazaki returns to write, direct and handle the visual effects. The latest trailer has been read by several outlets as confirming King Ghidorah as the other monster, though coverage disagrees on how clearly he was shown.

**Why it matters**
Minus One was a surprise awards-level hit, so the question is whether a sequel with a monster rival can keep the same human-scale drama. The rogues gallery angle is the big one. Bringing in Ghidorah risks turning a grounded film into a monster mash.

**Numbers**
- Japan release 11/3/26, North America 11/6/26.
- Domestic opening projection of $25M to $35M (Box Office Theory, a forecast, not a result).
- Godzilla Minus One opened to $11.4M domestic, for comparison.
- Separate franchise note: Godzilla x Kong: Supernova is dated March 26, 2027 (Legendary MonsterVerse).

**History**
Toho revealed the title at Godzilla Fest 2025 in Tokyo. Minus Zero is not part of the Legendary MonsterVerse and connects only to Minus One. The MonsterVerse has its own Ghidorah, which Monarch labels "Monster Zero." Some outlets say the title nods to that name, which is speculation.

**Colour**
- Same team behind both films, effects included.
- The title overlap with Monster Zero is a fan theory, not a Toho statement.
- The film lands two days after the show, so expectations are the only thing to talk about live.

**Quick hits**
- Japan 11/3, US 11/6
- Yamazaki writes, directs, does VFX
- Ghidorah as rival: reported, not uniformly confirmed
- $25M to $35M opening forecast vs $11.4M for Minus One
- Not the MonsterVerse

**Why it matters**
Two long careers end at once, Captain Picard and Charles Xavier, and the Marvel question is now how Xavier is handled in Secret Wars. The note on the list says "will appear in Secret Wars." That is not confirmed (see below). The tribute clip already covered the news itself, so the live angle is the legacy and the Picard season 3 send-off.

- Xavier first appeared in 2000's X-Men. Stewart came back as an older Xavier in Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness, in a different-universe version, which is why he is tied to Doomsday and Secret Wars talk.

**Colour**

- One outlet reports Christopher Abbott as the new Xavier in the incoming X-Men class. One source only, unverified.

**Quick hits**

## 3. X-Men and the Doomsday pile-on

Seven sub-items from the list, each sourced separately.

**What happened**
- **Ben Wang as Iceman.** Deadline reported (November 2026) that Ben Wang is circling or in talks for Iceman in Marvel's X-Men reboot, directed by Jake Schreier, dated May 5, 2028. Reports differ: Variety and Deadline say "circling" or "in talks," while another outlet and one trade report say he has joined. Marvel has not commented. Deadline also names Gabriel LaBelle, Jackson Kelly and Rhenzy Feliz among others who tested. Wang's credits include The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping and Karate Kid: Legends.
- **Defenders in Secret Wars.** One insider (James Mack) reported the Defenders will play a significant role in Secret Wars. No official Marvel confirmation. Jessica Jones, Luke Cage and Iron Fist have no announced returns.
- **Ghost Rider rumor.** Years of rumors, some tied to Nicolas Cage in Secret Wars, others to a post-Secret Wars project. Reports disagree on which Ghost Rider: Johnny Blaze, Danny Ketch or Robbie Reyes. No confirmed plan found. Some of the sourcing dates from 2025 and early 2026.
- **Storm.** Maya Boyd was announced as Storm by Kevin Feige at D23 on August 14, 2026. She is a Broadway actress (Merrily We Roll Along, & Juliet) and a University of Michigan musical theatre graduate. X-Men is her feature film debut. The "training with the Daredevil crew" detail was not found.
- **Final trailer by 11/19.** Rumor only. One insider says before November 19 (the GTA 6 date), another puts it between November 10 and 25. No Marvel or Disney announcement.
- **Doom popcorn bucket.** The glowing Doctor Doom head bucket seen online is from Cinemark Brazil (reported presale price R$299). In the US, Regal has a "Doom's Throne" bucket and Sentinel cup, AMC has an Avengers/X-Men/Dr. Doom bucket set and a Doom collectible with large popcorn, and Cinemark lists an LED Doom statue bucket. One outlet says Regal, AMC and Harkins start selling December 17. As of mid-October, one report said no US chain had announced a Doom bucket, so check which image Winter Wolf means.
- **Doomsday presales vs Supergirl.** Headlines say Doomsday presales already top Supergirl's entire run. The actual numbers: more than $125M in worldwide advance sales (third-party tracking, reported by Comic Basics, cited by Collider on 11/2) against Supergirl's $126.4M worldwide total. That is roughly level, not clearly past it. Forbes was also cited at about $125M. Ticket sales opened August 14. Spider-Man: Brand New Day holds the all-time presale record at $150M. Global Box Office projects a $260M to $300M domestic opening, a forecast.

**Why it matters**
Marvel is stacking its own pre-release hype while the DCU stalls (topic 5). Presales are not box office, but the comparison is the story of the week. The X-Men casting also shows the post-Doomsday direction.

**Numbers**
- Doomsday: December 18, 2026. Secret Wars: December 17, 2027. X-Men: May 5, 2028.
- Presales: more than $125M worldwide (third-party) vs Supergirl $126.4M.
- Record presale: $150M (Brand New Day).
- Opening forecast $260M to $300M domestic (Global Box Office, forecast).

**History**
Shawn Ashmore played Iceman in the 2000s X-Men films. The Defenders miniseries aired on Netflix in 2017. Daredevil: Born Again season 3 has finished filming and was reported as slated for March 2027, with no official date.

**Colour**
- Doomsday's presale tickets opened the same day as the D23 Storm announcement, August 14.
- Germany reportedly sold 118,000 Doomsday tickets on day one, more than Endgame, per one report (not verified further).
- Supergirl's Lobo bike bucket drew fan complaints for holding very little popcorn.

**Quick hits**

## 5. DCU standstill per Nov ? report

**What happened**
A Cosmic Book News report on November 2, 2026 says the DCU has ground to a halt after the Skydance-Warner Bros. Discovery merger. It says the merger closed October 6, with David Ellison now owning DC, and that Gunn and Safran's contracts expire in April 2027 with no extension announced. Nothing is in production at DC Studios right now. The sourcing is thin. The article is itself the report, leaning on anonymous insiders plus on-record comments from Gunn, Safran and HBO's Casey Bloys.

**Why it matters**
A "standstill" and a "pipeline between phases" look the same from outside. Earlier reporting, about a month old, says Paramount wants to keep the creative teams. Gunn said around August nothing is on hold. Both can be true at once, which is the debate.

**Numbers**
- Man of Tomorrow wrapped filming August 26, opens July 9, 2027.
- Superman (2025): about $616M worldwide. Supergirl: $126.4M worldwide.
- Gunn and Safran contracts: April 2027, three months before Man of Tomorrow.
- Lanterns season 1 ended October 4. Safran says a season 2 decision comes by year's end.

**History**
The report calls Supergirl the lowest-grossing DC film. The Authority is described as shelved and The Brave and the Bold as an unmade script. The Waller series was never greenlit (Gunn confirmed on his podcast). The Peoples v. Gorilla Grodd has wrapped with no date.

**Colour**
- Talent claims about the Supergirl and Superman leads' deals are attributed to unnamed insiders only, so do not repeat as fact.
- Ellison reportedly told Gunn and Safran to "keep doing what you're doing." A reported meeting outcome, no new deal followed.
- No official Paramount or DC confirmation of either side was found.

**Quick hits**
- Report dated 11/2/26, merger closed 10/6
- Gunn/Safran contracts end April 2027
- Nothing in production at DC Studios right now
- Man of Tomorrow 7/9/27
- Insiders vs Gunn's "nothing on hold"
