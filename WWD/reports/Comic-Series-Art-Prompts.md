## How to use this

- **Model.** Firefly Image model, the newest on Zac's menu (exact name not verified, the saved menu note covers video models only). It is the commercially safe pick, use it first for every prompt.
- **Partner models (Nano Banana Pro or similar): UNVERIFIED on Zac's account.** Only worth trying for multi-reference work (attaching 2 to 4 images at once to hold a face or outfit steady). If present, use the same prompt text. Check the model menu first. Partner models carry different commercial terms, so wwd-rights should look before any partner-model art goes public.
- **Aspect ratio.** Firefly offers square, portrait 3:4, landscape 4:3 and widescreen 16:9 (verify on the panel). 4:5 may not be offered. For Instagram 4:5 (1080 x 1350), generate portrait 3:4 and crop the sides. Every panel prompt below says "keep the subject inside the center 80 percent" so the crop is safe.
- **Lettering.** Image models garble text. Every prompt asks for the lettering to be left out or kept to what the tool can do. Add KINGZ, WINTER WOLF, the banner and the FINAL WORD stamp afterward in Adobe Express or Photoshop. Fix the text there, not by re-rolling.
- **Balloons.** All text and balloons get added after generation. Prompts ask for empty space only.
- **Generate 4 variations each, pick the best, save the seed.** Write the seed into the filename. Use the same seed range when a character reappears.
- **Save convention.** `L:\Winter Wolfs Den review show\WWD Comic\Character Sheets\<name>_<view>_<seed>.png`. Zac may choose a different folder, then tell graphic-design.

## 6. Clearance and safety checklist (run on every result before it goes to Matt)

- No studio, streamer or network logo, mark, or typeface in any panel or window.
- No real postal, courier or delivery uniform, logo, or color scheme. His coat is plain oilskin with no patches. The mailbox is rusted steel, no markings.
- No recognizable console or microphone brand or shape. If the console reads as a real system, regenerate with "plain rounded slab".
- No real newspaper masthead. (Not used in these prompts.)
- No crested wax seal. The stamp face shows a single full stop only. If the tool adds a crest, letters or a coat of arms, reject the image.
- No text from the tool. Reject anything with garbled letters in the art, or crop it out.
- No figure, face or silhouette for Oracle.
- No em dashes in any lettering you add.
- Credits: all art carries the AI-generated disclosure the channel already uses. wwd-rights should read the final set before posting.
