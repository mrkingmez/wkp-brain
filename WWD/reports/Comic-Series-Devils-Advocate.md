# Devil's Advocate Ruling: WWD Comic Post Series
Date: 2026-10-30. Requested by Zac via Jarvis. New-idea gate, WWD off-brand check.

## VERDICT: GO WITH CHANGES
Not off brand. As pitched (3 full pages a week, styled like the EP094 page), not a good idea. Cut it to 1 a week, tied to FrostCast, built as a carousel or motion Reel, with no other companies' characters, and run a 4-week test.

## (1) Off brand?
No. This is the most on-brand thing WWD has made. The EP094 page already shows the visual identity: the KingZ crown hoodie, the masked red-eyed figure, the Winter Wolf jacket, the Den banner, the mics, "One pack. One Den." It shows the hosts as characters, and no other review channel can copy that.
The gritty noir look also matches the written tone benchmark in WWD\CLAUDE.md (the Spider-Noir review). One small flag: the review videos since 2026-10-14 carry a locked "have fun" layer (Wolfie Awards, spoiler alarms, wrong name counters). If every page is all rain and red eyes, the comics will feel darker than the show people get when they click.

## (2) Still a good idea? Case against
- **The account rewards Reels and ignores stills.** On 2026-10-19, 28 of the last 30 Instagram posts were Reels. The Carrie review carousel got 2 likes and the Other Mommy carousel got 3, while the top Reel got 17. A static comic page is the format this audience already scrolls past.
- **A full comic page does not read on a phone.** The EP094 page is a tall 2:3 with 12 panels and about 25 text boxes. Instagram crops the feed to 4:5, and the lettering ends up tiny. The thing that makes it a comic (lots of panels) is the same thing that breaks it on mobile.
- **3 a week costs more than WKP time can pay.** Each page needs an AI art pass, the writing room for every word, marketing, and no-ai-slop. Character looks have to stay the same across every page, and AI art drifts. That means about 150 pages a year for an account with 68 followers, while Kingdom Planners is the main effort. The likely ending is week 3, when it quietly stops.
- **Other companies' characters are a real problem.** The EP094 page shows the Daredevil logo, Mario, Sonic, the Mortal Kombat dragon, Kratos and Grogu, all drawn new by AI, in a promo post. That is not the commentary use that covers review stills. It is new art of trademarked characters used to sell the show. Firefly will also refuse most of these prompts, so the look cannot be repeated with the paid tool anyway.
- **Matt is the art side of WWD.** He makes the thumbnails and paid for the Firefly credits. WWD\CLAUDE.md also makes branding a joint decision with Matt. A series built on his likeness and his credits needs his yes before anything gets built.

## The 3 biggest risks
1. **Wrong format.** Stills get the lowest engagement on this account, so the series earns nothing.
2. **Cadence collapse.** 3 a week is more than fragment time can carry, and a series that stops does more harm than one that never started.
3. **IP exposure.** AI-drawn versions of other companies' characters in promo art, plus Firefly refusing those prompts.

## Changes required for GO
1. **One page a week, on FrostCast day (Wednesday).** It replaces an existing promo post instead of adding to the load. The EP094 page already proves that topic-list format works.
2. **Build it as a carousel.** Slide 1 is the hero panel, each topic panel gets its own 4:5 slide, and the last slide says LIVE TONIGHT. Also cut a 15 to 20 second motion Reel that pans across the same panels, so the comic shows up in the format the account actually rewards.
3. **No other companies' characters or logos.** Represent topics with the hosts reacting, props, silhouettes or the Den set. Run every page past wwd-rights before it posts.
4. **Lock a character sheet first** (KingZ, Winter Wolf, Oracle, the Den set). Every page prompts from it. graphic-design owns the prompts and Matt signs off on the looks.
5. **Run the gates every time.** script-room writes every word, then marketing-director, then no-ai-slop. Topic panels tease and never give the verdict. That part of the EP094 page was already right.
6. **Add some of the show's humor.** At least one panel a week should play like the hosts actually play on air.

## What would change my mind
- **Toward full 3 a week:** if, over 4 Wednesdays, the comic carousel or Reel beats the account's median Reel on likes, comments and shares, and the page takes under an hour of Zac's time each week.
- **Toward NO:** if Matt does not want it, if a page takes over an hour, or if it cannot be made without other companies' characters.
- At 68 followers the numbers are close to noise, so judge the test mostly on cost and on whether chat or comments mention the comic.

## What is working (keep it)
The hosts as characters, the red and black look, "One pack. One Den. See you in the chat.", and the numbered topic panels that tease without answering. The EP094 page is a good piece of work. The problem is the plan around it, not the page.
