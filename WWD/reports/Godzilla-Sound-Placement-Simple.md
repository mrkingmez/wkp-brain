# Godzilla Minus Zero: What Sound Goes Where

Simple version. Read top to bottom while you edit. Every sound file is in one folder: `P:\WWD Kit\Godzilla\Sounds\`. In Premiere, import that whole folder once (Ctrl+I, pick the folder, Import Folder), then put sounds on audio track A3.

**How to find each spot:** open Window > Text > Transcript, type the words in quotes, click the highlighted word. The time is a guide only. Times marked (est) can be off by 3 seconds either way.

**How loud:** Quiet = about -28 to -22 dB. Medium = about -20 to -14 dB. Loud = about -12 to -6 dB. Set the real level by ear. Nobody has heard these yet. Voices always stay on top.

**Each cue says REQUIRED or OPTIONAL.** Do the REQUIRED ones first. The OPTIONAL ones are jokes or extra flavor, skip any that feel like too much.

**No cue at all** during the Hiroshima and clinic talk (10:08 to 11:00) and Professor Noda's sacrifice (25:16 to 26:05). Leave those silent.

4. **Cracking and breaking** (REQUIRED):

   - `ST4 crack 4 concrete smash - Crash Concrete Smashing Through Wood Debris On Pavement Short 01.wav` start 0.05 seconds after landing, raise 6 dB, medium.

6. **The roar** (REQUIRED). Starts 1.1 seconds AFTER landing, as the foot lifts away. It is the second beat, so keep it about 4 dB quieter than the stomp.
   - Best: your generated kaiju roar from the list above.
