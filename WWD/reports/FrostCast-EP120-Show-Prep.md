## 5. Which movie monsters scared you most?

**Status: discussion question, no news.** Facts below are talking points only. All were read on Wikipedia pages (OBSERVED), which cite their own sources, so treat them as SECONDARY-grade if quoted on air. No Pennywise, Freddy or Jason facts were retrieved (NOT AVAILABLE).

**Jaws (opened 6/20/75)** - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaws_(film)
- Three pneumatic prop sharks nicknamed "Bruce" after Spielberg's lawyer. Joe Alves designed them. They broke down constantly in saltwater.
- Spielberg later said, "The shark not working was a godsend." He leaned on the floating barrels and dorsal-fin shots instead.
- John Williams's two-note theme won an Oscar. About $495M worldwide across all releases, the highest grosser until Star Wars in 1977.

**Alien (5/25/79)** - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alien_(film)
- H. R. Giger designed the creature. He sculpted the adult body using snake vertebrae and Rolls-Royce cooling tubes.
- Bolaji Badejo, a 26-year-old design student, stood about 6'10" and played it in most scenes.
- The chestburster was shot in one take and the cast did not know about the blood squibs. It won the Oscar for Best Visual Effects.

**The Thing (6/25/82)** - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Thing_(1982_film)
- Rob Bottin led the effects crew at age 21 and was hospitalized for exhaustion. Materials included mayonnaise, creamed corn and bubble gum. The effects cost $1.5M.
- Opened to $3.1M and finished at $19.6M on a $15M budget with mostly negative reviews. It opened against E.T.

**Predator (6/12/87)** - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Predator_(film)
- Jean-Claude Van Damme was first cast in the suit, replaced by 7'2" Kevin Peter Hall. The first design was scrapped and Stan Winston redesigned it, with mandibles suggested by James Cameron. About $98.3M worldwide.

**Michael Myers (Halloween, 1978)** - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Myers_(Halloween)
- The mask is a William Shatner Captain Kirk mask, enlarged eyeholes, painted white. Nick Castle was paid $25 a day. Carpenter's direction: "Do nothing, just walk."

**Godzilla Minus One (Japan, 11/3/23)** - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Godzilla_Minus_One
- Takashi Yamazaki wrote, directed and supervised the effects. Budget reported at $10M to $15M with no official figure. About $116M worldwide. Won the Oscar for Best Visual Effects at the 96th Academy Awards.

**Pyramid Head (Silent Hill 2, 2001)** - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyramid_Head
- Masahiro Ito designed it as "a monster with a hidden face." Read as a manifestation of the lead's guilt.

### Stewart's real service (OBSERVED, Wikipedia first 100k characters, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Stewart)
- Rejected for low weight in November 1940. Enlisted February 1941, inducted as a private in the Air Corps 3/22/41. Commissioned second lieutenant 1/1/42.
- Sent to England in November 1943 with the 703rd Bomb Squadron flying B-24 Liberators, at RAF Tibenham, later RAF Old Buckenham. Major January 1944, full colonel March 1945.
- Decorations: Distinguished Flying Cross, French Croix de Guerre with palm, Air Medal with three oak leaf clusters, Air Force Distinguished Service Medal on retirement.
- Air Force Reserve after the 1947 service split. Promoted brigadier general 7/23/59. Flew as a non-duty observer on a B-52 Arc Light mission in February 1966. Retired 5/31/68 at mandatory age 60 after 27 years.
