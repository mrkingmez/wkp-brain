## How to apply in Premiere

1. The timecodes are for your edited sequence. Drop each image on the track above the host cams at the start timecode and trim to the end timecode.
2. Effects panel, Video Transitions, drag the named transition to the head or tail of the clip. Right-click it, Set Transition Duration, and type the frames.
3. For hard cuts, no transition. The PNGs have transparent padding, so the host cams show behind the image.
4. Spot-check one or two placements against the audio first. If the drift is more than a couple of seconds, shift all of them by the same amount.
