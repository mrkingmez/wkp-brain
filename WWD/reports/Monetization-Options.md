# Method 1: Tool affiliates (tools WWD already uses)

Why it fits: the show already uses these tools every week and can say so truthfully. No evidence was found on how many viewers care how the show is made. Not generic: only tools the hosts use and can say so on air.

Attach points for all three: full-video description (clickable), pinned comment on the full video, on-air one-liner in FrostCast or a behind-the-scenes video. Not in Shorts descriptions (not clickable). FTC: say plainly that it is an affiliate link and the hosts get paid if someone subscribes; place it next to the link, not buried. (General FTC endorsement guidance, 16 CFR Part 255, from background knowledge, not retrieved this pass; clearance or an attorney should confirm. Surfshark's unresolved disclosure question in TASKS.md is the same question.)

### 1a. OpusClip affiliate
- What it is: referral program for the clip tool WWD already uses for Shorts.
- Payout: 25% recurring commission. OBSERVED (https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/affiliate-program-faq). Duration conflict on the same page: first year versus as long as the subscriber stays. The terms page (SECONDARY, search summary) says max 12 months.
- Cookie: not specified on the FAQ. A directory says 60 days. SECONDARY.
- Threshold: 20 dollars earned before first payout, paid on the 15th by PayPal, 3 to 5 days to land. OBSERVED.
- Signup: free application, reviewed (about a week per the terms summary). Every creator may apply. No subscriber minimum stated. Accounts with no traffic in 6 months are deactivated, affiliates are expected to mention Opus regularly. No paid ads with the link. OBSERVED.
- Suits 744 subs: yes. Caveat: Zac's Opus plan is paid already (the Shorts scheduler is built on it), so mentioning it is honest.
- Disclosure: "affiliate link" line next to the link.

### 1b. vidIQ affiliate
- What it is: referral program for the YouTube tool the show uses for keyword and outlier research.
- Payout: 15% recurring (0 to 10 sales), 20% (11 to 50), 25% (51+), lifetime while the referred user stays subscribed, no commission on refunds. OBSERVED (https://support.vidiq.com/en/articles/7866006-vidiq-affiliate-commission-everything-you-need-to-know).
- Cookie: not specified there. Directories say 90 days. SECONDARY.
- Threshold: 10 dollars, paid by PayPal in the first week of the month. OBSERVED.
- Signup: free, self-service inside the vidIQ app, no approval step stated, no other requirements. OBSERVED (https://support.vidiq.com/en/articles/7668821-the-vidiq-affiliate-program-who-can-join-and-how). Note: WWD's vidIQ sits under Matt's login, so the affiliate account would be Matt's or a new one. That is a WWD joint decision.
- Suits 744 subs: yes.
- Fit caution: promoting a growth tool from a channel whose subs are flat can read badly. Say what it did for the show (the outlier research), not "grow your channel".

### 1c. StreamYard affiliate
- What it is: referral program for the streaming and cam recording tool used for the show.
- Payout: 25% of each subscription sold. SECONDARY (search summary of StreamYard's support page; the page itself returned 403, NOT AVAILABLE). Recurring is stated only by a third-party review.
- Cookie and payout threshold: NOT AVAILABLE.
- Terms noted in the summary: no running ads with the link, no coupon-site use, StreamYard may change or end the program, refunded transactions earn nothing.
- Signup: free. Requirements NOT AVAILABLE (page blocked).
- Suits 744 subs: probably. Verify terms at signup.
- Also looked at and left out: Riverside (recurring up to 20%, cookie and minimum not stated on its page, https://riverside.com/affiliate-program OBSERVED) because WWD records on StreamYard, not Riverside; Epidemic Sound (referrer earns subscription credit, Ambassador after 12 paying referrals gets 50% of first 12 months, requires an Epidemic subscription and contractor or company status, OBSERVED https://www.epidemicsound.com/community-program/) because WWD's rule is owned music plus Suno, not Epidemic.

# Method 3: Books and physical media retail

Fit: Den Files episodes compare a true story to the film, and reviews of book adaptations (Shattered Empire is Zac's own, separate). "Read the real account" is a natural link.

### 3a. Bookshop.org affiliate
- What: independent-bookstore marketplace; non-bookstore affiliates (reviewers, influencers, anyone) can join and set up a storefront. OBSERVED (https://support.bookshop.org/en/support/solutions/articles/65000191390-can-you-explain-bookshop-org-s-affiliate-program-).
- Payout: bookstore affiliates 30%. For non-bookstore affiliates the official page gives no set rate; third-party sources say 10% (SECONDARY, unverified). Verify before relying.
- Attribution: last touch; non-bookstore affiliates earn on sales within 48 hours of a click. OBSERVED.
- Payout threshold and schedule: not stated on the official page. Directories say monthly, 10 dollar minimum. SECONDARY.
- Signup: free, no audience minimum listed. Bookshop's affiliate profile page returned 403 (NOT AVAILABLE).
- Attach: Den Files and book-adaptation reviews, description link, pinned comment on the long video. 48-hour window is short, so it only works when the link is clicked at the time of reading, not as a "later" link.
- Suits 744 subs: yes, no minimum.
- Disclosure: "affiliate link" next to the link; Bookshop shows the affiliate name at checkout.

### 3b. Amazon Associates
- Rates (OBSERVED, https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/welcome/compensation): Digital Videos 5%, Physical Books 4.5%, DVD and Blu-Ray 2.5%, Physical Video Games and Consoles 1%, Digital Video Games 2%, Toys 3%, all other 4%.
- Required disclosure wording: "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases." OBSERVED (operating agreement, https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement).
- Account rule: a new applicant has 180 days to drive 3 qualifying sales or the application is closed and cannot be reinstated. SECONDARY (Amazon's Account Dormancy help page seen only through a search summary). Treat as true until read directly.
- Cookie: 24 hours is widely reported but was NOT in either official page we could read. NOT AVAILABLE.
- Payout threshold: not on the pages read. NOT AVAILABLE. Amazon's landing page says commissions pay about 60 days after month end (OBSERVED by market-research).
- Attach: description and pinned comment on the long video, never Shorts. Amazon also has a separate Influencer Program for social creators, terms NOT AVAILABLE.
- Suits 744 subs: weakest of the three because of the 3-sales-in-180-days clock on a channel with about 400 views a day. Amazon is also the easiest to link, so it only makes sense if the link is already in the description for something Zac wants to point to.

### 3c. Zavvi (Awin)
- What: physical media, collectibles, steelbooks, 4K releases. Horror-heavy catalog fits WWD's reviews.
- Cookie: 30 days on the US and Canada and UK Awin listings. SECONDARY (directory listings). One international listing shows 90 days and 1.25%, unverified.
- Commission rate: NOT AVAILABLE. Only visible after joining Awin.
- Signup: through Awin, needs signup and likely a property review. Whether Awin charges a fee: not checked. Mark as a Zac decision before any signup.
- Attach: "own it on 4K" end-card or description line for classic horror Retro Watch and review videos.
- Suits 744 subs: unknown.

- Suits 744 subs: weak. It is a joint decision with Matt (anything touching format or branding).

### 4c. YouTube channel memberships
- Gate: 500 subs, 3 uploads in 90 days, plus 3,000 long-form watch hours (12 months) or 3 million Shorts views (90 days). OBSERVED. Creator keeps 70% (SECONDARY).

- Suits 744 subs: depends on the Studio answer.
