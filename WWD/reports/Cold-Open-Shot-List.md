## Firefly swap: what changes from the Higgsfield plan
Verify each of these in Firefly before committing; I could not check them live (the Adobe connector in this session is not authenticated).
1. **Character consistency is the main risk.** Higgsfield was chosen for a locked Warden across many shots. Firefly needs a reference image on every generation. Lock one hero Warden still first, reuse it as the reference for every character shot.
2. **Clip length.** Firefly video clips are short (believed about 5 seconds). The shots above are sized for that. Longer VO lines get two or three cuts, not one long clip.
3. **No text in generations.** Titles and signs get built in After Effects over clean plates.
4. **Prompts.** Firefly prompts for shots 2 to 8 can be written as soon as Zac approves the shot list. Warden prompts wait on the design.
5. **Credits.** Check the Firefly generative credit balance before a batch. Do not assume it is unlimited.
6. **Higgsfield stays dormant.** The cost-analysis task (free trial vs. $49/mo) remains Zac's call. This plan does not need it.
