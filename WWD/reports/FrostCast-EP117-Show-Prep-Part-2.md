## Topic 9: Crawlers and what happened to horror this spooky season

**Verified first:** "Crawlers" is a real 2026 horror film (CONFIRMED, Celluloid Junkie, The Numbers chart). Spider-infestation horror originally titled "Arachnid," directed by Angel Gomez Hernandez, written by Jayson Rothwell, starring Matilda Lutz as Serena, manager of the Paradiso Palms apartment complex. A viral outbreak turns out to be a venomous spider infestation and residents are quarantined. US distributors Roadside Attractions and Saban Films (rights deal announced Apr 30, 2026). Rated R. Opened Oct 16, 2026 (it slid from Oct 2, confirmed by the Oct 16 weekend chart showing three days in release). Not the 2019 film Crawl, not the 2020 Into the Dark episode "Crawlers."

**What happened:** It opened to $462,691 in 1,040 theaters, rank 19 (The Numbers). Screen Rant reports $468,457 from 1,037 theaters, $451 per screen, and says it ranks No. 163 on Box Office Mojo's worst-debut per-screen list, the third new horror release in three weeks on that list. Critics were split: RogerEbert.com "fitfully creepy," TheWrap "fiendishly fun flick," Fangoria harsh (REPORTED).

**Why it matters:** The season has a few huge hits and a pile of small openings, so "what happened to horror" has two answers depending on the title.

**Numbers:**
- Weekend Oct 16-18, 2026 (The Numbers, total $103,513,297): Street Fighter $30,775,377; You Can See Everything $15,601,418; Other Mommy $8,204,680; Whalefall $7,837,153; Sense and Sensibility $7,203,465; Verity $6,046,599; Resident Evil $5,501,456.
- Resident Evil (Zach Cregger): about $60M domestic opening Sept 18-20, a franchise best (REPORTED, AP). $148,459,411 domestic after day 31, over $350M worldwide (REPORTED, Screen Rant).
- Other Mommy: opened Oct 9-11 at No. 1 with $27,805,825 (The Numbers), $45.2M global debut (REPORTED, AP), budget about $19M (REPORTED). Second weekend $8,204,680, about 70.5 percent down by arithmetic from the chart figures. Total $44,709,120 after 10 days. Screen Rant calls it Blumhouse's best opening of the year.
- Small openings (Screen Rant): The Influencer Project, Oct 2, $376 per screen; Beware Boiuna, $491 per screen, $924,608 worldwide so far.
- Obsession (Curry Barker): reportedly a $750,000 budget and more than $500M worldwide (REPORTED, IndieWire, paywalled summary).
- Horror in 2026: more than $1.1B domestic (REPORTED, AP-sourced, date not pinned). Screen Rant says only Other Mommy and Resident Evil succeeded this Halloween season.
- Insidious: Out of the Further: $66,092,129 domestic after 59 days.
- 2025 context (REPORTED, individual articles): Conjuring: Last Rites $83M domestic opening, near $500M worldwide; Sinners $279.6M domestic, $367.9M worldwide; Final Destination: Bloodlines $315.8M worldwide; Together (2025, Neon) $34,573,161 worldwide.
