## What authors usually get wrong (this topic)
- Hades and the Big Three oath. People treat "forbidden children" as Zeus and Poseidon only. The show has Hades's children too.
- Titans versus gods. Atlas, Prometheus and Epimetheus are Titans, brothers. Sisyphus is a mortal king. People swap them.
- Atlas holds up the sky, not the Earth. The "holding the world" picture comes from later globe imagery. The show says sky.
- Persephone's months vary by version.
