## Title correction (read first)
The handoff called this "Season 3 (The Sea of Monsters)". Sea of Monsters is Season 2 (Dec 10 2025 to Jan 21 2026). Season 3 adapts The Titan's Curse, premiered Nov 20 2026, eps 1 and 2 released together (Wikipedia, TMDb season 3 page). I pulled Season 3 eps 1 and 2: Ep 1 "My Rescue Operation Goes Very Wrong", Ep 2 "An Old Dead Friend Comes to Visit". Titles on upload copy should say Titan's Curse, not Sea of Monsters.
