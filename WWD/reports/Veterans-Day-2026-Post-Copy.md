Reject checklist (reject on any yes):
1. Any person, hand, face or silhouette, including a window reflection or a far-off figure.
2. Any flag, flag-colored bunting or red-white-blue striping.
3. Any readable or pseudo-text, on the mat, mug, mailbox, house numbers or signs.
4. Any patch, insignia, emblem or logo, including on boots or mug.
5. Boots wrong: not a pair, fused, melted laces.
6. Mood somber: dim, cold, gray, dusk-dark, boots look abandoned instead of set down at home.
7. Combat or memorial props: camo, helmet, candles, crosses, dog tags.
8. AI artifacts: warped railing, melted steps, doubled door frame.
9. Porch light not visibly on, or overexposed so the glow is lost.

## 7. Facts check
