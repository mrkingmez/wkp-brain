# Theater or Straight to DVD: Netflix Originals

**The question:** If streaming didn't exist, would this movie have gotten you into a theater, or would it have gone straight to the DVD bin at Walmart?

**How to run it:** Read the title and the one-liner, each host calls THEATER or DVD, then argue it out. The verdicts are yours. The list is spread across genres so you get a mix of easy calls and fights.

**Note:** Titles run through 2025. 2026 releases weren't checked. Numbers are left off on purpose. If you want budgets or scores, ask and I'll pull and check them first.

## Big-budget action swings

1. **Red Notice** (2021). Dwayne Johnson, Ryan Reynolds, Gal Gadot. Art-thief heist chase.
2. **The Gray Man** (2022). Ryan Gosling, Chris Evans, directed by the Russo brothers. CIA hitman on the run.
3. **6 Underground** (2019). Ryan Reynolds, directed by Michael Bay. Ghost squad of vigilantes.
4. **Extraction** (2020). Chris Hemsworth, written by Joe Russo. One-man rescue in Dhaka.
5. **Heart of Stone** (2023). Gal Gadot. Spy movie built to be a franchise.
6. **The Old Guard** (2020). Charlize Theron. Immortal mercenaries.
7. **Army of the Dead** (2021). Zack Snyder. Zombie Vegas heist.
8. **Rebel Moon, Part One** (2023). Zack Snyder. Space opera that started as a Star Wars pitch.
9. **The Electric State** (2025). Millie Bobby Brown, Chris Pratt, Russo brothers. Robots and a road trip.
10. **Bright** (2017). Will Smith, directed by David Ayer. Orcs and elves as LA cops. Netflix's first big blockbuster swing.
11. **Atlas** (2024). Jennifer Lopez. AI villain, mech suit.
12. **Lift** (2024). Kevin Hart. Heist on a plane.
13. **Back in Action** (2025). Cameron Diaz's return, with Jamie Foxx. Ex-spies pulled back in.

## Sci-fi and fantasy

14. **The Adam Project** (2022). Ryan Reynolds, Mark Ruffalo. Time travel, meets his younger self.
15. **Damsel** (2024). Millie Bobby Brown. Princess fed to a dragon fights back.
16. **Don't Look Up** (2021). Leonardo DiCaprio, Jennifer Lawrence. Comet satire.
17. **The Platform** (2019). Spanish sci-fi horror. A prison where food comes down floor by floor.

## Horror and thriller

18. **Bird Box** (2018). Sandra Bullock. Blindfolds, don't look.
19. **Fear Street: 1994** (2021). Start of the Fear Street trilogy, R.L. Stine.
20. **His House** (2020). Refugee couple, haunted house in England.
21. **Hush** (2016). Mike Flanagan. Deaf writer vs. a home invader. (Flanagan callback for the Carrie fans.)
22. **The Ritual** (2017). Hikers in the Swedish woods.

## Prestige and awards bait

23. **The Irishman** (2019). Scorsese, De Niro, Pacino, Pesci. Three and a half hours.
24. **Glass Onion: A Knives Out Mystery** (2022). Daniel Craig. Got a one-week theatrical run before streaming.
25. **All Quiet on the Western Front** (2022). German WWI film, Oscar winner.
26. **Frankenstein** (2025). Guillermo del Toro, Oscar Isaac, Jacob Elordi.
27. **Roma** (2018). Alfonso Cuarón. Black and white, Spanish.

## Comedy and sequels

28. **Murder Mystery** (2019). Adam Sandler, Jennifer Aniston.
29. **Happy Gilmore 2** (2025). Sandler goes back to the well.
30. **Rebel Moon, Part Two: The Scargiver** (2024). Does a sequel to a split movie earn a ticket?

## Animated

31. **KPop Demon Hunters** (2025). Went to theaters anyway as a sing-along weekend after it blew up on Netflix.
32. **Klaus** (2019). Santa origin story, hand-drawn look.
33. **The Sea Beast** (2022). Monster hunters on the high seas.

## Bonus round ideas

- **Lightning round:** read 5 titles fast, one-word answers only.
- **Matinee or full price:** for any THEATER call, would you pay full price or wait for the cheap show?
- **Which one ships to DVD with a sequel nobody asked for?** Pick one DVD call and pitch its direct-to-DVD sequel title.
