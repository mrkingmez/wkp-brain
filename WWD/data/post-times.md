# WWD Posting Times (Eastern Time), researched 2026-11-02

Purpose: the upload package picks release slots from this file. Slots are Eastern Time. Confidence is stated per row. Read "Evidence basis" before trusting any slot.

## Evidence basis (honest version)

1. Channel data, what we have:
   - Daily views and subs for 2026-08-04 to 10-31, publish timestamps for the latest 50 long videos, 50 FrostCast streams and 50 Shorts, with views as of 11-02.
   - What we do NOT have: hourly or first-48-hour views per video (needs vidiq_video_stats at 5 credits per video, credits are at 0 until 2026-11-11), VidIQ's own "best time to post" from subscriber activity (the subscriber_insights report was still generating and the retry had no credits), YouTube Analytics "when your viewers are on YouTube", audience geography, and any Facebook or Instagram timing data (Instagram has 68 followers so IG insights are locked; Facebook not pulled).
   - Result: nothing in the channel data can separate publish hour from topic. Every release slot we have used in 2026 is a morning to midday ET weekday slot (reviews at 9:00 to 11:30 AM ET; FrostCast VOD posted about 11:00 AM ET the day after the Wednesday recording), so there is no variety to learn from. Shorts have more variety, see below.
2. Outside sources: Buffer (published 2026-03-25, "over 52 million posts", Instagram about 10 million posts), PostFast (6,175 YouTube posts from 97 workspaces, 90 days to 2026-09-04, article updated 2026-09-20), plus a search summary of a SocialPilot study (301k videos) and a search summary of Sprout Social Facebook data that was not opened at the source. Studies disagree. They are mostly about large accounts and are not WWD-specific.
3. Our audience assumption: US, Eastern time (hosts are in the US, "clayface review" demand was 32% US per the 2026-10-26 VidIQ pull). Not verified for our own subscribers.

Confidence key: LOW = outside studies only and they disagree, or n too small. MED = outside studies agree and channel pattern does not contradict. No row is HIGH.

## Full videos on YouTube (reviews, Vision Quest, Den Files)

Movie and show reviews live on search (YouTube search was 15,243 of about 69,800 views in the last 90 days, second only to the Shorts feed). For a new release, being live before the search surge matters more than the hour. Publish the day of or the morning after the hosts record, not days later (the Clayface search spike was the week of 10-26; our review went live 10-27 11:00 AM ET and got 217 views against a niche where small channels got 359K, though other factors apply).

Shorts are 91% of channel views but only 0.24 subscribers per 1,000 views, and the studies disagree most here.

Sources: Buffer 2026 (Friday 4:00 PM best, then Saturday and Thursday, Tuesday weakest, weekday evenings 6 to 11 PM, Friday 6 and 7 PM also good). PostFast (5,928 Shorts, 90 days to 2026-09-04, each post scored against its own channel median at 48 hours): 1:00 PM local best at 1.28x on 348 posts, then 9 PM, 10 AM, 8 PM; weakest 6 PM and 3 PM; Saturday 1.13x and Sunday 1.12x best, Wednesday 0.89x worst; 0.95x to 1.05x counts as flat and its split-half repeat on Shorts was only 0.52, so even this is shaky. Industry windows (Buffer March 2026, 1.8 million videos, via PostFast): 7 to 11 AM mornings.
Channel check on 7 Shorts with 100+ views in the 10-23 to 11-03 batch (times converted to ET): Bane 10-23 3:01 PM Fri (156); COD 10-24 2:55 PM Sat (1,048); Joker 10-24 12:02 PM Sat (151); Black Adam 10-25 6:56 AM Sun (575); Green Lantern 10-25 12:01 PM Sun (106); Clayface post-credit 10-26 5:01 PM Mon (220); Stewart tribute 10-30 11:27 AM Fri (1,209). Mostly Friday to Sunday and late morning to mid afternoon, which loosely matches the table, but topic (DC, gaming, a death) explains the views at least as well as the hour, and 49 other Shorts went up at similar hours with under 100. n=7, so this is a hint, not a result.
Cadence note: posting about 5 Shorts a day (50 in 10 days) means slots are set by the schedule, not chosen. See the report: volume may matter more than the hour.

## Weekend versus weekday summary

- Full videos: weekday 12 to 3 PM or Sunday 10 AM. Facebook and Instagram: Wednesday and Thursday mornings are the best supported slots. Shorts: Friday to Sunday late morning to afternoon.
- If one slot has to serve everything: Thursday 12:00 PM ET is inside the good window for all four (YouTube Thursday 12 to 3, Facebook, Instagram near the 9 AM to noon window, Shorts Thursday strong). It happens to be when the FrostCast VOD already posts.

## How to replace guesses with our own data (all free)

1. Open YouTube Studio, Analytics, Audience tab, "When your viewers are on YouTube" and write down the top three day and hour blocks. This is the single best source and costs nothing. Add the result to this file.
2. After 2026-11-11 (credits reset), run vidiq_subscriber_insights (5 credits) for its bestTimeToPost windows.
3. Run a timing test: release comparable reviews at two different slots (for example 12 PM versus 6 PM) and compare first-48-hour views. Four to six reviews gives a weak signal at our volume; do not over-read it.
