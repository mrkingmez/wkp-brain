## 4. Look-ahead audit, ruling K6 (checklist 4)

Can I construct a counted entry before its signal? No. Evidence: (a) fuzz of `entry_index` with 20,000 random acceptance instants, time-known and date-only, weekends included: the entry open was never at or before the signal instant; (b) tests above; (c) the scorecard K6 automated check (`lookahead_records`) reads the same condition from the output file.

Can I construct a cluster that used an amendment row before its acceptance? No. Probe results (`scratch\qa-insider\lookahead.py`): a 4/A restating a buyer's row, accepted 21 Oct, supersedes the original filing and moves the signal to 21 Oct 10:00 (not earlier). A 4/A filed more than 5 weekdays after the trade is dropped as late and the original is still superseded, so the cluster falls to WATCH. Both directions are conservative.

Finding (non-blocking, N1): a cluster's label and status are decided using filings accepted AFTER the signal. Demonstrated with three officer filings accepted 12, 13, 14 Oct (signal 14 Oct 17:00) and a fourth filing accepted 19 Oct:
- if the fourth is a CEO, `cluster_type` flips from `officers_directors` to `ceo_cfo_involved` and `n_distinct_insiders` becomes 4, although nobody knew of the CEO at the signal;
- if the fourth has no relationship box ticked, a cluster that was COUNTED at the signal becomes `FAIL_UNKNOWN_TYPE` retroactively.
The FAIL direction only removes clusters (conservative). The type label only feeds EXPLORATORY splits. `signal`, `entry` and the `contributing_accessions` list correctly ignore the later filing (test `test_signal_ignores_later_filings_that_only_grow_the_cluster`). So K6 as the ruling words it is not violated, but a literal reader of "no information from after the signal" should know status and type are not point in time. Fix to describe, not do: compute status, type, n_distinct and total_value from the rows accepted by the signal instant, or state the limit in README.
