## Judgment calls I made (flagged for trading-director)
1. **Primary horizon = 5 trading days.** Fits the locked 5-day time stop. 20 days matches the literature but needs a locked-rule change from Zac. The ruling said pick one now, knowing that. Queued so trading-director can confirm before the first counted cluster (changing it later is a config reset).
2. **Freeze time = 2026-11-28T00:00:00 ET.** My placeholder. trading-director confirms or moves it when `LIVE-OK.flag` is written. A move is one value change and restarts counts (nothing is counted yet).
3. **K1 "metric AND median zero or negative"** is read on the binding benchmark (the smaller of the mean excess against SPY and against IWM, and the smaller of the two medians).
4. **Acceptance time zone** is assumed America/New_York (`timing.accepted_tz`). NOT VERIFIED against a live file.
5. **Pre-market cutoff 09:00 ET** (30 minutes before the open, a margin for the time zone assumption).
6. **Late limit 5 weekdays**, no holiday calendar (a holiday reads one day long, so the filter errs toward dropping, never toward keeping).
7. **A missing 10b5-1 flag is not excluded** (older filings never had it). It is recorded and flagged, and the scorecard flag counts them. "UNKNOWN is FAIL" is applied to the offering footnote screen, where the ruling named it.
8. **FILL_UNKNOWN is never FILLED.** With daily bars and no quotes, every cluster is FILL_UNKNOWN or UNFILLED. K2 can then read NOT EVALUABLE and K3 NOT YET until intraday data exists. This is honest, not a defect to hide.
9. **Amendments:** a cluster that existed only before a 4/A removed a buyer is dropped when the amendment lands (the original is superseded), instead of an as-of replay that would keep it as tradable at the time. Conservative, labeled in the README, not built.
10. **Cost applies to both ledgers**; the primary test reads the Zac ledger only.
11. **Cluster-level exclusions** (who counts, 10b5-1, offering, late) are logged in `skips.jsonl`; scoring-level exclusions (new listing, UNKNOWN listing age) are on the score record as `excluded` and shown in the scorecard line one.
