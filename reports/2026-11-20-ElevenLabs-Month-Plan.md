Date: 2026-11-20. Bench: music-researcher for music. Decision: Zac buys ONE discounted first month, downloads music and sounds, cancels before the second billing. Nothing here buys, signs up or generates. Zac buys. Nothing was heard by me; every audio line is a prompt, untested.

## 0. Read this first (the five things that change the plan)

1. **Creator does NOT get lossless WAV or 192 kbps music downloads.** The music terms mark High-Quality Downloads as No for Creator, Yes only for Pro ($99). Creator music comes as a plain download (the music overview lists MP3 44.1 kHz 128 to 192 kbps and WAV as formats in general, but the terms do not say which Creator gets). Treat it as MP3 and check the download button before the first generation. Sound effects: MP3, plus 48 kHz WAV for non-looping effects per the SFX docs (plan gating for SFX not retrieved).
2. **Creator music is 'For Individual Use Only'** and the word is not defined in the terms. The WWD LLC is on Matt, and Ian posts his own trailer. Whether Zac making music for WWD or for Ian counts as individual use is NOT stated. Queued as a question (see section 5). Until answered, use generated music for Zac's own channel work and treat Ian as a flagged risk, with the owned track as fallback.
3. **Film, TV, radio and Studio Games are excluded** from music commercial rights on every plan under Enterprise. Do not use ElevenLabs music in RUBY TWO (short film). Online video (YouTube, Shorts, Reels) reads as allowed but the terms do not define 'online'.
4. **Credits are not the limit.** Core jobs below cost about 25,395 credits of 121,000. The limit is Zac's hours before the cycle ends and the 250 minute download cap (not close). So the plan adds a spare-credit list in section 4.
5. **Cancel on day 1.** Cancelling takes effect only at the end of the billing cycle, so Zac can cancel right after buying and still use the full month. Resume is possible before the cycle ends. Unused credits are lost at the end.
