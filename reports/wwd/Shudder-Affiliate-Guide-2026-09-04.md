## The two real paths — pursue both, they are not mutually exclusive

**Path 1 — Standard affiliate program (money path).**
Shudder runs a consumer affiliate program through **Impact**, a third-party performance-marketing platform (confirmed by multiple affiliate-network listings — see Sources). Affiliates earn a commission (reported around $9.50 per sale on some listings — treat that number as a rough market signal, not confirmed pricing, since it comes from third-party affiliate directories, not Shudder directly) for driving Shudder subscriptions through a tracked link. This is the fastest, most concrete path to an actual "we're affiliated with Shudder" — it does not require anyone at AMC to say yes to a content deal, just approval into the affiliate program.

**How to start Path 1:**
1. Go to Impact's public site (impact.com) and look for their advertiser/campaign directory, or search "Shudder affiliate program Impact" to find the live application link — the exact signup URL moves around and I could not confirm one live URL from here, so this step needs you to actually click through and apply. If Impact doesn't turn up a direct Shudder listing, the affiliate program may also run through a sub-network — search the same way for "Shudder" on Rakuten Advertising and ShareASale as a backup, since I could not confirm which single network is current.
2. The application asks for basic info about your channel/audience (subscriber count, content niche, links). Have your YouTube channel link, subscriber count, and a one-line description of WWD/FrostCast ready before you start.
3. Approval typically takes a few days per third-party affiliate-program listings (not confirmed directly by Shudder/AMC, flagging as unconfirmed).
4. Once approved, you get a tracked referral link and can legitimately say "affiliate link" in descriptions — this is the real, immediate way to remove "we are in talks" and replace it with "we are affiliated."

**Path 2 — Direct content/press outreach (relationship path).**
This is a slower, human path: reaching out directly to AMC Global Media's partnership desk (the group that runs branded content deals for AMC/Shudder) and separately to Shudder's press line for screener/review access. This is the path more likely to eventually produce something bigger than a link — actual review copies, an early-access relationship, maybe eventual co-promotion — but it is a cold outreach to a media conglomerate's partnerships team, which more often handles large brand deals (their public case studies list POM, Bacardi, Hyundai, Mercedes-Benz), so treat this as a long-shot / relationship-building move, not a guaranteed yes.
