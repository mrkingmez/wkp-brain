# How to use Teacher

A short guide for learning to use AI. No experience needed.

## What Teacher is

Teacher is a small course that lives on Zac's computer at home. You open it in the web browser on your phone or computer, the same way you open any website.

The home page has four paths. You pick the one you want, in any order. Nothing is locked.

1. **Start Here.** What AI is, what Claude is, and how to ask it well, from the basics to advanced.
2. **Make Things.** Pictures, video, postcards and printables, and how to sell what you make.
3. **Build and Automate.** Making things work for you, automating tasks, building small tools.
4. **Stay Safe.** Checking what AI tells you, privacy, scams, and when not to use it.

## What exists so far

Only one lesson is written so far. It is in the Stay Safe path: **How to check what AI tells you**. It has three practice tasks and a short quiz.

Everything else is planned but not written yet. Start Here, Make Things, and Build and Automate each show a list of planned lessons marked "Coming soon". Three more Stay Safe lessons (privacy, scams and fakes, when not to use AI) are also not written yet. Their pages say so.

One more page is open: in Build and Automate, the **Advanced doorway** is a links page to a free course made by someone else, for people who build AI software. It is optional. You do not need it.

New lessons will show up on the same pages as they are written.

Two things to know:

- Teacher only works while Zac has it running on his computer.
- You must be on the home wifi. It will not open anywhere else.

## Before you start

1. Make sure your phone or computer is connected to the home wifi.
2. Ask Zac if Teacher is running. If it is, go on.
3. You will need an AI chat open for the practice. If you do not have one yet, ask Zac to help you set one up first.

## Step 1: Open Teacher

The address is:

**http://192.168.68.53:8800/**

On your phone:

1. Open your browser (Safari on an iPhone, Chrome on most Android phones).
2. Tap the bar at the top where the web address goes.
3. Type the address above, exactly as written, with the numbers and dots.
4. Tap Go.

On a computer:

1. Open your browser.
2. Click the long bar at the top.
3. Type the address above, exactly as written.
4. Press Enter.

You should see a page that says **Teacher**, with four boxes, one for each path.

## Step 2: Save the address (bookmark it)

So you do not have to type it again.

On a computer: press Ctrl and D together (on a Mac, Command and D), then click Done.

On an iPhone: tap the Share button (a square with an arrow), then tap Add Bookmark, then tap Save.

On an Android phone: tap the three dots in the corner, then tap the star, or tap Add to bookmarks.

Zac may change the address one day. If your bookmark stops working, ask Zac for the new one.

## Step 3: Pick a path

1. On the Teacher page, tap the box for the path you want.
2. You will see that path's page with its list of lessons.
3. A lesson marked "Coming soon" is not written yet. You cannot open it.
4. The only finished lesson is in **Stay Safe**. Tap the **Stay Safe** box, then tap **How to check what AI tells you**.

## Step 4: Read the lesson

1. Read it from the top. It takes about 10 minutes.
2. It has three steps for checking anything an AI tells you. Take your time. You can leave and come back.

## Step 5: Do the three practice tasks

The tasks are lower down on the lesson page, under the heading **Practice**.

1. Open your AI chat in another tab or another app. Keep Teacher open too.
2. Do Task 1, Task 2, and Task 3. Each takes about 10 minutes. Any order is fine.
3. Each task has a box of words you can copy and paste into the chat. Use the copy button on the box.
4. Each task has a checklist. Read each line and check it off in your head or on paper. The boxes on the page do not click.
5. When you have done the tasks, tap the button that says **I did the practice**.
6. You will see "Saved. Practice marked done."

## Step 6: Take the quiz

The quiz is at the very bottom of the lesson page.

1. Scroll to the bottom to **Check yourself**.
2. There are three questions. Tap one answer for each.
3. Tap **Check my answers**.
4. It tells you which ones were right and why.
5. Getting 2 out of 3 right counts as passing. If you do not pass, read the lesson again and try once more. There is no penalty.

## Nothing is locked

You can open any path and any page that exists, in any order. If you ever see a page that says **Not open yet**, tell Zac.

## What is saved, and who can see it

- Your quiz scores and your "I did the practice" taps are saved in a file on Zac's computer. They are not saved on your phone.
- The file has no name on it. It only lists the lesson, the score, pass or fail, and the date and time.
- Nothing is sent to the internet.
- Teacher has a page that would show Zac your progress. That page is turned off. It stays off until you say yes.
- Anyone on your home wifi who knows the address can open the course. Teacher has no password.

## If the page does not open

1. Check that you are on the home wifi, not mobile data.
2. Check that you typed the address exactly: **http://192.168.68.53:8800/**
3. Close the browser and try once more.
4. If it still does not open, ask Zac. Most likely it is not running, or the address changed.

Please do not change any settings on your phone, computer, or router to fix it. Zac will do that.

## Good to know

Teacher never asks for a password, a credit card, or any payment. If a page ever does, close it and tell Zac.
