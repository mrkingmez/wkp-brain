## The formats

**What If (Format A)** — a real historical fork, run forward through a
nine-step weighted branch model. Not battle-exclusive. JFK is the model
case. The machinery is the product.

**Hidden Hinge (Format B)** — the credited battle versus the overlooked
one that carried the actual causal weight. Backward causal-chain
correction. Military events only.

**AAR Commander (Format C)** — a battle run through a professional After
Action Review. Four questions. The value is the framework, not the
history.

## FORMAT LAW — absolute

**Never mix What If and Hidden Hinge.** A predicts FORWARD from an open
fork. B re-traces BACKWARD through a resolved chain. Branch-probability
machinery does not belong in a Hidden Hinge episode.

**AAR Commander: no invented dialogue, ever.** Participants speak only
through the documented record. "He never explained this, and we don't
know" is the honest line.

**Improvements must be period-legal.** Tested against technology,
doctrine, communications, and institutional culture actually available.

**Judgment against contemporary standards only.** A competent peer, in
that army, in that decade.

## Rules

No em dashes. Evidence tags on every claim: ESTABLISHED, SUPPORTED,
CONTESTED, SPECULATIVE. No percentages, no numeric confidence scores.
"Unknown" is a valid and expected answer.
Never produce a citation you are not confident is real.
