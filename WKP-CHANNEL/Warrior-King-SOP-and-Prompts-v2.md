### Instagram "What If" Carousels + YouTube Cinematic Episodes

# PART 1: INSTAGRAM "WHAT IF" CAROUSEL

## Step 1 — Pick the Scenario
A battle, decision point, or leader's choice.

## Step 2 — Audience Hook Check + Content Score (do this before research)

```
I'm considering this "What If" scenario for Warrior King Productions: 
[YOUR SCENARIO]

Before I invest research time, evaluate it:
1. Why would someone care? What emotion or curiosity does this trigger?
2. Does it challenge something people think they already know, or is it 
   too obscure/too obvious to hook attention?
3. Score it 1-10 on: Historical Interest, Curiosity/Hook Strength, Debate 
   Potential, Visual Potential, Evergreen Value. Give me a total out of 50 
   and your honest read on whether it's worth producing.
4. If it scores low, suggest a sharper angle or a different divergence 
   point on the same topic that would hook harder.

Be honest, not encouraging — I'd rather kill a weak idea now than after 
building it.
```

If it scores below ~35/50, either sharpen the angle or pick a different scenario before moving on.

## Step 3 — Deep Research Prompt

```
Scenario: [YOUR SCENARIO]

Do deep research and give me:
1. The real historical sequence of events (4-5 key beats)
2. The exact decision point or moment of chance where things could have 
   diverged
3. Two or three plausible alternate outcomes at that divergence, ranked 
   by likelihood, with reasoning
4. For the most likely outcome, trace ripple effects forward through 5 
   years, 20 years, and to today — military, political, economic
5. Separate every claim into three categories: Known Fact / Strongly 
   Supported Projection / Speculative Projection
6. Cite the sources backing this — primary sources, military historians, 
   academic scholarship, or recent research where available. Note where 
   historians disagree or where the sourcing is thin.

Keep this analytically rigorous. This becomes a reference bank I can 
point followers to if they ask "where did you get this."
```

## Step 4 — Plausibility Score

```
Based on the research above, give this scenario a Historical Plausibility 
Score out of 10 (10 = highly likely given the real conditions, down to 
2 = requires ignoring major logistical/political reality). Explain the 
score in 2-3 sentences. This will be shown on-screen or in the caption 
for credibility.
```

## Step 5 — Devil's Advocate Pass

```
Act as a military historian trying to prove this alternate timeline 
wrong. Point out every weak assumption, unrealistic logistical leap, 
political impossibility, and overlooked historical factor in the 
timeline above. Be ruthless. Then tell me if the timeline still holds up, 
or if it needs to be adjusted — and how.
```

Adjust the timeline based on what comes back before scripting.

## Step 6 — Your Unique Take

```
Given everything above, what unique insight, argument, or angle can 
Warrior King Productions bring to this that generic alternate-history 
content wouldn't? I have hands-on military operational experience — where 
does that lens change or sharpen the analysis compared to how a typical 
history channel would cover this?
```

Read this one carefully — this is where you edit in your own voice. Don't just accept it verbatim.

## Step 7 — Panel Script Prompt

```
Using all of the above (research, plausibility score, devil's advocate 
adjustments, and my unique take), write a 6-10 slide Instagram carousel 
script:
- Slide 1: Hook (bold line + specific date/moment)
- Slides 2-4: Real history, one beat per slide, under 20 words each
- Slide 5: The divergence point
- Slides 6-8: Alternate timeline unfolding, one consequence per slide
- Final slide: Ripple to today + plausibility score + a question to the 
  audience + follow CTA

For each slide give me the exact on-screen text and a Firefly image 
prompt using this style: [YOUR LOCKED STYLE DESCRIPTOR — see Step 8]
```

## Step 8 — Lock Your Visual Style Bible (one-time setup)
Write this once, reuse in every image prompt:
```
Camera/lens: [e.g. low-angle, 35mm cinematic]
Lighting: [e.g. dramatic, high-contrast, golden-hour or overcast]
Color palette: [e.g. desaturated, muted earth tones]
Weather/atmosphere: [e.g. fog, smoke, rain]
Realism level: [e.g. painted-photoreal hybrid]
Historical accuracy: uniforms, equipment, and terrain must match the 
correct year/theater
Texture: [e.g. subtle film grain]
Aspect ratio: 4:5 (1080x1350) for Instagram
```

## Step 9 — Generate Images in Firefly
Run each slide's prompt with your style bible appended. Generate 2-3 variants, pick the best.

## Step 10 — Build Your Photoshop Template (one-time setup)
Text layer, safe margins, Warrior King watermark, locked font/colors. Reuse for every carousel.

## Step 11 — Drop In Text + Export
Populate template, export PNGs at 1080x1350.

## Step 12 — Caption & Hashtag Prompt

```
Write an Instagram caption for this carousel on [SCENARIO]. Include:
1. A hook first line
2. 2-3 sentences of teaser copy that doesn't give away the full ripple 
   effect
3. The plausibility score, framed as a credibility marker
4. A closing question to drive comments
5. 10 broad hashtags + 10 niche/specific hashtags
```

## Step 13 — Post + Engage
Consistent posting time. Reply to every comment in the first hour.

## Step 14 — Track Performance
Log saves and shares per carousel — this is your signal for what becomes a YouTube episode.

# PART 2: YOUTUBE CINEMATIC "WHAT IF" EPISODE

## Step 1 — Confirm the Scenario Is Proven
Only greenlight once the Instagram carousel outperformed your average on saves/shares.

## Step 2 — Deep Scenario Modeling Prompt (expands on the IG research)

```
I'm expanding [SCENARIO] from a proven Instagram carousel into a full 
cinematic YouTube episode. Using the research, plausibility score, 
devil's advocate notes, and my unique take from that project, go deeper:

1. Full historical context — commanders, terrain, logistics, political 
   pressures at the time
2. The full decision space available at the divergence point (not just 
   one alternate outcome — the realistic range of choices)
3. Two or three distinct timelines branching from that point, ranked by 
   likelihood, each grounded in doctrine, logistics, and precedent
4. For each timeline: immediate consequences (days/weeks), short-term 
   (1-5 years), medium-term (one generation), and the ripple to today
5. Where these timelines might reconverge with real history vs. diverge 
   permanently
6. Full source list: primary sources, academic historians, military 
   papers, and recent scholarship — organized so I can reference or link 
   them if viewers ask
7. Flag any contested interpretations to caveat on-screen

This needs to hold up to viewers with real military knowledge.
```

## Step 3 — Retention-Focused Script Prompt

```
Using everything above, write a full narration script for a [10-15] 
minute episode. Identify and build in:
- A 30-45 second cold-open hook, no filler
- A first-minute payoff so viewers know what they're getting
- A pattern interrupt (new visual, new question, or twist) every 
  45-60 seconds
- A mid-video revelation
- The biggest surprise placed near the end, not buried early
- A final question or hook into the next episode

Structure the body as: real history → divergence point → timeline 
exploration → ripple to today → close.

For each section, give me production notes: what needs full 3D 
(Blender/UE5), what's motion graphics (After Effects), what can be AI 
filler (Runway), and where the plausibility score and my unique take 
(from the IG project) should be woven in on-screen.
```

## Step 4 — Storyboard
Rough panel sketches in Photoshop/Fresco, one per scene, mapped to shot type.

## Step 5 — Voice
Record narration in ElevenLabs (or your own voice).

## Step 6 — 3D Assets (Blender)
Build/import per production notes. Reuse and expand your library each episode.

## Step 7 — Cinematics (UE5)
Scenes, lighting, camera work in Sequencer per storyboard.

## Step 8 — Motion Graphics Prompt

```
Based on the script above, list every moment that needs an animated map 
or data overlay. For each: what the map shows, what animates (troop 
movements, borders, arrows), and roughly how many seconds it runs.
```

## Step 9 — Assembly Edit (Premiere)
Cut narration, UE5 renders, AE graphics, and music together.

## Step 10 — Color (DaVinci Resolve)
Grade to your locked visual style.

## Step 11 — Audio Mix (Audition)
Balance narration, music, SFX.

## Step 12 — Pre-Publish Devil's Advocate / Accuracy Pass

```
Review the final script and shot list one more time as a skeptical 
military historian: Are dates correct? Are uniforms and equipment correct 
for the year/theater? Are logistics realistic? Is every claim either 
supported by the sources gathered or clearly labeled as speculation? Is 
the title/thumbnail concept compelling without being misleading? Flag 
anything that needs a fix before this goes live.
```

## Step 13 — Thumbnail + Title Prompt

```
Give me 5 YouTube title options for [SCENARIO], optimized for curiosity 
and searchability without being clickbait. Then 3 distinct thumbnail 
concepts I can build in Firefly/Photoshop, each with a different visual 
hook (commander/face focus, map/battle focus, "versus" split-screen).
```

## Step 14 — Publish + Clip
Upload. Cut the strongest 30-60 second moment into a Short same-week, driving traffic back to the full episode.
