## Section 5. Speech and accent lock, by person

This is the speech lock. One block per person. Each block says where they are from, how they talk now on the page, what they never say, and my plan for showing their accent without making it hard to read.

Three ways to show an accent on the page, in plain words. Spelling means changing how a word is written, like runnin' or 'em. Word order means moving words around, like The door, it was open. Rhythm means how long the sentences are and how they bounce, with no spelling change at all.

My general rule, from your Annex. Use rhythm and word choice first. Add spelling last, two or three marked words the first time a person speaks in a chapter, then trust the reader. Never spell a word so hard that a reader has to stop and decode it.

Sample lines are copied word for word from your chapters and checked by a script against the chapter files. None are made up. Annex sample lines are not used here because they are not from your chapters.
