Paste one of these right after Harmon says Walk with me.

Chapter 4, make the Mjakazi history clear
Zac's ruling. Harmon helped Mjakazi escape, but Mjakazi broke the bond himself. Harmon worked from the shadows and did not watch his every step. Once Mjakazi was settled, before he married, Harmon stopped watching and only listened for tales. He never heard any because Mjakazi had changed. So Harmon did not know about the son until Weslin spoke of his father. Chapter 4 already has Harmon arranging the tavern, the dockyard job and the bakery from the shadows, but it never says three things. It does not say Mjakazi broke the bond, it does not say Harmon stopped watching, and it does not say when Harmon learned who the boy's father was. There are three small insertions.
Insertion A, when Harmon learned whose son Weslin is
Line before. Today’s work was at the end of this road, in a bakery that smelled of warm bread and everything Harmon was about to disturb.
Insert after it. Pick one of the options below.
Option 1, minimal. He had put it together an hour ago, from a few words about a father who baked. Harmon had lost track of that man years back. He had not known there was a son.
Option 2, immersive. An hour ago the boy had said enough about his father for Harmon to know exactly who he was. Harmon had lost track of that man years back. He had listened for a rumor, a name, anything at all, and heard nothing, because the man had made himself someone else. He had not known there was a son. He had not known there was a bakery.
Option 3, voice. An hour ago it clicked, from a few careless words about a father who baked. Harmon had listened for that man for years and heard nothing, because the man had changed. He had never once guessed at a son.
Line after. He fell into step beside the boy. “Do you think your father will welcome the news?”
Insertion B, Mjakazi broke the bond himself
Find. “Mjakazi, I risked my life to get you out. I brought you here to give you a chance at a new life.”
Option 1, minimal. "Mjakazi, I risked my life to get you out. I opened the door. You were the one who walked through it, and the chain in your chest was yours to break, not mine."
Option 2, immersive. "Mjakazi, I risked my life to get you out. I could open the door and nothing more. What was in your chest, you broke yourself. I only stood outside it. Then I brought you here to give you a chance at a new life."
Option 3, voice. "I got you out, Mjakazi. I did that much. The chain in your chest, you broke on your own. I never could have. Then I brought you here for a new start."
Insertion C, Harmon stopped watching
Find. “I did what I could. And when I saw you with Zeriah, happy, building a family, I knew you would be fine. You had finally found peace.”
Paste one of these straight after that closing quote, as the next lines of Harmon's speech, or fold it into the same quote.
Option 1, minimal. "After that I stopped watching. I listened for a name, a rumor, a story. Nothing ever came, because you had changed too well."
Option 2, immersive. "After that I let you be. I only listened for stories, a rumor of a man who baked, a name I knew. Nothing ever reached me. You had changed too well to be found, and I took that as a blessing."
