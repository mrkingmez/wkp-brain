Prompt Reference Document
This document contains all editing prompts, numbered to match the Updated Editing Calendar.
Stage 1: Structural & Flow (Sept 4 – Oct 15)
#2 Scene Change Check
Here’s Chapter [X]. Act as a developmental editor. Go scene by scene and tell me:

1. What changes in this scene (plot, emotion, tension)?  
2. If nothing changes, tell me exactly where it stalls.  
3. Suggest 2–3 ways I could adjust those moments so the scene ends with momentum.

Do NOT rewrite the scene. Just analyze shifts and suggest targeted fixes.
#8 Continuity & Timeline Check
Here’s Chapter [X]. Cross-check it against earlier chapters and tell me:

- Do the events line up logically?  
- Does character knowledge/behavior remain consistent?  
- Are there any contradictions in time of day, geography, or continuity?  
- If yes, flag the exact line and suggest how to align it.
Stage 2: Voice & Character (Oct 16 – Nov 15)
#1 Voice Bible Dialogue Alignment
Here is the Voice Bible for my characters and here is Chapter [X].

I want you to act as a professional line editor and apply ONLY the Voice Bible to the dialogue. 
Do not change narration, plot, or scene structure. 
Do not add or remove lines. Only adjust the words inside quotation marks so they fit the character’s voice and accent.

For each change, output:

Character: [Name]  
Original Line: "[original dialogue]"  
Revised Line: "[new dialogue]"

#5 Character Consistency Audit
Here’s Chapter [X]. Go through each speaking character and tell me:

1. Does this dialogue/narration match their Voice Bible?  
2. Flag any lines that sound out of character.  
3. Suggest how to tweak word choice to stay true.  
4. Tell me if the character voice drifted since earlier chapters.

Format:  
Character: [Name]  
Strongest Moment: [line that nails the voice]  
Weakest Moment: [line that drifts]  
Fix Suggestion: [voice-corrected option]

#6 Accent Authenticity Check
Here’s Chapter [X]. Using the Voice Bible accents, check every character with a distinct regional accent.

For each, flag:  
- Lines that sound too modern or generic.  
- Lines where the accent fades.  
- Suggested revision to keep their regional flavor without overdoing it.

Stage 3: Immersion & Style (Nov 16 – Dec 5)
#4 Immersion/Tension/Momentum (ITM) Pass
Here’s Chapter [X]. For each scene, break it down into:

- Immersion: Where can I add 1 sensory detail to deepen setting?  
- Tension: Is there emotional or situational pressure? If weak, suggest a tweak.  
- Momentum: What moves forward? If nothing, suggest how to raise stakes.

#3 Cut the Fat
Here’s Chapter [X]. Identify paragraphs or lines that are overwritten, repetitive, or slow pacing.

For each, output:  
Original: [line/paragraph]  
Issue: [why it’s weak]  
Cut Option: [shorter version with same meaning]  
Tension Option: [alternative that sharpens mood or pressure]

#7 Combat & Action Flow Audit
Here’s Chapter [X]. Analyze each action/combat sequence:

1. Does description bog down?  
2. Does the action reflect POV’s personality?  
3. Are stakes/tension clear?  

Suggest targeted fixes to streamline or heighten urgency.

Stage 4: Final Polish & Proof (Dec 6 – Dec 20)
#9 Grammar/Word Choice Polish
Here’s Chapter [X]. Perform a word-level polish. For each flagged line, show:

Original: [line]  
Correction: [line with grammar/spelling fix]  
Note: [why it reads better]

Do not rewrite style—only polish mechanics.

#10 Strengths & Weaknesses Report
Here’s Chapter [X]. Give me an editor’s report:

1. Two-paragraph summary of the chapter.  
2. Strengths: list what works (voice, pacing, imagery, dialogue).  
3. Weaknesses: list what falls flat.  
4. Suggested Focus: top 3 things to fix.

Be blunt, no flattery. Margin-note style feedback.

Stage 5: Wrap & Prep (Dec 21 – Dec 31)
Chapter Title Generator
Here’s Chapter [X]. Suggest 3 possible titles (2–6 words each). 
They must be evocative, tone-appropriate, and unique. 
Explain why each fits and recommend the strongest one.

Harmon’s Journal Entry
Here’s Chapter [X]. Summarize in 1–2 sentences, written as Harmon’s private journal entry. 

Voice: grim, clipped, duty-bound. Short, deliberate sentences. Rare humor, dark if present. 
Must sound like Harmon’s honest thoughts, not narration.

Final Global Checklist Prompt
Here is my full manuscript. Using the Final Global Checklist, analyze the whole book for:

- Name & lore consistency  
- Pacing balance (Action/Intrigue/Dialog/Exploration)  
- Emotional arcs flowing naturally  
- Sensory variety (not just sight/sound)  
- Metaphors fitting world/era/POV  
- Distinct voices in dialogue-only form  
- Any slow or dragging chapters  
- Clear hook and core stakes (query-ready)  
- Subtle series seeds without gaping loose ends

Give a detailed report with flagged examples and suggested fixes. Do not rewrite scenes.
