Jesfin was whistling a merry tune as he rode on the back of his horse Osse, as he travels the lone road back to the Capital of Morlin, from this back country of the realm.  The horse bubbling at each note of Jesfin toon as each note is off key and more wind than music.  Yet he just kept going on as if he didn’t have a care in the world.  “Oh, you stop your whining.”  The horse bray again at him, and he just laughed as they rode on.  Jesfin takes in his surrounding as he takes a breath and stops his tune for a bit.  The sun was on the lower third of the sky on it descend and the shadows, so the tress is now covering the entire rode.  “Well, what do you say there Osse, do you think it time to stop and make camp for the night, if we wait any long, I am afraid I will not be able to find a fresh meal tonight.  And you know I am tired of the hard meat and bread.  Yes, I do think it is time to stop.”
Jesfin takes in the area this time looking for a nice flat place to set up and tie Osse up, not that he is worried she would run off, but more to make sure no one runs off with her.  As he has heard the rumors of this area, the horrors some of the people told him before heading out, he made a visual show of checking of the dagger he was wearing on his leg.  They rode on for about an hour longer down the road before he spotted a place a that looked perfect for them to set up, it was a bit off the road, right by the tree line for some good game hunting.
