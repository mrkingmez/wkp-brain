	“What in the blue hell are you three doing?” Ask the man as he sees three people sitting around a table playing cards.  The verbal question shocked the three at the table, who were also dressed in guards’ uniforms.  When they see where the question was coming from and who asked it, all three of them sprang to their feet to the position of attention as they see it is Sergeant of the Guard Mu Pua.  Mu Pua walked closer to the three playing cards; he towered over each of them.  “I asked a question.” Boomed the commanding voice of Mu Pua. 
“Sir, we were just playing cards to kill the time.” Answer Alix Sentel, the guard who was closest to Mu Pua.  “These shifts up here can get really boring and just wanted something to kill the time.”  
“Just needed to kill some time is it?” Asked Mu Pua, in a calm tone from what he has shown.
One of the other guards chimed up the girl across from Alix Sental, she said. “Yes sir, that is it just something to get through the time, no one getting hurt and no gambling.  I mean nothing has happen in years, maybe even longer.  They are not even sure if anything is even out there anymore.”
Mu Pua turns towards the young female guard, “You are Shaed, right?”
“Ashlyh Shaed, sir.”
“Well Ashlyh,” He pauses then grabs the small table they all were sitting around and throws it over the side of the wall towards the pass. He then continues in a much louder and aggressive tone. “that is not your job to think or listen to rumors, is it.  You are here to make sure we are not going to get attack and yes, they are still out there.  You are here to be the watch on the wall to ensure we don’t get slaughtered.  We are here to protect from the creatures that still roam the frontier, not play cards.  Do that on you own time.”  He turns and walks over to the edge of the wall and looks off to the distance. “The problem with you new soldiers we don’t take you out to see what you are really fighting against here.  That is what we use to do with all the new recruits or first timers here, we would take them out there.: He points out towards the fronter.  “We would test their metal against the nightmares that live there.  We lost better people than you will ever be.  We were forged into solders out there.” He then removes his helm reveling a long scar on his face from the corner of his left eye down to his jaw line. A distinct feature against his tan skin.  “So here is what I am going to do, you three will have wall duty for the next week straight, you will have mid-day until mid-night, to bed then back to the wall, your only other authorize stops is the dinner hall and the wash rooms, nowhere else.  If I so much as hear a rumor that you are doing anything else, I will throw you over the wall myself, am I understood?”
The three guards said in unison, “Yes sir.”
“And if you are slaking off again during your shift again, I will have you hung for dereliction of duty, understood?”
Again, all together, “Yes, sir.”
“Good, you are luck it was me who caught you, our new lord will be here today and from what I hear he is stricter that I am.”
Ashlyh spoke up, “New lord. Sir?”
“Yes, the king has sent a new regiment up here, as Sir Jorah goes back after three years up here.  I forget a lot of you have not been up here long, this happen ever so offen.  Sir Jorah will return to his keep with his solders and the new lord comes in for three years of time.  Lumps like you will be here always as this is where you are assigned unless the lord recruits into his regiment.  That will not happen if you keep playing cards.”
The third guard who has not said anything yet, “Sir, when is he supposed to get here, and who is it?”
“His caravan just reached the outer gate and should be in the fort in an hour or two as they make their way through Variz.  It is going to be Sir Evens Garkins of Woford.”
All of their face grew into a shocked look, Alix was first to speak, “The Sir Evens Garkins, he led the force again the costal raids as well as the rebellion of the east.” 
Before Mu Pua could answer, Ashlyh spoke, “It is said he is going to be a ring bearer taken over the Brotherhood and led us against the great evils return.”
Then the third guard turns to the Sergeant and asked, “Is that turn sir, is he going to be a ring bearer, is he here to led us into the fronter and take it back?  Is here to rebuild the shared empire?’
“Damn, not just lazy bumps on a log, but one who believe in children fairy stories as well.  No, he is here to be the lord of the garrison and hold the pass protecting the lands of man.  I can’t tell you who will be a ring bearer or not or if there are really rings at all.”
The third guard, who closes to Mu Pua Hight stood a bit straighter before he spoke, “Sergeant, these are not children stories or folk tales, there are rings they are real.   You right about one thing you nor does anyone know who will bear one for the rings choose who will wield one.” 
“Oh, are they now,” Said Mu Pua as he got into the face of the third guard.  He gets a bewilder look on his face as he looks the man under her helm.  “Who are you again?  I thought I knew everyone on shift.”
The guard steps a bit closer to the Sergeant just smiling.  To Mu Pua eye’s it looked as if the guard was growing in size with each step.  The other two guards start to move closer to Mu Pua all facing this creature walking towards the three of them.
“Sergeant you were right about another thing too, we are nightmares out there, and today we become yours again.” Said the third man as continues his slow walk towards the sergeant.  He removes his helm, to show his glowing eyes. He stops and just looks at the three guards.  His human feature start to melt away as his face grows pale, almost pure white.  His eyes grow dark with glowing iris, his nose is gone and its face is smooth with no texture to it at all.  
“Who are you?” Ask Alix as with fear lacing his voice.
Then it speaks again, “You are right Alix I guess you should know the name of the person who is going to kill you, I am Dar. “Oh, Mu Pua it has been too long hasn’t it, you don’t even believe the stories anymore.  Let me be the one to tell you, they are true, all of them.  So, think hard and it will come to you.”
“Lucent?”
“Very good.  This is what he wanted me to tell you.” He pauses.  An arrow takes Alix in the neck as he drops to the ground.  He laid there gasping for breath as blood just poured out of him pooling around his head as he died slowly.  Then another arrow out of the emptiness, right in the eyes of Ashlyn, killing her instantly.  Mu Pua looks at his two solders dead at his feet.  Rage builds in the Sergeant as he takes a battle stance, by placing his left foot behind him giving him a good center of gravity, he has his sword in hand.
“Don’t just stand there, come at me man, let get this done.” Barked Mu Pua

Mu Pua just gasped in pain, dropping his sword as Dar lower him next to his dead two soldiers.  Dar gets really close to his ear and whispers, “The time of Man is done.” Dar darts back up to his feet as two short green creatures join him.  Mu Pua hears him giving orders.  
“Take up fire position up here take out as many as you can, I will get the gate open for our bothers.  Give me a few minutes to get in place to open the gate before you start raining death on all of them.”
