3. Zhaelor expects to turn on his master (SG7). SG1 says his closing line is about standing instead. Both recorded, not reconciled.
4. Edric and Mirelle's plan reaches past Aldoria (SG7).
5. Luca holds the Judgement Stone and can make his own seed stone. How the Stone moved from Adin to Luca is Book 2 and off screen by magic (LOCKED).
6. The Dark Touch will be Luca and Nyssa (SG3).
7. Nyssa remade, contract price (severance from Dain), Dain's lock of hair.
8. Mjakazi's sword and his Sentinel past; the Chainheart Sigil and Master of Chains (unnamed shadowy figure).
9. Reau, the only Shardborn; Kaelen Dray (rogue Alchemist-Scribe) still unaccounted for.
10. Kaelith the Zhar'ai Runeweaver and Taerel's rune ability.
11. Lucent's hidden third son; First Sword.
12. Irothis page; Riven's fate (open).
13. The crowned figure older than Korr; Sythrael Korr, the gate and the unsealed rune-gate.
14. Cotter is dead (no one knows); Ting; Opindale court loose ends.
15. Breaches and the Debt (thinning bloodlines).
16. Ringbearer count, seven unclaimed rings (SG, ruled by Zac), and the Nine with the secret ninth (never reveal).
17. Dain's surname and the Rendsdale cover.
18. Weslin's parentage (Sentinel line) held off the page.
19. Ash's origin (barrel of waste, mother's broken charm, Cindertongue), unresolved.
20. Counts and survivors inconsistent on purpose (SG4, SG5, SG7). Do not tidy.
21. Fort Dreknor vs the Pass fort (Zac's call).
22. Open items Zac owns: OD-1 to OD-7 in canon_ledger.md.
