## CAST BLOCKS

### HARMON (Diem)
Region/class: neutral Common baseline, longevity overlay (no pure regional identity, old regional patterns flicker under stress). Educated Professional (PERF).
Cadence: measured, little wasted, silence after heavy lines. "Words land like they were tested before release." Under anger/fatigue: contractions drop, sentences shorten, calmer, lower, more dangerous (ANNEX).
Humor: dry, slightly off, "a soldier who learned humor late" (PERF). "Try not to die. I have buried enough heroes already."
Forbidden: rapid-fire, street phrasing, filler, over-explaining, soft mentor only, threats where silence is stronger (ANNEX).
Anchor: "We move." not "We need to move." ANNEX: "You can argue with me after we survive. I may even pretend to listen."

### ASH (Caedric Valen)
Common, Oppindale cadence + blacksmith/dockworker. Fast, contractions heavy, short punches, jokes are armor, interrupts tension. Under fear humor thins: "That's bad." Forbidden: formal cadence, long metaphor chains, noble diction. Phonetics allowed (ANNEX).
Anchor: "Yeah, great. Locked door, blood smell, and a hallway that screams trap. Beautiful."

### BROTHER ALDREK
Opindale, former knight, priest. Slow, deliberate, full sentences, rare contractions, no profanity, moral framing, volume lowers when angry, old command voice returns. Forbidden: casual slang, sarcasm, swearing, generic church voice.
Anchor: "I have buried men for less than what you are considering. Do not ask me to bless it."

### RIVEN DUSKWIND
Bridge class code-switcher, Aldoria? (ANNEX unsure), urban criminal. Quick, practical, charm sharpens to surgical, reframes others' words, smile drops under real threat. NOT a male Nyssa, avoid "mischievous". Anchor: "I can lie prettier if you want, but the ugly version saves time."

### DAIN KERSWELL (cover Lord Alric Rendsdale)
Opindale fallen noble, Bridge class. Polished court sarcasm, educated warmth, anger sharpens into formality, awkwardly polite vulgarity, avoids fragments (PERF). Not Aldorian cadence. Anchor: "Careful. I am being civil because the room is crowded, not because I have forgotten how to be honest."

### NYSSA QUILL
Opindale urban/mercenary, Bridge+Educated hybrid. Fast, sharp, flirtatious, sarcasm 8/10, indirect answers, two meanings per line, softer and "lost" when exposed, humor drops and lines shorten under fear. Anchor: "Do not mistake the smile for mercy. I smile at locked doors too, right before I pick them."

### WESLIN
Opindale small-town, homeschooled, 14 (ANNEX) or 13 (PERF). Young, anxious, bright, hesitates and self-corrects, blurts observations, over-explains to prove himself, stutter ONLY under anxiety and not constant, deep terror can bring strange clarity. Must NOT sound like a scholar, strategist or analyst. Older cadences from his father slip in occasionally. Wonder audible early. Anchor: "I know what I saw. I wish I didn't, but I do." / "I think-- no, wait."

### AURELIA
Professor-calm, controlled, analytical, dialect master; true voice unstable, NO stable true voice. Surgical under stress, emotional breaks rare and unsettling. Voice shifts must be known performances.

### REAU
Oppindale plain. Few words, no metaphor ever (IDIOM), quieter under stress, emotion through action. Not poetic, not silent because of the collar. Anchor: "Say where to stand. I'll hold it." Wears binding collar. Shardborn status is off page.

### NEAVIN
Hill/mountain miner, medium-heavy dialect, dropped endings, ain't/reckon/ye/yer/gonna/'em/nothin'/coulda/shoulda/figger. Land, stone, weather, mine metaphors. Manners under the grit (mother raised him). Quieter and more focused in danger. Thickens when tired, angry, among his own. Ch 7 is the anchor. Anchor: "Ain't sayin' it's wise, sir. Just sayin' the mountain's already made up her mind." Earth-creature bond, stone manipulation.

### GARR (Garran Thornebrook)
Unnamed eastern country, Texan-like (PERF: Kareth, Southern/Texas rhythm). Plainspoken, warm when safe, blunt under pressure, direct, short, tactical, leadership insecurity, not Neavin. Anchor: "I ain't sayin' I know the right road. I'm sayin' somebody's gotta pick one before the dark does it for us."

### LYSANA / LYSARA VAELOR
Moradaer scout (Swedish/Norwegian lift, PERF). Confident, direct, warm when safe, bold when teasing, aggressive and fearless under pressure, wants permanence. Not Nyssa. Dead by Ch 40 aftermath. Anchor: "Easy. If death wanted us neat, it should've brought better manners."

### LUCA
Chameleon: no fixed voice, mimics the room, multiple languages. Compact, short-to-medium sentences, stress becomes focus not panic, quiet rare humor to Aymir/Lysana. Not the same grim assassin voice in every scene. Born in the First Magic War (EP ruling). Anchor: "I can be what they expect. That is easy. Being what you need is the part I have not learned."

### AYMIR
Altisflumen, older, disciplined, risk-taker, clear structured educated, soldier-buddy humor, "We do this clean. Then we go home." Humor vanishes under loss. Not stiff, not Taerel.

### TAEREL
Altisflumen aristocrat, Victorian London flavor, formal dry controlled, thinks in odds/wagers/tells/bad hands, self-corrects to exact words, less talkative, injured arm later (grunts, clipped pauses). Anchor: "Poor odds, ugly table, and no honest dealer in sight. Still, I have played worse hands." NOT Aymir, NOT frontier.

### ADIN
Ancient stillness, declarative, no contractions under threat, calm threat. Public voice evokes Luthen Rael: composed, layered, weary, dangerous. Private: whispering, protective, haunted. Vague on purpose. "You misunderstand the scale."

### KING EDRIC (Wymark)
High nobility, balanced proclamation sentences, warm vision, "My father dreamed...", warmth with Mirelle, public ceremonial, private reflective. Did not appoint Drake. Not naive. Avoids sarcasm.

### QUEEN MIRELLE
Silk and steel, public musical poised, private cooler shorter. Quieter under stress. Never loses control with the assassin. Daughter of Adin, unknown to her.

### GOVERNOR MERIDIAN DRAKE (first name unconfirmed, do not use on page)

### LORD MERRICK (Iven)
High nobility, calm surgical, never cartoon cruel, "How ambitious." Under pressure ornament fades. Personal sigil four foxes around hammers.
