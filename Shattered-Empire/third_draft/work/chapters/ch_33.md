#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the team-up chapter before the Stormhaven missions split, and it has a lot of heart. The banter between Dain and Nyssa is the best of its kind in the book, the dress and the garter of knives are a character reveal and not just a gag, and Reau's two scenes with Weslin are the strongest in the chapter. His warning about the ring ("the ring comes first") and the collar are real plants, and Aurelia's shield lesson gives the book a rule it will need when the mind-control reveal arrives. Harmon and Garr on the way out are good for the first half, with the cane that is not for walking and the line about "ghosts in our pockets". The problems are mostly bookkeeping. The pairs Harmon names in the loft are not the pairs who leave, Dain and Nyssa leave twice, Aldrek prays three times, the cane appears before it is picked up, Harmon gives the meeting order three times, and the closing talk with Garr slides into aphorism and meta-commentary. Chapter 34 gets the same care as this one, because the stored simile note for this chapter is in Chapter 34's text.
