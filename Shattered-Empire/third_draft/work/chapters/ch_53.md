#TITLE Chapter 53
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the debrief and the payoff chapter, and it mostly earns it. The walk to the stable is the best opening in the back third. Weslin's "What does it really take?", Harmon's "The blade comes last" and the ring answer ("This morning kind") give Harmon the wisdom and the tact the alpha reader wanted, and the line about the iron door being a story to let go of is a good small plant. The pardons scene is clean and quiet ("The words went into the room and didn't come back"). Reau's walk to the door, Adin's scroll and Reau staying "for that much" are a strong, unexplained beat. Nyssa's exit is the heart of the chapter, and the reveal that Dain was in the room all along has real force. Garran refusing the half-name ("Lysana gave me that name. It was hers to give, and it stays with her") is the best line in the chapter and pays off a name that has run since early in the book. The coin left on the table is a good closing image. The weak points are these. Everything told at the table is a retelling of scenes the reader has just seen, and the Lysana account does not match Chapter 52. Nyssa "sees two empty seats" while Dain is speaking in the same room, and the scene narrates his lines before she looks up. Adin says "tonight" in the morning of a stable meeting days after the Feast. The Nine appear where your notes say the Eight. The "the way a man does X" simile habit runs through the back half (Taerel's chair, Taerel's "unhurried precision", the silence "the way it gathers around a man", the name "the way names land", Dain's "particular stillness"). Zac's five stored notes are applied (two Keeps, three cuts). The whole chapter uses straight quotes, so the batch fix is large.
