#TITLE Chapter 34
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the investigation chapter, and it is the best use of the occupied city so far. Four pairs go out and each one comes back with a piece of the same shape. The boutique gives the tea invitation that smells like a trap, the blind man gives the warning that the nobles are gone though their voices walk, the apothecary and the temple woman give the name Korr and the way it slides out of the mind, and Tevin gives the best beat of the chapter when the name takes his memory in front of Riven and Ash and he closes the door smiling. The name that cannot be held is a fresh and frightening device, and it works because every witness behaves a little differently. Dain and Nyssa carry the opening on charm, Neavin and Aldrek and Aurelia get the temple with its scrubbed plaques, and Riven's history with Tevin is a strong thread. The weak spots are in the plumbing. Reau counts sources he could not have seen, Tevin tells the same story twice, the shop is reached twice, several characters drop their contractions and sound like a report, and a few places explain what the scene already shows. The ending, with the note that says not to trust what people say, especially me, is the right close.
