#TITLE Chapter 13
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the first Pass-team chapter in a while, and it plays like a different book in a good way. The storm, the bandits and the campfire are cold, quiet and physical, and the four riders have a real rhythm already (Taerel's mattress joke, Aymir's "Dried feet with ambition," Lysana's "Don't knock ambition"). Luca's shadow work on the road is quick and ugly, and the best beat in it is the one after the killing, when Taerel says he needs "some time to decide how I feel about traveling with you" and Lysana answers with one word, "Both." The campfire ghost story that mimics the voice of its prey, and the scream that comes in the night, are well planted. The weaker parts are the long watch passages, which are mood without incident, and a run of stock similes in the storm description. A character list slips in mid-action (a shoulder brush that carries Taerel's skin, eyes and braid). The most important fix is the name. Aymir is spelled Aymer twenty-three times in this chapter. The Zac note on Aymir's wary look at Lysana is logged and used. The campfire stories and the closing stack of "night waited" lines need a trim.
