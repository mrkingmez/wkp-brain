#FIRST IMPRESSION
Taerel finally takes the mask off, and the first half is the best of the chapter. The reveal is staged well, with Aymir giving him the nudge, the horses stopping, the face gone with no ceremony, and Lysana's "You could have warned a girl before you turned into a walking night sky" landing the laugh. Luca not flinching and Aymir's "Told you he was dramatic" keep it light, and the brother explanation gives Aymir and Taerel a real history. The second half is a recon chapter, and it is competent. The camp is well drawn (the tar, the forge smoke, the totem, the staff that matches the last one), Luca's urge to strike and Lysana's "No" is a clean character beat, and "Orcs don't do siege warfare" is the right alarm. But the chapter is long for what it does. The team counts the camp, reports it, scouts again, counts it again, reports it again, and asks twice how to reach the survivors. The rotation plan is given twice, the careful-not-blunder line is said by four people, and the closing paragraph is a prophecy line the chapter does not need. Taerel also slips into a modern register in two lines, and there is a run of small mechanical breaks, including a paragraph split mid-sentence.
