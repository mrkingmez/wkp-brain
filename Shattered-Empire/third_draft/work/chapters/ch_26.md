#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is a quieter, tighter chapter than 25 and it earns its length. It opens inside Aymir's head with a smell, and the burning oil sends him back to a war the reader has not seen, which is the best use of Aymir so far. Taerel's knuckle against his arm is the right gesture, and the bet about the worg is the right way out of it. The arrow with the note is Lysana's idea, built from what is on the page (the volleys, the quiver, the cloth), and the answer from the church in dyed bedding flags is a clean payoff. The farewell between the brothers is the emotional center, with the coin, the two ritual lines and "tell Dad I tried", and it works because nobody explains it. The closing flag message and "Thank you, God the Creator" give the chapter a real exit. What holds it back is small. The chapter changes place and time four times with no scene break, the church was a chapel in Chapter 25, the dialogue has two voice slips (Taerel's "Hey" and Lysana asking Aymir to explain his rifle for the reader), a handful of stock similes sit in the weather paragraphs, and the last line sends Taerel off with a cliche.
