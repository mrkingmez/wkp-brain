#TITLE Chapter 9
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the first chapter of the book that moves like a story on the road. Weslin sees his first town and the town is alive (the bakery woman, the fox in the reeds, the man arguing with himself about which way his sign should face). Harmon splits the teams and hands out the bracelets with real teeth ("He says I need new clothes" is the best small joke so far). Aurelia takes Weslin on. Then Nyssa gets burned by a merchant, runs, and the scene on the road, "I know. I ruined it," is the second great Nyssa and Harmon beat in two chapters. The ending, with Weslin looking back at Rellisford (he did not look back at Opindale), pays off a quiet plant from Chapter 8. The weaknesses are on the page. The opening runs eight paragraphs of town before anything happens and leans on smells and similes. A duplicated sentence sits in the whistle beat, a name is spelled wrong (Taeral), two quote marks are broken, and a thought is unitalicized. The team roster, the bracelets and the Barrowmead stop all match the ledger, which is a good sign for the next chapters.
