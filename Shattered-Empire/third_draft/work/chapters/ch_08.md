#TITLE Chapter 8
#FIRST IMPRESSION
After the noise of Chapter 7 this is the quiet boat chapter, and it is a good one once it gets moving. The first dozen paragraphs are dock, fog and river, and they read like a writer clearing his throat. Then Adin and Harmon talk on the quay, Harmon gives the briefing, Dain's "compensation question" gets answered, and Nyssa finds him at the rail. Her scene is the best thing in the chapter. "He is family. Not blood. The kind that means more than blood because you chose it" and "Is that…" / "It means get some rest, Nyssa" are the voices working as they should. The problems are craft and bookkeeping. The boat is "smaller, sleeker" in one paragraph and "wide and flat-bottomed, built to haul cargo" in another. Harmon says Mr. Holt holds the pay, then says Adin does. A line from a famous thriller turns up in his mouth. A typo ("dry from thrust") sits in the middle of a briefing. Nothing here is deep damage.
