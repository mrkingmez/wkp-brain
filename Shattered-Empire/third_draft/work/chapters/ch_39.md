#TITLE Chapter 39
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the poisoning chapter, and it is the most fun the book has had in a while. Luca's infiltration works because it keeps almost going wrong, with the dropped orb on the rim, the cook's cleaver over his head and the Goblin thrall who just stands there and, to Luca's lasting regret, relieves himself. "I got held up, and pissed on. Not in that order" is the line that makes the scene. The long wait on the ridge is the book's warmest ensemble stretch, with the spoon-and-fork story, "Shadow finally found himself a candle" and Lysana's "Start thinking about how it begins". The turn is clean. Just as the poison works and the camp falls, the ground opens and the Joblins pour out, and the chapter's quiet theme, that the orcs' discipline was a screen for the digging, comes due. The problems are mostly bookkeeping. The poisons Luca carries and the poisons Chapter 37 laid out are not the same set, the ale is on the wrong side of the camp, and the soldier count has drifted from the ledger. The chapter also states its main idea about the orcs' cracks three times, pads the wait with a second round of Dorn stories, and uses the held breath again. Zac's three simile cuts are applied as Option A.
