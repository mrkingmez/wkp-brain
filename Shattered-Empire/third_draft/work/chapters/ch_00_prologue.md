#TITLE Prologue
#FIRST IMPRESSION
A clean, effective cold open. In about fifteen hundred words it gives you a gruff sergeant, a set of ordinary guards you like quickly, a quiet third man who is not what he seems, and a villain with a message. The dread works because Vey is a believable hard case and the card game is human. The ash smell on the wind is the right small detail. Where it weakens is the back half. Once Dar has stabbed Vey, three separate speeches tell him in different words that he will live and carry the message, and the same "time of man is over" idea lands twice. The dagger also goes in, comes out, and goes in again without the page admitting it. The mixed straight and curly quotes show this file was pasted from more than one place. The tone, the stakes and the hook are all in good shape.
