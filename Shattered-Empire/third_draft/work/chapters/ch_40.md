#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the chapter the Pass thread has been walking toward, and it hits hard. The Joblin tide, the Blackfire orbs, the twelve orcs that stand back up, and Fenrick's death are fast and bloody, and the action reads clearly because Zac keeps the beats short and the sounds loud. Lysana's death is the heart of it. It is not a clean beat, since she is stabbed in the neck, has a few seconds, and smiles. Luca's reaction is the right one, a man who does not scream and moves instead, then stops. Aymir's "No, no no no" and Taerel who "didn't breathe" are the best secondary notes. The darkness when Luca pulls everything from the shard is the scariest passage in the book so far, because it is mostly sound. The last line, "I did.", is quiet and exactly right. The chapter works because nothing is explained. The problems are small. Taerel has two orbs and then a "last one" after two throws. The darkness falls on a "grove" in a valley camp, and a goblet is dropped on a stone floor in the middle of a battlefield. The chapter says Taerel "had never seen death up close" about a man who has fought orcs. The prose uses "death" as a stock word five times in a page and leans on the same phrases for the swarm. Nothing here needs a rewrite, and the death scene should be trimmed, not rebuilt.
