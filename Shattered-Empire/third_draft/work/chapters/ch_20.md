#TITLE Chapter 20
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is Luca's chapter and the book's central origin story, and it works. The ride and the camp give the pass team its first warm hour (Aymir's cursed elk with antlers made of knives, Taerel's "Perish the thought entirely", the goat scream), the camp routine delays the confession long enough to make the reader lean in, and the confession itself is the strongest piece of storytelling so far. Luca drops into a formal voice for the telling, Ariel is given pity and a flaw, and the beats land in order (the healed mind, the first true word "Eryel", the killing in front of the angel, the request to walk without mercy). The Night Veil and the Judgement Stone arrive without a lecture. Lysana's hand stopping at his glove is the quiet emotional peak. What holds it back is craft housekeeping. A negation turn ("not X but Y") appears in the narration six times. The telling has two continuity knots, the paragraph after "killed Eryel in front of him" slides from Luca's story into narration without quote marks, and the burial comes before Luca finds Ariel kneeling at the body. The Pearl line contradicts the locked fact (the Pearl shattered in the Second War, Luca was born in the First). Lysana is ahead of the brothers and then behind them. The quote marks in the brothers' signing scene are in three different styles. The Zac find (paragraph 107) and the Pearl ruling (paragraph 109) are both logged first.
