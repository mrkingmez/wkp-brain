#TITLE Chapter 19
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the book's best argument scene, and it earns its length. Two pairs come home angry, the team splits along real lines (Garr and Ash over the archive, Nyssa and Neavin over the street), Harmon shuts it down with a fist on the table, and then the chapter does something braver. Aurelia, who was warm and patient with Weslin in the morning, turns on the whole room and then on the boy, and Harmon follows her into the corridor and takes her arm. That sequence is the spine. The Merrick note, the broken seal and Nyssa's colour leaving her face give the second half a clean turn, and the planning at the end (the bread carts, the second bell, Weslin's idea about the crowd) is the plan, brief, execute, adapt cadence at its most satisfying. The voices are strong. Garr is plainspoken, Ash is funny and tired, Nyssa is controlled until she is not, Harmon is exact. What holds the chapter back is a habit. The prose explains every silence with a rule ("the kind of silence that happens when", "the way rooms do when", "the way decisions get made between people who"), and the habit runs from the opening paragraph to the last. Zac has already caught ten of them and I have logged each as Option A. The rest are in the AI tell items below. There are also a few housekeeping slips (the note is called a foreman's note, Aurelia stands at the far wall and then by the door, Harmon unfolds a note he handed back, "Second lesson" after a morning that already was one).
