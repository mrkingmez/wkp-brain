#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the breath between the strike and the infiltration, and it does its job. Harmon alone at the window with the ring on his finger is the strongest stretch, because it gives him a body (aching knees, a knot under the shoulder blade), a cost (the ring he has "stopped counting") and a reason to be the commander the others lean on. The breakfast is warm and the assignment scene is the book's plan, brief, execute, adapt cadence at work, with each pair given a job and a joke. Dain's husbands, Nyssa's peach, Aldrek's two-word "Fed. Focused." and Ash's "Still alive. That's my standard lately" all land. What holds it back is the opening. The first twenty-four paragraphs carry a simile in almost every sentence (fallen leaves, an old coat, an army held back with teeth, a living machine, broken teeth, a confession to the morning, an old friend), and several of them are borrowed from earlier chapters. The chapter also contradicts itself on who is where at breakfast. Garr is already downstairs and then walks up to the table, and Aurelia is at the table and then pacing upstairs. The survivor count (ten) cannot be right after Chapter 16's deaths. Weslin is "the scout" once. The Zac cut in paragraph 1 and the ring note in paragraph 12 are logged as Option A.
