#TITLE Chapter 48
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the escape chapter, and the engine of it is good. Korr's astral projection bursting through the windows is a strong cold open, the sealed door "that was not there before" is a clean creepy beat, and Aldrek's summoned guardian is the best use of his faith in the book. The wind-catch shaft pays off the crates from Chapter 47. Ash talking the boy through the jump is warm, and Dain's dry summary of what Aldrek did ("with a limp arm and then taking a bolt down a shaft for the privilege") is a gift. The bell countdown that closes the chapter, one chime per beat while the three groups split, is the best structural device in the last act. The weak points are fixable. The cost of Korr's projection is stated five times. Several cart and character assignments disagree with each other (Briala is called the boy's mother, Ash rides alone beside a cart he is guarding, Aurelia and Neavin change carts on the page). The split is announced twice. The carts wait on a rise in one paragraph and in a grain passage in the next. Harmon's recognition scene (the crowned figure) tells his backstory in four overlapping gestures ("a different era", "under a different sky", "over centuries", "thirty years"), and one of those lines states what Korr is to the crowned figure, so I log it as a question and add nothing. Zac's three stored simile cuts are applied as written. Nothing here changes who Korr is or who the crowned figure is.
