#TITLE Chapter 6
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the chapter where the book catches fire, literally. Weslin hearing thoughts on the balcony is the best new idea in the first six chapters, and the way it builds is exactly right: one thought, then three, then a flood he cannot sort. Allison is a joy (“You look like you swallowed a loaf and regretted it”), Braden is a real bully with a real answer waiting for him, and the "Burn!" beat lands because the reader has watched Weslin get pushed down for a hundred lines. Harmon's "That is mine to carry, not yours" is the right note. What holds it back is page work. A garbled paragraph sits at 98, a misspelled name, an em dash, a stray quote, and Harmon explains the Adept mechanics in a speech that reads like a briefing. Zac's seven simile notes are all good calls. The chapter's biggest structural flaw is small: Allison is shown leaving twice.
