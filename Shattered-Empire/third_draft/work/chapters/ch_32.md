#TITLE Chapter 32
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the book's best city chapter so far, and the reason is restraint. Stormhaven is not shown by crowds or battle. It is shown by what is missing (no rats, no beggars, no noise), by one woman sweeping a clean stoop, and by one man in a blue tunic who drops like a cut string and is stood back up on his corner. That jerk is the scariest thing in the chapter, and Weslin's one word for it, "correction", is the right word. The entry through the smugglers' shaft and the Underlane gives Riven his home ground and a reason to be gloomy about it, the team banter keeps its edge, and the curfew bells land like a door closing. The weak spots are repeats. Riven's report says again what the chapter has already shown, the "not X, not Y" device runs three times in a few pages, Neavin and Dain slip out of their voices once each, the minutes between "dark" and "running out of light" do not agree, and Weslin's relay from Luca leaves out the plan he says Luca has. The ending is strong, though the last line has been used before.
