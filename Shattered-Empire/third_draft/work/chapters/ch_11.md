#TITLE Chapter 11
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is a setup chapter in three rooms, and it works better than it should. The lesson scene gives Weslin and Aurelia their first slow, real exchange ("You can't catch a river in a closed fist" is the best line of the chapter). Neavin's view of Veyrith, with the animal thoughts in the first person ("Humans chitter like mice"), is a good strange moment. Then Harmon hands out jobs, and the best beat of the chapter is Weslin's quiet reading of "observation is your mission tonight" as a consolation prize, and his decision not to hear it that way. Aurelia's linking of the team is the first time the magic does something the reader can picture, and the banter on the tether ("Chains dressed in finer words") is lively. The chapter is weaker in the lesson dialogue, where Aurelia explains and Weslin answers by rote, and at its end, where three stock closers stack up. The mechanical load is high: tense slips, doubled speech tags (four), unclosed and stray quotes, a mixed-up Veyrith, and two continuity problems that matter (Aurelia's eye color, and Garr having no job and no link). The Zac note on the chant is the only one in this chapter.
