#TITLE Chapter 50
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the landing after the pass battle, and it is a quiet, strong chapter. After four chapters of Stormhaven noise, it hands the book to Aymir and Taerel in the snow with Lysana's body on the third horse, and it lets them grieve without a speech. The brothers' talk is the heart of it. The three roads home, ship work and the old thieving trade are real choices with real costs, and Aymir's "I didn't like all of who I was in it. But I liked not guessing who I should be" is the best line either brother has. The coin in the sunrise is a good small image, and the laugh over "Dad" at the end earns its warmth. Corren's account of the changeling is the plot payload, and it lands as horror because the smell ("sweet, like overripe fruit ... and underneath it, oil") is concrete. The weak points are specific. Aymir recalls Corren's description at paragraph 49, long before they meet him. The slogan is counted as "Seven words" and it has six. The pass team's thread returns with no time anchor after four Stormhaven chapters. The brothers' talk runs about thirty paragraphs and restates its options once, and silence is described four times. The "filed it away" motif runs three times in the inserted passages. Zac's five stored notes are handled as written (three cuts, two Keeps), and the Brotherhood leader with three fingers gone is left unexplained per the open decision. I say nothing about who or what Korr is.
