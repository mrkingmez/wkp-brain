#TITLE Chapter 43
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the tower team's approach chapter, and it is the strongest "plan, brief, execute, adapt" run since the Pass. Garr's crew moves like people who have done this before. Nyssa kills without a sound, Ash and Aldrek take the third man, Neavin's falcon scouts, and the moldy bread "still arranged with care" says everything about the city in four words. Aurelia's seizure is the set piece, and it earns it. The accent "that didn't quite belong", the babble about veils and cracks in the firmament, then the whole district freezing to listen is the best creep in the book so far. The puzzle-door sequence that closes the chapter is a good team beat. The lock, the collapse, the key, the hidden seam and the four columns turn into a trust exercise, and "Let the stone tell you" is a fine line for Aldrek. Two things hold it back. The chapter explains the tower twice (the opening paragraphs and again in paragraphs 25 and 26), and the pep talk to Ash runs on too long, with Ash arguing the same point three times and Garr repeating the exit plan twice. A few lines of the puzzle also say the same thing more than once, and the lock chain leaves a second key lying unused. Some of the dialogue drops its contractions and reads stiffly. The prose carries a handful of stock beats ("it was a message", "a scream that would haunt the dead", "That felt earned"). The Dain and Nyssa moment at the lock is a quiet payoff of Chapter 41 and works. Aurelia's voice stays unstable, as locked.
