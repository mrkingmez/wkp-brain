#TITLE Chapter 10
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the chapter where the book stops being a road trip and becomes a team. Forgehallow is a real place (the filtered helms, the fans, the chalkboard with "NO QUESTIONS" underlined twice, the stablemaster with the tiny pliers) and the Iron Flask is a warm, believable room. Dain's platinum coin is a quick, funny exchange that also characterizes Garr and Harmon in six lines ("I will need change, and have them give you a receipt"). The strongest beat is Weslin saying he does not want to be the boy who knocked over the armor. It pays off Chapter 4 and it is the first time the reader hears what he wants. The weakest stretch is the second half of the tavern scene. Seven characters explain the team's alignment aloud ("It's alignment. Temporary. Functional.") and the narrator then restates it ("Not warm. Not cold. Solid."). The opening is another stack of scenery, with two similes before anyone moves. The mechanical problems are many and plain: two unclosed speeches, a stray quote pair, a typo ("then,o"), a doubled speech tag, and a run of present-tense slips in the drink scene. Continuity is mostly sound. A few small questions are for Zac, not for me to decide.
