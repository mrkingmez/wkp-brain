#TITLE Chapter 18
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This chapter is two scenes and the second one is the better. Aurelia teaching Weslin to listen is a good idea (hearing is weather, listening is will) and the best beats are the small wrong notes in Aurelia herself, the candle that leans toward Weslin and thinks better of it, the face checked against a memory, the voice that goes somewhere older. Those are planted well and I would not touch them. The lecture around them leans on stock similes (captured sunlight, a drawn bow, a heartbeat caught in crystal, a sun under water) and the shard history is told twice, once as lore and once as a teaching beat. The Gilded Chamber scene is the sharpest sparring in the book so far. Merrick arrives as a real antagonist, a man who writes ledgers instead of receipts, and Dain gets to be funny and dangerous in the same breath. Merrick's servant Albin and the warm goblet do more to build him than any description. What weakens it is housekeeping. Aldrek's robes change colour between paragraphs, it is mid-morning and Dain says "Good evening", Dain sips whiskey in a hall of spiced wine, the bar becomes a table, and the debrief with Aldrek tells us they spoke in fragments and then lets them speak in full paragraphs. There is also a cluster of blade and cut images (drawn blade, guillotine, well-oiled dagger, edge of a blade, broken blade) that starts to dull itself. Two new names land with no setup, the Bastion and the Iron Halls, and Ironrun. The Zac find in paragraph 77 is logged as Option A.
