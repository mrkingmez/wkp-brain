#FIRST IMPRESSION
The Epilogue is two scenes and a hook, and both scenes work. The mirror chamber is short and well pitched. Zhaelor's report is clean, the mirror's reply is calm and cruel, and "Do not gnaw on scraps" and "the softening was the threat" give the voice a chill without a single raised word. His last line ("we shall see whose chains hold whom") is a strong final turn. The warehouse is the stronger scene. Luca's entrance, the price he names ("he cannot be yours"), Nyssa's memories of Dain, the flicker of Riven, and her dry-eyed "I want it all" make a real turn for her, and the lock of hair surviving the change is the right small detail. The weaknesses are small. "Gave nothing back" is used for Zhaelor's eyes, the mirror, and (in Chapter 52) the river. The burned-clothes beat is told twice. "The one he wore" is a shard he is holding. Luca's "I am finally trusted and ready to hold it" is unclear. Three simile clauses of the "a man who" kind sit in the warehouse scene. The Brotherhood line sits next to Chapter 53's "disbanded" line. Zac's one stored note (the tailor's crooked grin) is a Keep and is left alone. Nothing in this chapter is explained that the Book 1 reader should hold, and I explain nothing.
