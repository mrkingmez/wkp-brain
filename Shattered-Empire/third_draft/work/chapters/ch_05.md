#FIRST IMPRESSION
A strong political scene wearing a messy coat. The negotiation in the council chamber works. Harmon asks for pardons and a title that dies with the mission, Edric weighs it, and "As a friend" is a quiet, earned moment. Drake "understood nothing except that no one had said no yet" is the best line in the chapter. The sensory opening in the Grand Hall is good. Where it stumbles is on the page. There is a leftover fragment ("s?”") in paragraph 28, an orphaned closing quote at paragraph 69, an invented form of address ("Your Sire"), a sentence with no verb at paragraph 26, and Zac's four simile notes to fold in. The chapter also asks the reader to remember a High Seer who is never seen again. The scene itself needs very little. The page needs cleaning.
