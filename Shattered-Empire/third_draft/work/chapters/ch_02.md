#TITLE Chapter 2
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the chapter where the book gets its engine, and it mostly delivers. The tavern is alive, Harmon and Adin have real history in their banter, and "Now I mean to use it" is the line the whole book hangs on. The ending, with Adin setting the cup down undrunk, is the best beat in the chapter. The trouble is that the page is not clean. A leftover editing instruction sits inside paragraph 10, a locked spelling is wrong, one quote is broken and another has lost its opener, and the plan is explained three times in a row. Adin also shifts between a formal ancient voice and a modern tavern joker, so the man the book needs the reader to feel is old does not read as old until the very end. The strongest fix is to cut the repeats and let the two old men sit in silence a little more.
