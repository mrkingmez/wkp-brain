#TITLE Chapter 30
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the quiet chapter of the Pass thread, and it is the one that has to make the Luca and Lysana romance land before Chapter 40 takes her. The tunnel descent is well staged (the hidden gap, the chimney-effect joke, the false-collapse ceiling, the fork, the rotten scaffold, the hatch that buckles) and the two leads finally sound like two people instead of a plan and a blade. Luca's contract story and Lysana's camp story are the heart of it, and the line "People don't talk to weapons" is the best thing in the chapter. The weak spots are the usual ones. The tunnel is described as a body (throat, jaw) three times, the emotion is told after it has already been shown, the closing exchange stacks fragments, and a Gabriel for Ariel slip sits in the middle of the best speech. The chapel ending sets the stakes cleanly and hands the next chapter its job.
