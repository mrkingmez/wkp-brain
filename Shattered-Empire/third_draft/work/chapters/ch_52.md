#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the dock chapter, and it is built in three movements that each land. The opening ride is easy and warm. Dain's "Road tax" ambush, Taerel's "I thought every tavern was a fortress" and the poultice scene let the reader breathe after Chapter 51, and the Lysana account is carried well by what Aymir does not do ("Not looking at it directly"). The waiting-on-the-pier stretch plants the calm that the ambush needs. The ambush itself opens with a clean snap, "A snap cut the air, crossbow string," and the countdown game is a strong, cruel device. Riven stepping into the bolt is the best beat in the chapter, and "But the current gave nothing back. Nothing but silence." is the right size. Then the torture runs long. From the moment Riven goes into the river to the arrival of the shooters, the same threat is said at least four times ("Every scream. Every drop of blood" is almost word for word in two speeches), and Rouric's "work of art" monologue arrives with a different register and a pronoun ("him") that has no clear owner. The weak points are craft slips. Nyssa is described as held before anyone takes hold of her. The blade "dragging along the bolt" puts the bolt in the wrong wound. The sun is sinking at the end of a scene that has been lit by torches and fog. "He filed it" appears three times. Zac's two Keeps are left as written. His note about trimming Rouric's torture has no stated scope, so it is logged as a question and nothing is cut. The locked spelling is fixed (Forgehallow, twice).
