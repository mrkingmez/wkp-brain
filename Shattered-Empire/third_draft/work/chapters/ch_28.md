#TITLE Chapter 28
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is a talking chapter, and it is one of the strongest in the book because every conversation moves someone. Harmon is back and the first thing he does is thank Garr and Dain, then take Garr aside and make him promise not to tell Weslin about the ring. That is the cost of command in two paragraphs. The debriefs after it are quick and each one gives a different freedom. Torrek wants to disappear, Haldrin wants his work, Riven gets a pardon and tells Harmon he joined for her, and the reveal that Calen is Reau, the son of Harmon's old friend Arthur, with a binding collar and a stolen sword hanging in Merrick's house, is the best surprise since the prison. Harmon's "Good lad" to Weslin and the morning pack-up with Aurelia's "little flame" close it softly. What holds it back is that the chapter repeats itself more than it needs to. Harmon tells the camp to rest and then to wait for the new blood three times. The camp is shown breaking up in four separate passages. Harmon says twice that he waited to see if Riven would run. Several lines are garbled, Torn's name is still Tarn, the speech marks wander between straight and curly in about seventy places, and a few continuity threads need Zac, including the armor Harmon straps on a morning after it vanished, the collar nobody mentioned in Chapter 23, and whether Reau's father is dead.
