#TITLE Chapter 29
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the cleanest action chapter since the prison, and it belongs to Taerel. A lone rider on a frozen ridge, a bad ankle, a rock and a length of twine for a trap, seven bandits and a rifle that coughs once into the wind. It reads like a tight short story inside the larger book, with a clear want (supplies for the people behind the walls), a clear plan, a clean execution, a price paid, and a payoff that is a hug and a pair of lines. "Letting bone and tendon settle into a lie that would hold for a few more minutes" is the best sentence in the chapter, and "I got what I could" followed by Aymir's cracked "I know" is the right size of reunion. The weak spots are small. The motive is stated four times in the first half. The weather changes between paragraphs. The civilians are behind the fortress walls here and in the ruined town in Chapter 25. A dangling "It" opens the crossbow paragraph, the injury and the loot are told a little twice, and the ending reaches for a stock close. There are only a few mechanical slips.
