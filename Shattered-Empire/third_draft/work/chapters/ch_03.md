#TITLE Chapter 3
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This chapter works hardest when Adin is hiding something. The ring on the shelf, the shove when Luca jokes about taking it, and "I withheld, it is different" make the best scenes in the chapter, and the painting and the returning daggers give the cellar real texture. It sags in two places. The recruiting list is a run of names that never come back (I searched all 55 chapter files, and Gusterson, Renic, Senna, Orist, Fell, Cael and Pelkin appear nowhere else), and the banter before the ring scene runs long and slides into modern phrasing. Adin also keeps changing voice, formal and ancient in the first speech, a modern shop owner by paragraph 25. A reader who comes straight from Chapter 2 gets another two men in a room talking, so the shape repeats. The cure is to trim the roster, give Adin his old voice back, and let the ring scene carry the chapter.
