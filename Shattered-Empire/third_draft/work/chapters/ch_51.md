#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the regroup chapter, and it does three jobs well. It lets the team breathe by the fire, it pays off the collar and the medallion from Chapters 28, 42 and 44, and it splits the group again for the last act. The camp section is the best stretch. Dain and Nyssa's banter ("Who would I be if I didn't?") earns its warmth, Weslin calming the horses with "No one had mentioned it" is a good quiet plant, and the line "It was the kind of rest that comes between things rather than after them" is one of the best in the back half. The collar scene is the real set piece. The medallion on the seam, the shared vibration, the "welcome turned to invasion" turn and Reau's retching make the cost of freed magic physical, and Harmon's reaction, with the line about the student worth keeping, gives it feeling. The lizardmen fight is quick and clean, and the ending on "They didn't slow down to count" works. The weak points are small. "The boy" in Harmon's thought reads as the governor's son when it means Reau. Harmon's orders to Dain give a deadline and then take it back ("You have two days" and "I don't care how long it takes you"). The ambush paragraph says "That was when they saw them" three paragraphs before they do. There are two time-of-day signals that do not match Chapter 48. The collar scene stacks six similes in eight paragraphs. Zac's one stored cut (Ash and the tree line) is applied as written, and the Aurelia placement note is logged as a question. The locked spelling is fixed.
