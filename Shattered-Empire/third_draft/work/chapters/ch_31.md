#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the regroup chapter, and it does its job well. The team is whole again, the cavern becomes a base, Taerel's wound and Aymir's report give the poison plan a believable root, and the banter (the jerky older than his humor, the tyrants, the "shadow") finally lets four people sound like a squad. Aymir's report of the orcs sitting down to eat in formation is the best beat, because it is a thing seen and not a thing explained, and the poison idea grows out of it in the right order. The trouble is the numbers. Taerel's account of how he got hurt does not match Chapter 29, the headcount of the chapel has drifted again, and the narration slips into other heads twice. The tunnel and cave are given body images again (wound, throat, veins), and a few lines tell the feeling after the scene has shown it. The chapter closes on the plan and on the right line for Aymir.
