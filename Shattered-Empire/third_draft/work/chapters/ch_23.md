#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the payoff chapter for the prison arc and it plays like one. Dain's tackle by Aldrek is a good opening beat, and "Improvisation. Your tackle was appreciated. Brought the role together" is the best Dain line so far. The rescue itself is brisk. Weslin disarms two wards in a row and grows into it, the reunion with Riven carries a charge that Dain notices and Weslin wisely looks away from, and Torrek and Haldrin get just enough on the page to be remembered. The courtyard exit works because the fire gives the cover and Dain sells the voice. Neavin popping out of the fountain with a score for Ash's explosion is the right release before the last scene. Then Harmon, three Forged and the collapse, which is the real hook of the chapter. The kills are fast and cinematic, and the armor vanishing on the word "horse" is a strong image. What holds it back is small. Dain's account of the drainage tunnel contradicts what the team just walked through, Riven is a "brother" in the dialogue and something more in the embrace, the new prisoners are called "the new guy" for two men, and the collar on Torrek is never dealt with. Two stock similes also sit in an otherwise clean action scene.
