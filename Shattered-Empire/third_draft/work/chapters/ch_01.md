#FIRST IMPRESSION
A strong, warm opening that earns its pace. The slapstick fall into the armor gives Weslin a face before the plot starts, Harmon's dry humor shows up on the first page, and the chapter turns hard from comedy to dread when the painting hits Harmon. The scene with Drake is well built, and the double news (Stormhaven, then the pass) lifts the stakes cleanly. The chapter ends on a good Harmon line. What drags it down is housekeeping. A whole sentence is pasted in twice, the key to the hidden panel is drawn twice, a tense slips mid-speech, and the narration drifts between Weslin, Harmon, the King and Drake without a clean rule. The simile habit shows up in the stragglers. The thing to fix first is Terance's allegiance, which a first reader cannot follow.
