#TITLE Chapter 27
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the book's first long exhale, and it is written like one. After the prison, the ride and the rescue, the camp is quiet and everyone is carrying something. Garr turning the messages face down on the table is the best single gesture in the chapter, because it shows the cost of command without a word of narration. Weslin's lesson with Aurelia is tender and well paced, with the candle failing once and then holding, and "Safe" as his one-word answer. Dain and Garr in the middle section give both men a real wound, and Dain's "Not everything has to stay broken" to Nyssa is the right size of hope. The stew scene is warm and funny, Calen's thank-you lands, and Haldrin's tale of the Veylnar and the Pearl gives the reader the lore as a story the fire has to hold. Then Harmon walks out of the dark and corrects the tale. That is a great chapter ending. What holds it back is repetition and a handful of continuity slips. Garr's weight of command is told six times. Torrek is "pale and wiry" here and massive in Chapter 23. Dain quotes lines Garr never said. The chapter changes place three times with no scene break, and the plural "gods" sits in a chapter the packet says already says God the Creator.
