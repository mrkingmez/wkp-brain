#TITLE Chapter 37
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the cavern-camp chapter, and after two crowded Stormhaven chapters it breathes. It is also the first chapter where the Pass team gets to be funny, and it earns it. "Chivalry. Dead and buried." and "Hope he dances better than he flirts" land because the people saying them are tired and fond of each other. The structure is clean. A supply cart arrives, Taerel reads the orc camp as staged, Lysana and Aymir follow a cart into a tunnel that should not exist, they find a Joblin hub and eight unmarked riders, and Luca lays out a poisoning plan at dusk. Taerel's wish for "a back door through the mountains" pays off a few pages later, which is how a planted line should work. The problems are small and mostly about bookkeeping. Aymir is in the cavern at midday and on the trail at the same hour. The four vials are a glob, a powder, a vial and a vial. Brehn is named before he is introduced. The prose reaches for blood, embers, mouths and breathing in places where the scene is already working. The ending line is a slogan.
