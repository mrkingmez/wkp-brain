#TITLE Chapter 41
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is Riven and Nyssa's chapter, and it is a gift. The first half is a long walk and talk on a mountain ledge that pays off a decade of history. Riven finally says "Why is it never me?", Nyssa says "Because I can't lose you", and he gives her his blessing for Dain in a speech that costs him. "I think he'd be good for you. If you let him" is the line that makes the scene. The second half is a caper, and it is a good one. The empty statue, the hidden bolt-hole, the "stashy-stash", the rappel race, the half-plate mercs, the shaft Nyssa remembers from a drunk night and the vent that is "always a tight fit" all show two people who have done this for years. The banter ("Define cheating", "Loser buys the next round", "Flexible truth") is the best two-hander in the book. The problem is that Riven and Nyssa have the same conversation three times, on the ledge, at the statue and in the bolt-hole, and Nyssa says "I might stay", "give him a chance" and "I want you in it" in nearly the same words each time. She even jokes about the "hard sell" herself, which helps, but the middle talk is the weakest of the three. The speeches also sometimes explain the feelings the scene has already shown. A handful of details clash with Chapter 36, mainly the keys, the vault and the statue. The dialogue is typed with straight marks throughout, which needs a global fix. Zac's one Keep, the "words ran out" line, stays.
