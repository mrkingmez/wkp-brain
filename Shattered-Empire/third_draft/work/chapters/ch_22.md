#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the breach and it moves. Aldrek sits in the interrogation room being a quiet heretic, the first boom shakes the dust, and from there the chapter is a descent. Four floors, each with its own texture (the keys and the Warden's office, the stacked wards, the cell menagerie on the third level, the vault on the fourth), a fight that Ash walks into on purpose, and then a reveal. Ash is the star. "That one was mine", "Third surprise. I might've set fire to your artillery shed" and "I need the practice" give him the best run of dialogue in the book. The cell menagerie (the Joblin who waves, the man who needs no air, the Orth) is the strongest world texture in the chapter and it earns its space. Weslin gets his first real mistake, the missed ward, and the link to Aurelia is severed, which raises the stakes for the rest of the book. The reveal of Dain in a guard's uniform pays off Nyssa's wish from Chapter 21. What holds it back is a handful of tangles. The interrogation opens mid-answer, with Aldrek replying to a question the officer has not yet asked. Paragraph 23 is garbled. Weslin is described as tall and adult. Weslin explains his training to Ash at a length no scared fourteen-year-old would manage. The chapter has five interrupted lines marked with a dash, which the house rule does not allow. Ash says in Chapter 21 that Dain is outside the walls, and Dain turns up inside the prison.
