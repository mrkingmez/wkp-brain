#TITLE Chapter 35
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the long, braided chapter of the Stormhaven day, and it has the book's best images so far, set against its most tangled bookkeeping. Weslin's push through the shard is the strongest scene in it, with a silence that is gone and not hushed, a surge, a breath and a masked figure that turns. The tower where five citizens walk a circle, the tea garden with the six chairs, "It's in the water", and the lemoncake exit are all sharp and in tone. Dain's speech about his sister's name gives him a reason to be the way he is. The war-beast cradle at the end earns its "declaration" through the details of fuse coils and blood channels. The trouble is repetition and offstage recaps. The trio meets the same scrubbing, scratching, forgetting worshipper in one chapter that Chapter 34 gave them already, and then a third sanctum with a boy. Dain and Nyssa visit a second boutique that the recap treats as the first. The recap lists events that are not on the page. The count of chairs, the capital, the name Albrecht, and the steel Garr reaches for and does not have all need a pass. The prose leans on "held breath" three times and on jaws and lungs for buildings. Underneath all that, the chapter has a strong spine and the ending is clean.
