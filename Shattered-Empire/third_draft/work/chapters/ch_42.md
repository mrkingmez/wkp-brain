#TITLE Chapter 42
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This chapter is built like a heist and it works. The dawn huddle sets the stakes, the split into three teams is clear, and the Merrick manor sequence is the most fun Reau and Riven have had together. The Keryn meeting is the best scene in it. "Define 'live'", "carrying more silence than armor" and "the blade that makes him bleed" give her a voice in four minutes, and the cellar, the study, the false vault and the trapped case move fast. The market ending is the real prize. A city of people turning in silence to face one cart, with only the snow still moving, is a great closing image and the Havern Ball banter just before it makes the drop harder. The chapter's problems are small and mostly about the plan. The team is told twice to meet at Blackcap Alley and then Weslin announces a different order. The hunt for a key gives way to a case with no lock. The scene leaves at night and arrives at midday. And the place names around Merrick (Ember Hill, Stormhaven, Forgehallow) do not agree with the ledger. The prose carries a few stock images in the tense stretches, mostly the clock-mechanism woman, "something older than that" and the weather chill. Zac's Keep on Riven stopping after four paces stays exactly as written.
