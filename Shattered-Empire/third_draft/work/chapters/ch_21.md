#TITLE Chapter 21
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the book's heist chapter, and it has the right pulse. A cramped room, a plan nobody is sure of, and then three tight scenes in the dark (Nyssa and Weslin in the alley, Ash alone with his flasks, Weslin at the ward). Nyssa and Weslin are the warmest pair in it ("Repetition keeps the screaming quiet", "Nope. Just true."). Ash's solo is the best character work, since he starts with a plan, hears a gut itch, climbs through an unmarked hatch and finds a room of explosives with "aren't you just asking to be set on fire?". Weslin finally gets to use what Aurelia taught him, and the quiet "Thanks" to whoever is listening pays it off. The chapter is also where the Ch19 rift with Aurelia is answered, since she is the one who worries aloud about putting him in danger. What weakens it is the setup room and a few tangled passages. The headcount (seven figures, nine named), the dismissed Dain who is then addressed, the link that goes to "each of them" and then only to Weslin, and Nyssa's speech with narration inside the quote marks are all fixable in a line each. The last of the three is the worst, since two sentences of Weslin's inner life are spoken by Nyssa. Paragraph 92 is a garble. The vocabulary slips toward modern (tactical armor, utility junction, concrete). The Zac find in paragraph 78 is logged as Option A.
