#TITLE Chapter 24
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the book's first long look at the court, and it is a good change of air after the prison. The Accord feast has real texture (Vaugre and the pearls, the peacocks and the lion, deals dancing as much as feet), Edric is warm and public and sure of himself, and the quiet cut to Drake in the corridor is the best beat in the chapter. The threat with the velvet pouch and the lock of hair is cold and specific, and Drake taking a breath to put the mask back on is exactly the right restraint. Merrick's rage scene is a strong swing, a man who built himself out of the gutter watching it slip, with the bounty list and the half-remembered Emberlight lord as the sting. The Queen's bedchamber scene gives a clean plant (the guards barely checked the carts) and the watcher on the roof closes the chapter with the right chill. What holds it back is a heavier hand of stock lines (the world on his shoulders, wine like a river, the veins bulging), an unclear alliance in the corridor (who does Cotter serve, and what is Drake hiding about Merrick), a report from Jerric that contradicts itself, and a batch of small mechanical slips, including six uses of the wrong name for Cotter.
