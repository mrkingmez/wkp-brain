#TITLE Chapter 12
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the first chapter in a while where something is actually at stake on the page, and the scene earns it. Dain walking into the Magistrate's Tower in a borrowed coat is funny and tense at once, and Warden Caldra Hesk is a real opponent (her line "Try not to trip over your pedigree on the way out" is the best line of the chapter, and her slip about the "half-dead mercenary" is a good reversal that Dain catches). The tether is used the way it should be (Harmon, Nyssa and Neavin in Dain's head, Dain's two-word "Old man" that he did not plan to say). Then the chase out of the tower splits into three tracks and ends in a Merrick plant and an informant who tells them what the reader half knew. The chapter is also the messiest so far on the page. Paragraph 2 is garbled (a stray quote, a half-sentence of leaked staging and a doubled line). The informant repeats his reason three times in four speeches. The Warden scene piles up five similes in five paragraphs. A locked spelling is wrong (Forgehollow). A modern word slips in ("notification"). Two continuity items matter (the prison is "above" in one paragraph and "below" in another, and Merrick's sigil does not match the ledger). The Zac note and the Zac "keep" are both logged.
