#TITLE Chapter 15
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This chapter is a debrief in an inn war room, and it works as one. The opener is the best line in it ("Luca's found an orc raider camp."), and the book then does what it does well, which is let tired people be funny at each other before the news gets worse (Neavin's "I learned birds are smarter than half this team," Nyssa's "It was one spoon," Dain's boot in the alley). Nyssa's Merrick reveal is the real payload, and it lands, mostly because she says it with her jaw tight and her eyes on the fire instead of on Harmon. The closing scene between Harmon and Weslin is quiet, earned and the strongest writing in the chapter, and the last line about the boy who believes in dangerous men is a keeper. What holds it back is stacking. Three reports (Luca's camp, Dain's Tower, Aldrek's tail) each get a full measure, then Nyssa retells part of what the room already knows, and a few lines near the middle are garbled badly enough to read as typing slips (paragraphs 93, 95, 105 and 163). The orc count wobbles between four, five and three. Aldrek, who is silent and heavy everywhere else, delivers two paragraphs of portrait. One stray line (paragraph 74) hands the reader the next scene's setup in a voice that is not Aldrek's. The Zac finds (two simile trims) and the Merrick sigil fix are logged as Option A. Six of the plants in here (the box, "three days," three off-book prisoners, "older than the crown," the Shadow Walker, the candle drill) are the kind a reader will thank you for later, so I left them alone and logged them.
