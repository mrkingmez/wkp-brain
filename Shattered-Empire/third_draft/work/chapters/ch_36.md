#TITLE Chapter 36
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the stable-loft night, the regroup after the Stormhaven day, and it holds the book's cleanest statement of its own mystery. Harmon's "Stormhaven didn't fall. It was overwritten." is the best line in it, and Aldrek's "built over a prison" and Reau's "it won't defend, it'll lead" are close behind. The Dain and Riven flare-up has real heat and costs both men something. Weslin's guilt after the shard push is handled gently, with Reau as the anchor and Aurelia unsure how to be one. The note on the doorframe and the mirror call with Edric and Drake turn the night into a clock, which is what the chapter needs. The weakness is repetition. Harmon asks his three questions, the team answers them, and then Harmon asks them again as a "second pass" and the team answers them again, with the vision, the old woman, the tea, the loops and the unseen governor all restated. Several details also collide with other chapters, among them the governor, the war-beast chamber, the masked figure that "saw" Weslin and then did not, and the king's surname. The prose leans on bones, shadows and "not X, but Y" turns when the dialogue is already doing the work.
