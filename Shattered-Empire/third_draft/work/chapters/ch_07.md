#TITLE Chapter 7
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the recruitment chapter, and it is the best idea in the book so far wearing a few rough clothes. The room full of mercenaries bickering, Harmon's "Quiet!" and the white sword through the floor, Weslin pressing his thumb on the yellow stone while a dozen moods press back, Aldrek's "a breaker of men," the tearful woman with the two silver coins, and the closing "Motley crew" are all the real thing. Harmon turning a room by force and then paying the people he sends home is who he is in one chapter. The trouble is the page. Two versions of Harmon's opening speech sit back to back, a pair of paragraphs are garbled past reading (the Neavin exchange and Aldrek's entrance), names are used before anyone says them, a late woman and a tearful woman may or may not be the same person, and the cast arrives faster than the chapter can pay it off. Harmon also drifts into casual speech ("Ok, Next," "Alright") that does not sound like the man who said "That is mine to carry, not yours." Almost every fix is small. The chapter is worth the cleanup.
