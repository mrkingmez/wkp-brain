#TITLE Chapter 4
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the best chapter so far. The market opens with real sensory weight (the cobblestones swept twice is a perfect line), the bakery doorway scene lands, and Mjakazi's "It means I am proud of you" is the emotional high point of the book to this point. The weak spots are that the middle sags into three paragraphs of Harmon hesitating outside the door, Harmon makes the same argument three times in a row, Weslin's father is described with stock images (white orbs, a sword that cuts men like paper), and a handful of tense slips and a misspelled name need cleaning. Zac's three Packet insertions belong here, and one of them (Option 2 of Insertion A) contradicts a line two thirds of the way down. The scene works. It wants trimming, not rewriting.
