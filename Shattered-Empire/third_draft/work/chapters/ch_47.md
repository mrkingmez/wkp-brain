#TITLE Chapter 47
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is the climb chapter, and it is the strongest piece of sustained dread in the book. The team goes up through the tower floor by floor, and each floor is wrong in a new way. The abandoned throne room is kept like a tomb someone still visits, the servant quarters hold a pair of boots "placed", not tossed, the crates are too big to have come up any stair, the barracks floor is nothing but empty armor with a boot "still tied" in a double knot, and the gear is warm. Aldrek's "The tower. It hurts." and "Because some paths are meant to be walked only once" are two of the best lines he has had. Ash's jokes ("Big chair. Bet the view was great.") keep the dread from going flat. Nyssa's recognition of the governor's sister-in-law is a clean hook, and the trap that follows is a good one. Her hesitation over the mimic child ("Her hands disagreed by about a quarter-second") is the best character beat in the fight. The flesh golem is a fair brawl and the end, with something non-human hitting the door, is a good cliffhanger. The weak points are small. The geography of the thirty-second floor is hard to follow, since the reader meets the sister-in-law twice in two places. The execution is "within the hour" when the bells say two hours, the narration says "an hour ago" for something minutes old, and a few new names (Stormhaven Armistice, Hollow Spine) are not in my notes. The prose has a run of not-X turns, and Aldrek's lecture on the tower sounds like a guidebook. The one stored Zac cut (Aldrek and the nail) is applied as written, and "Tarnished" stays. An unsourced note about the mimic family needs Zac's confirmation, and I add nothing for it.
