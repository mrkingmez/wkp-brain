#TITLE Chapter 14
#FIRST IMPRESSION
This is a short tracking chapter that does one job well, which is to walk four people into the dark and withhold what they find. The brothers' banter is the best of it (Aymir's "Lightbearer," Taerel's "I'm the one who kept us from falling through that frozen creek," Lysana's "The worgs wouldn't even bother eating you, too much gristle"). Lysana carries the middle, reading the tracks and saying a line that matters, "Someone has let them off their chain." Luca's brief interior, about whether the trust trailing behind him was earned or simply a default, is the most honest thing in the chapter. Two things hold it back. First, the chapter contradicts the one before it on when and where things happened (a tavern "last night" and a storm "the day before," when the party slept in a hollow and heard a scream a few hours ago). Second, the worg explanation runs through four speeches that repeat each other, and then the chapter stalls on three withholding beats ("She stared. Mouth slightly agape. What she saw ... turned her blood to ice.") before the reveal it refuses to give. The spelling Aymer shows up twenty times again. The Zac keep (Taerel gesturing vaguely) is logged.
