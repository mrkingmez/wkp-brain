## 3.2 Frontier and pass (SG2)
- The Pass = formally the Northern Garrison. Damaged fort, adjacent town about 70 percent destroyed, old chapel with intact roof. Five-tunnel cavern/undercroft beneath the fort (storage and emergency exit, forgotten). Tunnel mouth "three hours west" of the pass (SG7 Where Book 2 Starts).

- Forgotten Lands = Forgotten Plains = Forgotten Planes = Abandoned Plains, all one place north of the pass beyond the mountains (ruled by Zac). Source of the beast army.
- Riverrun's End = gray-stone port town where the Andralis spills out of Blackfrost Lake. Neutral ground by decree, buffer between the three kingdoms, merchant docks, Aldorian garrison. Barrowmead = a stop on the pass team's route.
## 3.3 Forgehallow (SG2)

Stonewalk Promenade (Aldrek arrested here).

Capital of Aldoria, fortress-city carved into the Blackspine, ringed in silver wards, layered outer walls "wide enough to ride five horses across". Stormhaven is "Aldoria's capital"; Riven's home city. Silver wards, violet dome over the city center afterward (SG7).

# 5. MAGIC RULES

## 5.1 Cosmology (SG3 s1; ASM Basics, Bonded)
- Four planes plus the Fae veil = Material, Ethereal/Aetherial (source of magic, sealed), Heavenly (the Creator's realm, Watchers and angels), Infernal (demons; left unbarred), the Fae (in-between veil). "Heavenly Plane" is confirmed as the base term; a layered naming (mortal name vs angel name) is under consideration. (SG3 Still-open) ANX uses "hereafter" once for the same place. 
- The Nine/The Eight = see C-01. Rings made by God to fight them (SG3 s7). First Magic War = champions plus elves seal them; ASM says "defeating The Eight and sealing them within the Ethereal Plane with the help of the elves".
- Pearl of Magic = a single crystalline conduit built by the Adept Knights (Seven, led by Lucent) as a permanent bridge to the Ethereal. Built using lost Elven tomes and experimental artifacts "created by the Eight themselves" (ASM). Destroyed in the Second Magic War (LOCKED). Its shattering unleashed wild magic, distorted geography, created the Breaches. Who shattered it = see C-03.
- Breaches = small rifts, most no larger than a doorway, leak Ethereal energy, let small and medium spirits through, only way shards recharge, none closeable. Minor (ruins, battlefields) vs Major (mountains, volcanic, storm country). (SG3 s1, ASM)
## 5.2 Shards and Adepts (SG3 s1-3, ASM)
- Shards = hardened leyline power, "the power of life itself, the leftover power of God's creation". New shards form because the sealing slowed the trickle; source moves and cannot be mapped. Deeper color = more power; color fades with use; spent shard crumbles to dust.
- Adepts have an innate Aether Thread. Non-Adepts cannot channel but may use shard-infused tools.
- Colors (SG3) = White healing (cannot raise the dead), Red fire, Green earth, Blue water, Indigo air, Yellow mental/psionics, Orange transmutation, Purple arcane (invisibility, teleportation, summoning; improper use tears reality), Black/Onyx shadow. ASM also mentions "Life-affinity stones" (rare, powerful) not listed in SG. See C-31.
- Passive vs invocation use. Off-color use = reduced effect, faster depletion (Ash with a looted purple shard, Ch45).
- Resonance Test = touch a stone, watch for glow. Bright = strong; weak or flickering = lesser; none = not an Adept. Onyx tested separately in a darkened chamber (ASM).
- Recharge = only at a Breach, rare astral surges (eclipses, meteor showers, alignments), or slowly by immersion in the matching element. Overcharging = violent backlash. (SG3, ASM)
- Overuse ladder = misalignment, burnout, temporary severance, depletion to dust. Vampiric State and forbidden Blood Magic (pallor, fangs, thirst), taught only in secret. (SG3 s3, ASM)
- All technology in the world is shard powered, firearms included (ruled by Zac). The Altisflumen brothers' rifles are "special in a way they do not know yet". (SG3 s12, SG1)
## 5.3 Onyx and shadow (SG3 s4, ASM)
- Onyx shards are not mined, they were created and blessed by Ariel. Onyx does NOT lose energy but CAN lose integrity (crack and shatter if pushed too hard). ASM heading says "unlimited, never lose power".
- Ariel's order (SG3 s4) = first seed stone gave Luca his power; as Ariel began turning to stone he made a second seed stone so Luca could keep generating onyx shards; then he made the Judgement Stone, a separate artifact whose holder chooses the contracts Luca executes. Adin held it for a long span. When Luca proved himself (Ch40), it was granted, appearing in his pocket (mechanism = Book 2 topic, off screen, by magic, LOCKED). Once he held it Luca could create his own seed stone, the one used to remake Nyssa in the Epilogue. Ch20 says "Adin has the Stone now. He does not know the cost. Or maybe he does." and a "thirty-judgment limit" (SG-Tables Ch20).
- Luca's amulet shard broke in Ch40, it did not run dry, it cracked to black dust; broke and released the creatures of the shade of the Shadow Realm which destroyed the enemy (ruled by Zac). Luca "pulled everything from his shard" after Lysana died.
- Powers = shadow movement, shadow cloak, shadow constructs, shadow strike, Shadow Realm access, pockets of absolute darkness. Dangers = corruption, addiction, loss in the Shadow Realm. ASM adds weakness to bright light.
- Ariel = fallen Watcher, "Shadow Watcher", Shadow Lord, angel of judgment, slowly turning to stone, stayed first for love and second for revenge which he calls justice. Plea reaches Ariel, he grants "Contract granted" (Nyssa Ch52). 
## 5.4 Scribing and runes (SG3 s5, ASM)
- Runes are written like a language: central focus glyph, modifier runes, environmental runes, trigger runes. Anchored to a surface and powered by an embedded shard unless a power-holding rune sustains them. Active vs passive. ASM rune names (Aegis, Ignis, Umbra, Fortis, Fluxus, Vitae; Stasis, Mobilo, Extendere, Focus, Tempus; Silvanus, Ferrus, Terran, Aeris, Aquos; Contactus, Proximus, Vocis, Eternus; advanced Ruin of Eternal Hold, Animae Vincula, Tenebris Umbra, Murmur).
- Only the Zhar'ai can truly scribe (the White Thread). Outsiders can copy shapes but intent and flow fail. Special ruins (ruled by Zac) = a storage ruin plus a scribed item stores a shard's power. ("Zhar'ai" is canonical; ASM uses "Zorakai", retired. See C-18.)
- Taerel scripts enchantments, built the communication bracelets, makes bracers (Ch37), Blackfire orbs (Ch40). Taerel's mask hides white hair and bleached eyes (SG-Tables Ch20). Taerel reveals he is Zhar'ai in Ch25 (SG-Tables).
## 5.5 Ringbearers (SG3 s7, ASM Ringbearers)
- Nine ancient rings, made by God, tethered to the Ethereal. They choose their bearer; never found, sought or crafted by mortals. A fallen bearer's ring returns to the Ethereal. Two bearers at the story's start (Harmon, Adin) plus seven unclaimed (ruled by Zac) in SG. ASM says three bearers, six rings dormant. See C-04. Arthur (Reau's father) was also a Ringbearer (SG3 s7).
- Powers = all schools of magic from the Ethereal with no shard, single signature weapon summoned at first bonding (unbreakable, bearer not invincible), slowed aging, regenerative healing while holding the ring, subtly enhanced strength/speed/reflexes, senses Breaches.
- Veil = when the weapon is summoned a seamless shimmering energy veil covers the face (deep silver or dark violet), hides features from everyone including allies, vanishes when the weapon is dismissed. Ch7 shows "a white sword drives through the stone as his face becomes a veil of energy" (SG-Tables Ch7). Ch23 "white-edged sword".
- Costs = ring dependency (separated from the ring, loses powers, healing, long life, ages normally, old wounds become permanent), overdraw, emotional detachment, moral peril, vulnerability to anti-magic and runes, memories of past bearers bleeding in. 
- Harmon's ring specifics = ring on a chain (Ch1 plant), left hand (Ch8) vs right hand (Ch17), "pulsed cold on its own in Ch48", last pulsed cold unasked thirty years ago, Adin's ring is its twin. Hand = C-15.
- LOCKED on the ring cost, see C-02.
## 5.6 Bound Sentinels (SG3 s8, ASM)
- Cursed soldiers (rune-scribing, blood magic, shard infusion), all leashed to the Master of Chains through the Chainheart Sigil. Each bonded to a runed sword (life anchor). Armor manifests when sword drawn, fueled by blood spilled in combat, not shards. No natural aging or death while the sword is intact. Bloodlust. Cannot use any other weapon. Destroy the sword and the Sentinel dies; destroy the sigil and every bound Sentinel dies.
- Mjakazi = the only one to break free, "through love and regret rather than strength, shattering the control rune in his own chest". Lives as baker, sword hidden but close. ASM adds his armor, when manifested, is cracked and worn.
## 5.7 Shardborn (SG3 s9, ASM)
- Kaelen Dray's experiment (shards infused into bone, muscle, organ; runes carved into bones; tattoos required for the process (ruled by Zac); body recharges shards slowly during rest). Almost always killed or maddened subjects.
- Reau (Damu) = the only Shardborn, sole survivor; powers = physical enhancement, kinetic barrier, adrenaline surge, regenerative pulse, night vision, magical detection, overclocking at risk of shattering a shard. Runed iron collar suppressed it and hid his identity until Ch51 (medallion-key). See C-16 and C-17.
## 5.8 The Bonded (SG3 s6, ASM)
- Bonding is a rare dangerous ritual near an active Breach, needs proximity, willing intent or vulnerability, the entity's consent (deception possible). Entity fuses into the soul-thread. The entity is something trapped in the Ethereal Plane (ruled by Zac).
- Types = friendly spirits, Ethereal Elves, physical breach-creatures, malevolent entities. Abilities = ethereal projection, shared senses, physical enhancement, elemental magic following the entity's nature. A bonded person can push magic through the entity to extend range (ruled by Zac). Risks = abomination, two minds one body, bond sickness, sight trade-off (Neavin goes blind when using Veyrith's sight).
## 5.9 Communication and tools (SG3 s12)
- Communication bracelets (silver; arcanite core, Aether-harmonic bindings, nexus rune; one-way mental projection between partnered bands; distinct from a Yellow adept's mind-tether; crafted by Taerel). Mind-tether weave limited by shard, about three hours (Aurelia). Shard light terms (glowshard, glowstone, amber-glow crystal, dull-blue crystal) not yet standardized. Star iron for high-security doors. Tetherline rail and river-runners.
