# Third Draft Editorial Report
## The Patience of the Dark (Shattered Empire, Book 1)

Prepared for Zac. Every call in this report is a suggestion. The author decides everything. No source file was edited.

## Method in five lines
1. I read every one of the 55 sections (Prologue, Chapters 1 to 53, Epilogue) line by line from clean per-chapter text, 235,177 words as counted from the chapter files.
2. Each chapter got an honest first impression, numbered items, and a wrap. Every item carries a tag, a severity, the exact flagged line, the craft cause, the impact, options, and my pick.
3. Mechanical items (spelling, spacing, quotes) were run as a script net first, then batched, so the human read could stay on craft.
4. Your stored notes (the simile cuts, Keeps, and unsourced direction notes) were applied as written. Anything unsourced was logged as a question and nothing was added or cut.
5. Every chapter file was checked by a verifier (flagged line must exist exactly once, no em dashes, no colons in options, locked spellings, option counts). All 55 pass.

## The 12 voice conflicts and how each was ruled
1. Phonetic spelling. The Performance PDF and Idioms ban it. The Annex allows it ("spice, not soup"). The Annex wins. Neavin, Ash, Garr and mountain speech may carry it.
2. Weslin's age. The PDF says thirteen, the Annex says fourteen. The locked fact is fourteen.
3. Taerel and Aymir's region. The old bible says Far West frontier. The Annex and PDF say Altisflumen. The Annex wins. Taerel is formal Victorian, Aymir is clear and educated.
4. Neavin's spelling. The old bible has "Naevin Duskwind". The Annex locks Neavin (and Duskwind is Riven's surname).
5. The Drake family. The old bible names different children. The Annex names Karethiel, Lysa and Camryn. The Annex wins.
6. Aldoria. The PDF writes "Aldornian". The Annex locks Aldoria.
7. The king's surname. The old bible has Wymark, the Annex has Wyman. Both Edric and Wymark are fine, Wyman is wrong.
8. Ash. Fast, contraction-heavy dockworker (old bible and PDF) versus the Annex's blacksmith son. They are compatible.
9. Nyssa. Old bible mischievous, PDF sly and flirtatious, Annex sarcastic over a scared girl. The Annex wins, keep the sarcasm.
10. Lysana's spelling. The Annex has Lysana, the PDF and Idioms have Lysanna. First appearance rules, variants are reported.
11. Harmon's register. The old bible has grim and clipped. The Annex and PDF have warmth with steel ribs and dry humor. They win. The alpha reader found him "too human", which remains your call.
12. Aldrek's first name. The old bible and PDF vary. The Annex locks Aldrek.

## Differences between the full manuscript and the chapter files
I worked from the per-chapter files and did not run a line-by-line diff against the full-manuscript .docx. One signal that the two may differ is in Q76 (Chapter 16), where an editor-note exchange between Taerel and Aymir could not be found in the chapter file and may live only in the full manuscript. If the full manuscript is the live copy, a diff pass would catch anything else.
