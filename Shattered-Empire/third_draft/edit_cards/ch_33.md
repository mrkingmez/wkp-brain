## How the places are counted in this chapter
Paragraph numbers count text paragraphs only, starting at 1 with the first paragraph of the chapter text. The chapter heading is not counted and blank lines are not counted. Sentence numbers count inside that paragraph, each sentence ends at a full stop, question mark or exclamation mark followed by a capital letter or an opening quote. The first six and last six words are given so you can confirm the spot. The report did not diff the chapter files against the full manuscript, so trust the words over the numbers if they disagree.

When you delete words, close up so only one space is left, and check the punctuation at the join. Quoted manuscript words are copied exactly as they sit in the chapter text, curly marks included. Option text is copied exactly as the report prints it, and the report prints straight quote marks and apostrophes in its own option lines, so set them to match your manuscript when you type or paste.
