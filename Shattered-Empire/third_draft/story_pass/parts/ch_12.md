### STORY NOTES
IMPRESSION
This is the first real action beat in Forgehallow and it works as a scene with a clear goal and a clear failure. Dain plays the noble, fails to buy Riven out, and still gets two things from the failure, the Warden's slip and the tail. The Merrick turn at the end is a strong hook because it ties the prison to Nyssa's past. The weak spot is how much of the middle is a long verbal duel that goes over the same ground. The chapter also ends on a quick handoff and no beat of decision about what the team now knows.
REVEALS in this chapter
Riven is inside and has been held three days (planted by Caldra's slip). Three prisoners are held off the books, kept moving at night, guards on a payroll (new). The man who tails Dain says a second payroll was added today (new, unexplained). The second man from the tower walks into Lord Iven Merrick's estate (F-32, F-33 plant, planted only by Nyssa's reaction). Saewyn Olgrave appears as a feared name (new, no earlier plant).
MISSING
No beat of the team asking why a guard says three prisoners when they came for one. Nobody questions why Caldra says others have come for Riven. No moment where Dain or Nyssa weighs what Merrick's link means for the plan. The man who talks is never named and never explained, so the reader cannot judge whether to trust him. How Harmon knew the name Saewyn Olgrave is off page.
NOTHING CHANGES
Scene 3 runs about thirty lines of banter before the Warden slips, and the Warden's second half repeats the point of the first. Scene 6 drains tension because the tail simply confesses and leaves.
