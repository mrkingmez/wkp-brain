### STORY NOTES
IMPRESSION
This chapter is the middle of the break and it moves well. The opening with Aldrek is the best scene, because it shows why the arrest was safe and what he is. After that the team sweeps three floors with nothing but oddities, which is a long walk, and the pressure only returns with the brute and the announced "I was told someone would come." The ending, with the guard being Dain, is a good turn but the reader has no reason to expect it. The Weslin ward failure is well placed because it keeps the boy from becoming a flawless tool.
