### STORY NOTES
IMPRESSION The best moment is Aurelia seized and speaking, the city freezing, the line that they were seen before they arrived. That is the turn of the chapter. It gives the team a reason to quit and the team goes in anyway. The rest of the chapter after that turns into three puzzles in a row, a dwarven lock, a collapsed tunnel with a second key, and a four column balance puzzle, all repeating the same lesson, work together. The theme is strong but there is too much of it and the pace stalls just when the tower should be pulling the reader in. The Dain and Nyssa warmth is good but small.
REVEALS in this chapter A voice speaks through Aurelia and says it serves a Great Lord to come. The tunnel route in the plans is dead and a hidden way lies beneath. The Bastion has Red Cloaks and an Adept watching the north face. Planted by Ch36 Korr talk and the Aurelia thread. The Great Lord to come is new wording.
MISSING A reaction from Harmon to Aurelia, he has already left. A clear reason the team keeps going after being told the trap is already sprung, beyond duty. A line for Neavin after Aurelia runs. What the watching civilians do if the team does not hurry.
NOTHING CHANGES Scenes 9 and 10, tunnel and key, change little. Scene 11 plays the same teamwork beat three times.
