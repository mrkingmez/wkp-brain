### STORY NOTES
IMPRESSION
A tight, cold opening that sets the book's central dread, an enemy that waits and has already walked inside. The discipline scene is doing real work, it shows a fort that has stopped believing, then punishes the belief. Vey is a strong first face and the witness device is a good hook. The weak edge is that the chapter names its own stakes very loudly through Dar, so the reader is told what to fear instead of left to feel it.
REVEALS in this chapter (what the reader learns, and is it planted)
- Lucent exists and sends messages, planted only by Dar's own mouth. Fine for a prologue.
- Rings are real and choose their bearer, planted by the disguised guard, which reads as a quiet tell and is well placed.
- Evens Garkins of Woford, new lord, rumored Ringbearer. Planted for later, but Garkins never shows up on screen in this chapter and the reader is left wondering.
MISSING (moments the reader needs and does not get, decisions off page)
- The fort is never named on the page here, which matters for the Fort Dreknor question in the ledger. A reader cannot tell this is the same place as the Pass fort.
- No sense of what became of Garkins. If he arrives the same day, the reader may expect him in the attack.
- No trace of how Dar got onto the wall without being known, beyond Vey saying he knew every man.
NOTHING CHANGES (scenes where nothing changes, with scene number)
- None. Both scenes move the situation.
PITCH SEEDS (cut, merge, move, add scene, reveal timing, each with an EXACT ANCHOR quote)
- Reveal timing. Dar reveals himself and also lectures the whole plan in one beat. Consider holding Lucent's name until after the first strike so the name lands as a shock instead of a speech. Anchor "We will find the rings. And Lucent bids me tell you something first."
- Cut or merge. The three versions of "you will live" can become one stronger beat, which lets the cruelty of the twisted dagger land harder. Anchor "Stay alive, old soldier. Bleed, but not too much."
- Plant. A single mention of the fort by name near the new lord news would tie this wall to the place the reader meets again in Ch50, without Zac having to settle the Dreknor question first. Anchor "Sir Evens Garkins of Woford"
CONTINUITY (timeline, who-knows-what, geography conflicts with the ledgers, with anchor quotes)
