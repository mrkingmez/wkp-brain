IMPRESSION
This is the quiet heart of the Pass track and the biggest single reveal of Luca. The first two scenes are long, warm, and mostly ordinary, hunting and cooking, and they build the room for the confession. The confession is a full monologue in one block, and it carries a lot of lore at once. The best beat is Lysana's hand stopping at his glove. The chapter does not complicate the mission at all, it is pure character and lore, which is right placement if the mission pressure from the last Pass chapter is allowed to rest.
