### STORY NOTES
IMPRESSION
A quiet team chapter after the split at Rellisford. Almost nothing is at risk, which suits a first night in a hostile city, but the chapter is long on place description and short on a story pull. The one strong beat is the round of confessions, which gives the reader the team reasons for the mission. Harmon gives a clean mission statement and then the chapter slows down again.
REVEALS in this chapter
The team reasons for coming (Weslin, Aldrek, Aurelia, Garr, Ash), all new and mostly planted only in tone. Nyssa has history with Forgehallow, planted lightly and not yet tied to Merrick or Riven.

No mention of Riven beyond the opening line, so the reader does not feel why he matters. No thread of the wider clock (the fourteen day ship, the Pass and Stormhaven teams) shows that this is a side operation.
