### STORY NOTES
IMPRESSION One long interior night for Drake. It is strong on dread and the cost of the choice, and Whisperfang finally gets a purpose, which turns the plot from murder to possession. But the chapter repeats the same feeling many times. Drake is told he is trapped in several ways in the first half and the ballroom half goes back over it. The watcher on the roof is the one new thread and it ends fast. The strongest beat is the wedding warmth between Edric and Mirelle, since it gives the reader something to lose.
