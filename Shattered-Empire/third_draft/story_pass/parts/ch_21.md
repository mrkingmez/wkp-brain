IMPRESSION
This is the start of the break itself, and it works as a set of small solo set pieces. The best material is Ash alone in the tunnels and Weslin's careful ward work, each a clear problem with a clear answer. The weakest part is the opening room, which restates a plan the reader never got in detail. Aurelia's warm worry here sits oddly against Ch19, where she told Weslin to be less of a burden. The chapter ends on a quiet success which is a good beat.
