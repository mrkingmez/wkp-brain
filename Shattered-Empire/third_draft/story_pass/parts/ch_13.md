### STORY NOTES
IMPRESSION
The chapter works as a team bonding chapter, and the bandit fight is a strong, fast reveal of what Luca is. After that it settles into a long camp sequence of food, stories and watches that is warm but does little. The scream at the end is a good hook, helped by Luca's earlier story, although the tale and the scream are lined up a little too neatly. The weakest point is the gap between the team seeing Luca kill five people and the very light emotional response.
REVEALS in this chapter
Luca can pull shadows, vanish and return, and kill with a dagger that flies back to his belt (new on the page, F-20 area, Shadow Walker status). Lysana trained under Harmon (new). A thirty soldier Aldorian garrison is forming near the Pass (new). A scream in the north (new). The reader learns Taerel and Aymir are brothers only by the "brother" in line 103.
MISSING
No beat of the team talking about the killings after the ambush, only three lines of reaction. No explanation of why Luca's shadow power is shown here when the ledger says his status stays hidden until Ch31. No mention of the Pass or why they are riding north. Lysana's knowledge of these hills is never tied to a mission reason. No moment where Taerel's mask or Zhar'ai nature is touched.
NOTHING CHANGES
Scene 3 and scene 4 together run about sixty lines with the only change being that a fire is lit and a story is told. Scene 5 is mood only.
PITCH SEEDS
- Cut the second story and the second set of banter and give the team a real conversation about what Luca did, since that is the emotional turn of the chapter. ANCHOR "I will need some time," he said carefully, "to decide how I feel about traveling with you."
- Decide whether the team should see Luca's shadow power this early, because the ledger holds his Shadow Walker status for later and the fight undercuts that. ANCHOR "He stepped sideways into the shadow of his horse and disappeared."
- Open the chapter with the reason for the ride so the reader knows the mission, since the garrison line is the only link. ANCHOR "A detachment of thirty Aldorian soldiers occupied them"
