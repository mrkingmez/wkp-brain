### STORY NOTES
IMPRESSION
A long closing debrief that gathers the survivors, pays them, and turns the team into a standing unit. It is warm and earned in the mood, and the Nyssa exit, the Garran name and the left coin are the best beats. The middle is mostly retelling of events the reader just watched, so the pace slows right before the book ends. The Adin reveal and the shadow unit speech are the biggest new information and they sit inside the longest stretch of recap. The chapter sets up Book 2 clearly and keeps its secrets, but the end speech turns quickly toward killing powerful men.

- The company becomes a standing shadow unit serving the crown. New, only lightly planted.
- Reau's father was murdered and he has a hunt. Planted in Ch28, and Arthur is treated as one person.
