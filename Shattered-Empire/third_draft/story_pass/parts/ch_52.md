### STORY NOTES
IMPRESSION
This is the emotional low point of the book and it works as the pivot for the Nyssa contract. The first half is a calm reunion and recap and the second half is a long, brutal ambush. The Riven death is the strongest beat, the torture that follows is much longer than the story needs and starts to repeat itself. The prayer and the voice at the end is the real payoff and it arrives quickly after all the cruelty. Dain is mostly passive in the second half, so the chapter belongs to Nyssa and to Rouric.
REVEALS in this chapter
- Lysana is dead and Luca walked away alone after the killing. Told by the brothers, planted by the Ch40 events.
- Riven dies in the river, body not recovered. Planted in Ch41, Ch42 and Ch51, fully set up.
- Merrick still hunts Nyssa and Dain and knows Dain's cover name. Planted in Ch12 and Ch15, but how Rouric found them at this port is not planted.
- The contract granted by a voice. Planted in Ch20 and Ch40, fully set up, but the price is only felt in the Epilogue.
MISSING
- How Rouric and his men reached the port city, and how they knew this crew would be on this pier.
- Any sign of the price of the contract in this scene, so the Epilogue severance has no early echo beyond a lock of hair.
- What the Pass team did with Luca after the battle, and why Dain does not ask more about it.
- How Dain and the others know the Pass team will be at this particular port.
NOTHING CHANGES
- Scene 3, the bandage, snack order and small talk on the pier, where only the clock is repeated.
- Scene 4 changes the Riven and Dain relationship but could be shorter.
