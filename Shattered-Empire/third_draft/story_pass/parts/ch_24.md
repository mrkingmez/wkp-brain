### STORY NOTES
IMPRESSION
This chapter is the court track's first real hook. The best scene is Drake and Colton in the corridor, because it turns a smiling banquet into a threat and gives Drake a reason to lie. The Merrick scene reads as a villain rant and returns the reader to Forgehallow, where the break just ended, which makes it feel like it belongs a chapter earlier. The rooftop watcher is a good close, though it brings a new name list with no setup. The chapter is mostly short scenes that each plant something.

- Colton, the head steward, is holding Drake's family hostage. Planted only here. This is the reader's first look at Cotter as coercer (ledger F-13 places it here, which fits).
- Drake knows what Merrick is truly building and hides it. New. Planted by nothing earlier.
- A hooded watcher is hunting the queen with a list. Planted by nothing, the ledger notes this is not in the Guide.
- Merrick still names Dain only by his cover. Planted by Ch19.
