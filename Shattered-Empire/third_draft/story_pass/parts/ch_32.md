### STORY NOTES
IMPRESSION
A long atmosphere chapter that does its job. Stormhaven feels wrong, and the idea of a city emptied and ruled through a hand is strong. The correction scene, where a man collapses and is put back in place, is the best moment and the real hook. After it the chapter loops, the same point about occupation is made three times, and the stable and flirting scenes cool it down more than the stakes call for.
REVEALS in this chapter
The city is occupied and its people are being controlled, planted as a creeping fear and then shown plainly with the correction. Red Cloaks carry shards and are fed power on a schedule. A figure in robes watches from a roof. Riven's old smuggler route. Reau has been under a chain before. Luca has promised the chapel, and the clock is two days.
