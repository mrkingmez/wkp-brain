### STORY NOTES
IMPRESSION
This is the chapter where the team is born and the reader meets a dozen people in one room. It works because Harmon's veil and white sword are an event and because Baldwin's failure shows the price of age at the end. The weakness is the parade. Every new name gets one beat and the interviews are long, and the same address to the group is told twice. Several candidates walk out. Adin's list from Ch3 almost never appears, so the reader holds two casts at once.
REVEALS in this chapter
- Harmon's veil and white sword. Planted in Ch1 as a hint, fair, the first real show of power.
- Harmon is far older than the people in the room. New and fair.
- Luca now knows what Harmon is. New, a fair setup for Ch23.
- Aymir and Taerel are half brothers by one father, matching canon, against Luca's not really brothers in Ch3.
- Neavin is bonded to Veyrith from a Breach. New, planted.
- Aurelia is a Yellow and a second one after Weslin, with her temper about people thinking she reads minds.
MISSING
- Why Garran and Lysana are here and what they are to each other. They are named in the opening and then stand silent for most of the chapter.
- The late woman in dark robes with a staff. She arrives, is never named or addressed, and the reader cannot tell if she is one of the dismissed.
- Why Harmon trusts Dain. He says I have seen you duel, which tells the reader nothing, and he never says why Dain is a fit for a stealth mission.
NOTHING CHANGES
- Scene 3 up to the shout is a long argument among candidates that repeats the same noble boy and brawler beat.
- The second half of scene 5, the interview of Faelan and the mountain man, mixes who is speaking and leaves the reader unsure who Veyrith belongs to.
PITCH SEEDS
- Cut Adin's ledger from Ch3 so it names only the people who show up here, or make the late arrivals here the names Adin read. Anchor "Gusterson?" in Ch3 and "A woman in dark robes with a staff and a pack on her" here.
