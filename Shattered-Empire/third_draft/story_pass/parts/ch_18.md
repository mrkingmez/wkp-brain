### STORY NOTES
IMPRESSION
Two very different scenes sit side by side. The lesson is a quiet lore chapter that tells the reader how shards work and who Weslin is, and it gives a mood of Aurelia not being fully in the room. The Gilded Chamber duel is the better piece because it puts Dain and Merrick face to face and shows Merrick's menace. But the duel runs long and loops, and Dain gives away too much, naming Riven and calling him "our shadow" in public. The report from Aldrek adds the first real lead on the vault. The note at the end is the best hook of the chapter.
REVEALS in this chapter
Shards harden and radiate, and the Debt is the thinning of the bloodline (lore, partly new). The pen from the feast held Weslin's shard and Braden felt it burn (restates F-08, conflicts with C-22). Merrick bought the old Tannith silver mine six months ago, pushed off the miners, and moves chained people at night (new). Merrick is hunting a lockbreaker for an etched warding (new, ties to Riven and F-32). Merrick knows Dain is looking for Riven (new). A boy hands Dain a note, content unread by the reader (new).
