### STORY NOTES
IMPRESSION This is the chapter the Pass track has been building toward and it delivers the loss. The first half is a grinding melee with clear beats, Fenrick, the orbs, the false hope when the poison fails. Lysana death is quick, almost thrown at the reader, and then the chapter slows to one long beat of grief and the shard breaking. The darkness scene is the best writing in the block, because it shows the cost without showing the monsters. The end line gives it a hook. The weakness is that the melee drags a little between the orbs and the twelve orcs.
REVEALS in this chapter The shard does not run dry, it cracks to dust and releases shade creatures that destroy the enemy. Lysana dies. The poison fails on twelve. Planted by earlier Luca shard strain and the romance beats in Ch37 and Ch39, the shade creatures are new in how they look and are not planted before this chapter.
MISSING A reaction of any soldier to what the darkness was, and any view of Luca right after. A clear look at how the orcs survived. What happens to the dead, Lysana body, Fenrick body. No pause for Aymir and Lysana, who were just joking in Ch37, beyond the one cry.
NOTHING CHANGES Scene 5 repeats the earlier rescue by Lysana in a different form. Late melee between the orb and the twelve orcs adds length and little else.
