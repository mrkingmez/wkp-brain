### STORY NOTES
IMPRESSION The chapter is one long loft night and most of it is talking. The two debrief passes carry the same findings, the second one adding almost nothing new except Weslin's vision being tied to Korr. The best material is the human beats, Weslin and Reau, Dain and Nyssa, Dain against Riven. The note and the mirror call at the end finally give the chapter a clock. The shape is good, the middle is too long and too self aware about being a second pass.
REVEALS in this chapter The shrine is fake but the worshippers are real and rewritten. The explosion was a seal break from below. A warbeast vault sits under the forge district. Governor family is alive and has a dusk deadline. A vault of old plans exists and a key sits behind a statue plaque. All planted earlier except the note author, who is not planted at all.
MISSING Who left the note and why, the reader and team both get no follow up here. Why Drake would be reachable by mirror in Opindale at this hour is fine, but the team never asks how the note writer knew about the mirror link. Harmon never says out loud why he trusts a note.
