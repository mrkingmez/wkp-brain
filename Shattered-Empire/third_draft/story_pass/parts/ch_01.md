### STORY NOTES
IMPRESSION
A warm, quick, character first chapter that sets the court, the secret ring, and then jumps hard into two disasters. Weslin and Harmon are likable and the Cotter humiliation gives the plot an enemy early. The pace splits oddly, the first half is slow comic business and the last third is a heavy information dump of two attacks. The Harmon retirement and island request is a surprising long wind up that the rest of the chapter wipes out.
REVEALS in this chapter (what the reader learns, and is it planted)
- Stormhaven has fallen and the pass has fallen. Delivered flatly by the King and Terance, no earlier plant needed, fine.
- Harmon wants to step down and is aware of his age. Planted well with the knee joke and the flashback.
- The ring on a chain is shown. Planted, good.
- Cotter says nothing suspicious but Harmon notes his staff lied about the audience, which is a quiet and good plant.
MISSING (moments the reader needs and does not get, decisions off page)
- No hint of what the feast is for or what the Accord is. Feast day is named once and then forgotten.
- Harmon never says why the ring or the war memory means he must step down. His reason stays vague.
- Harmon never answers why he thinks the two attacks are linked or what he wants done, he says only that he will return in five hours.
- The island proposal is dropped without a word once the news arrives, so the reader is not told if it is dead.
NOTHING CHANGES (scenes where nothing changes, with scene number)
- Scene 2 mostly gives mood and one reprimand. It plants the secret stair and the Mad but does not change the situation.
PITCH SEEDS (cut, merge, move, add scene, reveal timing, each with an EXACT ANCHOR quote)
