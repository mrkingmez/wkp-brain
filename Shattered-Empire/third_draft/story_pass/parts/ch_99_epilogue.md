### STORY NOTES
IMPRESSION
Two short scenes that set up Book 2. The mirror scene is tight and gives the reader the villain's reply to everything the heroes just won. The warehouse scene pays off the Nyssa contract, the lock of hair, and the Luca stone thread in one place. The Nyssa scene leans on a long memory montage that restates what the reader already felt in Ch51 and Ch52. The remaking is clean and cinematic, and the closing line gives her a target, Merrick, which is smaller than the stakes the mirror just raised.
REVEALS in this chapter
- Another hand will be sent for the king and a second lieutenant is hidden even from Zhaelor. Planted only by the Prologue mirror, so mostly new.
- Zhaelor secretly plans to turn on his master. Planted by SG7, new on the page.
- Luca holds the Judgement Stone and can give a seed. Planted in Ch20 and Ch40, fully set up.
- Nyssa takes the Dark Touch and loses Dain as a lover. Planted by Ch52 and Ch53.
