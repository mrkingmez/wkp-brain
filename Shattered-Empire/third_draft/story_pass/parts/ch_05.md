### STORY NOTES
IMPRESSION
A tight political chapter with a real turn. The core is the trade, a nameless capability for the crown in exchange for pardons and the title, and the way Edric glances away before he agrees. Edric is shown as warm and calculating. The mask motif is good. The weakness is that Harmon re-argues a decision the reader already saw him make in Ch2, and the council scene runs two beats of "you ask a lot" before it turns. Drake's family oath is a fine plant but Drake is a passenger.
REVEALS in this chapter
- Harmon will leave his title at the end of the mission. New and strong, a quiet price paid by the main character.
- The crown wants a capability that carries no name. New, and it leans toward Edric's later use of the team.
- Harmon watched Edric as a child of seven. New, a lovely plant for F-03, Harmon being far older than he looks.
- Drake gives his gold and Harmon swears to find his family. Planted, pays in Ch36 and Ch45 to Ch48.
- Rhanis Tal-Kor feels wrong to Harmon. Planted, payoff not in this block.
