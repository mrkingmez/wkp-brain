### STORY NOTES
IMPRESSION
This is the launch chapter and the pacing is good, quiet, a little slow in the first page and a half, then Adin's scene gives a clear stake, a ship in fourteen days and a message to the king. The briefing is clean but retells what the reader knows. The Nyssa scene is the best part of the chapter, an unforced hook for the Forgehallow arc. The chapter ends without a clear answer from Harmon, which is a good beat for the next chapter if Ch9 answers it.
REVEALS in this chapter
- Harmon's mirror to the king and Adin's sending stone. Planted, pays in Ch36 and across the Accord days.
- The ship in fourteen days. Planted, reason not given.
- Help is already in the city but they do not know it is Adin. New, a good seed.
- Riven is in a Forgehallow cell and is family to Nyssa. New, fair.
- Two teams, a command cell. Planted here for the first time.
MISSING
- Harmon's answer to Nyssa. He says get some rest and the reader does not see a yes or no. The next chapter must show the decision or the reader will assume it.
- Why the ship is for fourteen days and what it is for. The map case is shown and the spot is not named.
- Why Aurelia and Aldrek are not on either team. The split names nine and the team is eleven.
