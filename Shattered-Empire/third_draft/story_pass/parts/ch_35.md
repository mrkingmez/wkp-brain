### STORY NOTES
IMPRESSION
This is the biggest chapter of the Stormhaven run and the one where the pieces finally fit. The plaza ward array, the walking tower, the conditioned civilians and the war-beast give the occupation a face and a plan. The Liora scene is the emotional centre and it earns its place. The weakness is repetition. The temple scene that Ch34 already gave is done again with the same lines, and the day is recapped inside the chapel, tower and tea. The ending on a quiet regroup after the war-beast reveal drops the force of the best discovery.
REVEALS in this chapter
The plaza was a ward array fed by twelve governor statues, broken from below on purpose, planted well for the Unity Square crater. The tower with walkers circling it is the mind-control device in early form. The nobles at the tea are loyal to the Speaker and say the Loyalty Test will reshape traitors. A war-beast from the Second Magic War sleeps under the city and is being charged. Weslin sees a masked figure in a chamber that notices him, and learns a ward muffles thought. Dain's sister Liora died in a staged riding accident.
MISSING
Nobody reports the war-beast to Harmon on the page, and Harmon and Garr do not say why they were waiting in the alley. There is no response to the Loyalty Test warning. Weslin's vision of the masked figure is not told to Harmon. The plaza and the war-beast are not tied together by anyone. How the two sources of the Korr name connect to the chamber is not said.
NOTHING CHANGES
Scene 3 mostly repeats the Ch34 temple scene. Scene 6 reads as a recap of what both Dain and Nyssa already saw. Scene 9 is a walk home with one atmosphere beat.
