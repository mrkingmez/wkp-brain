### STORY NOTES
IMPRESSION
This is the Pass track's first full set piece and it is clean and tense. The staff mystery and the false calm after the war chief give it a second wave. The weakest point is the sequence of kills, which runs long with little change after the first rush. The chapter's real hook is the vision of the face over the woman, the staff as the thing holding the prisoners and the three deaths when the spell peels. The reader learns what the Pass team is up against and it begins to explain the "orc cunning" in ch_15.
REVEALS in this chapter
A runed staff in the firepit anchors the mind control (F-51, planted here). Prisoners came from Fort Dran and were taken in the pass attack (planted, ties to the Prologue). Luca sees another face over the woman, pale, blue eyed, hair like summer wheat, a face he remembers (new, unexplained). Luca's amulet is an onyx shard on silver wire (F-21 plant). An orc war chief with a clouded eye leads the camp (new). Aymir whispers Zaa'hil, the eye opens (new, unexplained).
MISSING
Nobody asks who the woman with the second face is or why Luca saw it. The three prisoners who die when the binding peels get no beat of grief or choice. Why the team does not hide or destroy the staff and instead carries it. How Luca knew to cover the staff with a sack is a single beat with no reasoning. The team does not discuss the pass attack the survivors describe.
NOTHING CHANGES
Scene 3 is a long fight where only the count of dead changes. The second wave of two orcs at lines 116 to 127 adds no stakes.
PITCH SEEDS
- Cut the fight by about a third and give the vision of the second face more room, since that is the chapter's mystery. ANCHOR "he saw another face layered over hers, a face he remembered."
- Make the staff choice matter by showing why the team cannot destroy it and what it costs to carry it. ANCHOR "Taerel struck it with his scimitar. The blade rang and skidded off clean"
- Give the three deaths a beat of weight or tie them to the sack choice so the cost lands. ANCHOR "The spell wasn’t breaking. It was peeling. Slowly, unevenly."

The prisoners say Fort Dran, which is the mechanical fix to Fort Dreknor. ANCHOR "We were near Fort Dran. Heading west to trade." Aymir wields a saber and Taerel a scimitar, but ch_13 has them as riflemen with no blades named. Lysana tucks her braid under her hood and Taerel also has a braid in ch_13.
