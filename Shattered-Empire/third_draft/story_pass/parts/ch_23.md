IMPRESSION
This is the payoff of the prison break and it is mostly escape logistics. The break itself is quick, three rooms of ward and lock work followed by a walk out in disguise. The strongest beats are the embrace between Nyssa and Riven with Dain's flash of hurt, Tarn's long look, and the cold finish with Harmon swaying. The first half is plain and a bit long for a rescue that goes smoothly. The Harmon fight is the one real fight in the chapter and it is where the weight lies. Tarn's hatred is a good plant.
