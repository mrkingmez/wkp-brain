### STORY NOTES
IMPRESSION
This chapter turns the recruiting into a ledger and a list, and it is the first time we see Adin's own mind. The painting and the ring are strong images that give him a past. The ledger scene is too long for what it gives, because most of the names it lists (Gusterson, Renic, Senna, Orist, Fell, Cael, Pelkin) are not paid off here, and the reader will wonder which of them show up at the stable. The shove scene is the best beat, the only moment with real tension.
REVEALS in this chapter
- Adin withheld fragments from Harmon. Planted here, a good seed, payoff later in the book is not visible in this block.
- The ring would kill Luca if he touched it. New, tied to F-06.
- Luca is the last of his kind. New, partly seeded in Ch2, fair.
- Aymir and Taerel are not really brothers by Luca's word, which sits against the canon of half-brothers. Reader takes it as secret from the others, ruled fine if it reads as loose talk.
- The Last Light of Aether was stolen by Adin. Fair, but the painting does nothing yet.
MISSING
- Which of the named recruits actually come. The summons go out for about ten people and the reader never sees the list tied to the stable scene unless Ch5 does it.
- What Adin's withheld fragments are. The reader does not know what is being held back, only that something is.
- Why Adin wants his own man with Harmon beyond trust. The cost Luca names ("You know the cost") is never explained.
NOTHING CHANGES
- Scene 3 is a long tour of the room and a banter about pay, and nothing changes except the painting is named.
- The second half of scene 5, the long run of names, mostly repeats the same move, name then objection then approval.
PITCH SEEDS
- Cut the ledger run to the three or four names who matter, and have Adin decide fast. Anchor "Gusterson?" for the first name and "Start writing the summons. I will use my network to deliver them." for the end.
- Move the withheld fragments to a short concrete image, one thing Adin knows and does not tell, so the reader holds it. Anchor "I withheld, it" is not safe so use "He had not told Harmon about the fragments because fragments meant nothing yet" as the scene to sharpen.
- Merge the two ring beats into one scene so the shove lands at the first ring mention rather than after a joke run. Anchor "The speed at which Adin moved from his desk to shove Luca".
CONTINUITY
- Ch2 has Harmon say "Fourth bell. Tonight." and Ch3 says the ride is "at the fourth bell. Before dawn." Anchor "He wants to ride at the fourth bell. Before dawn." Check that the fourth bell is meant as the night bell in both.
- Luca says he stood before the painting "thirty years ago" and has worked at the Bolt "ten years now". Fine, but the canon says he runs the cellar with Adin for ten years, so thirty years ago is before Adin. Anchor "We have worked out of here for ten years now."
- Adin has a black-stone ring on a shelf while Harmon's ring is a twin worn on a chain. See QUESTIONS.
- Luca says "I am the last" and Adin says he understands, and the canon has the Night Veil near extinct, not extinct. Minor.
QUESTIONS
- Is the black stone ring bound in iron the same as Adin's own Ringbearer ring, or a different object? F-05 says Adin's ring is Harmon's twin and F-06 treats it as a ring that would kill Luca.
- Is it intended that Luca knows Adin has a Ringbearer's ring so early? He says "strength of twenty men" aloud.
