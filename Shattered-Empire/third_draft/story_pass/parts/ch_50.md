IMPRESSION
This is the pass track's quiet aftermath after about ten chapters away, and it works as a deep breath. The scene with Corren Vey is the plot payload and the chapter's reason to exist. The rest is two brothers in snow talking through the same three choices. The chapter ends on a joke and a warm road, which is a nice release, but it asks the reader to hold a lot of loose threads without a push.
