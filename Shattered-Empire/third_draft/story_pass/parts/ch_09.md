### STORY NOTES
IMPRESSION
This is the first movement chapter and it does two jobs, it splits the team and it turns the Riven ask into a plot. It works because Nyssa's failure forces Harmon's choice, so the detour feels earned, not asked for. The splits are clean and the bracelet system gives the reader a clear sense of how teams stay in contact. The weak point is the opening, which spends a full page on Rellisford sights with no beat of story. Aurelia's training of Weslin is fine but Harmon retells the feast again to explain it.
REVEALS in this chapter
- Aurelia will train Weslin and she is the communications lead. New, fair.
- The bracelets give direct thought contact, one send at a time. New, a rule for later.
- Nyssa has a record in Emberfast and a known face. Planted, the canon lists an Emberfast merchant claiming she robbed him.
- The plan is now to free Riven from Forgehallow. Planted in Ch8 and settled here.
MISSING
- Who made the bracelets. The canon has Taerel making the communication bracelets but the page has Harmon produce them from a bag.
- A goodbye between Weslin and Luca or Lysana. Weslin sees them go as a last glimpse, which may be the point, but the reader has no scene to hold.
- How the Stormhaven team learns of the Forgehallow change. Dain is told to tell everyone they meet outside the town, and then everyone rides east together.
- The Pass team's reaction to leaving. They do not appear in the chapter after the split.
NOTHING CHANGES
- Scene 1 is a long description of Rellisford that changes nothing.
- The first half of scene 4 repeats the awakening so Aurelia can learn it, and nothing new comes from it.
PITCH SEEDS
- Cut the opening description of Rellisford to a short set of details, and let Weslin's first look land on one image. Anchor "This was the first place Weslin had been other than Opindale".
- Let Aurelia learn the feast story from Weslin's own mouth or from Aurelia's read of him, and cut Harmon's retelling. Anchor "wanted him to burn, and the boy thought his skin was on fire".
- Add one beat to the split that shows who is going where and why Garr leads Stormhaven, so the Forgehallow change in scene 6 feels like a decision about a team and not only about Nyssa. Anchor "Lysana. Aymir. Taeral. Luca. The pass."
CONTINUITY
- The boat arrives under oars, and Ch8 describes the Wyrm's Wake as pulled by tetherline rails. Anchor "its hull creaking as oars dipped rhythmically on either side".
- Ch8 split the team into the Pass four and the Stormhaven five with a command cell. Ch9 puts Aldrek on the Stormhaven side with Ash, Dain, Nyssa and Garr, and Aurelia and Neavin on Harmon's side. This resolves the Ch8 gap, which is good. Anchor "Aurelia. Neavin. Weslin. You are with me."
- Taerel is spelled Taeral here, a locked mechanical fix. Anchor "Lysana. Aymir. Taeral. Luca. The pass."
- Harmon says "so I can relay it back to the crown" while the mirror was handed to Adin to take to the King in Ch8. Where Harmon's own end of the mirror sits is not shown. Anchor "Please keep me updated so I can relay it back to the crown."
- Six hours upriver from Opindale matches the canon. Anchor "They had traveled about six hours upriver from Opindale."
- Weslin was never tested. Ch4 has Mjakazi say he was afraid to test him and Ch6 has Harmon run a test with the pin, so this reads as consistent. Anchor "Not a late bloomer. His dad let him slip through."

- Is Nyssa's Emberfast accusation meant to be true? The canon lists it as a merchant's claim, and her denial reads as a lie on the page.
