### STORY NOTES
IMPRESSION
Strong turn, the strongest in this block. The thought list on the balcony is original and the reader can feel the sound of a crowd mind. The bully scene is classic and works because it costs Weslin something. The awakening is clear and the cost is shown, Braden's pain. The chapter then retells the event twice in the corridor, which slows the aftermath. The best new material is the thoughts that hint at other plots in the hall, the question is whether they pay.
REVEALS in this chapter
- Weslin is a Yellow Adept who projects thought. Planted by Ch4 Have you had him tested, fair.
- Allison is a close friend, not a noble. Her ease with the pages and the bully insult stable-warmer plant it, but the guard line sits against it.
- Harmon ran a test with a yellow shard pin. New, a clear object.
- The thoughts name loose threads. The Governor is to be moved soon, someone does not trust Ayhmeri, someone sold something to a Moradaer envoy, a guest room was prepared in the east wing in secret from the head steward. These are plants and the reader will wonder who they pay to.
