### STORY NOTES
IMPRESSION
Two old friends meet in a loud tavern and one chapter sets up the whole team idea. The engine is clear and the chapter is mostly talk. The best material is the silence in the news and Adin's refusal to ride. The weaker part is how often the same beat is stated, Harmon sees Luca as a soldier three times and the reunion toast happens twice. The chapter ends on Adin alone, a fine image, and no tension beat shows what the plan costs.
REVEALS in this chapter
- Stormhaven struck and the pass attacked, Adin's network has nothing. Planted in Ch1 by the feast news, so it lands as an echo, but the reader hears the same news a second time.
- Arthur's son is a past failure for Harmon and Adin. New and lightly planted, it pays off at Ch28 and Ch53.
- Adin cannot ride because of a price. Planted by the ring glint, fair.
- Harmon has used other names ("Harmon, still?" and "Harmon, that is what you are going by now"). New, a fair plant for F-03.
- Weslin is a baker's son and the baker is known to Adin ("Not that baker's son?"). A plant for Mjakazi, fair.
MISSING
- How Harmon knows the Veydrassil are behind the strike when reports are scarce and Adin's people know nothing. No source is named, so Harmon looks better informed than the spy chief.
- Harmon never says why he is not simply asking the King for a small team, which would be official. The reason it must be outside official means is only stated as a preference.
- Why Adin trusts Harmon after Harmon's patrols took his lads. It is waved away by a laugh.
NOTHING CHANGES
- Scene 1 and the second half of scene 3 hold a lot of observation with no change. Scene 7, apart from Weslin being called, is a farewell that repeats Harmon's earlier cheer.
PITCH SEEDS
- Cut one of the two toasts and one of the three Luca is a soldier beats. Anchor "Merriment this loud rarely comes without purpose" for the opening and "Then keep the full second description intact." for the pasted note.
- Move the question of how Harmon knows who struck Stormhaven into the booth scene, so the reader sees Harmon has a source of his own. Anchor "Stormhaven has been struck. Aldoria reels, and the word is that".
- Sharpen the cost of the plan. Right now the team is a good idea, nobody names a deadline or a personal risk. Anchor "Armies march too slowly, and they draw too much attention."
CONTINUITY
- Adin has a scar that pulls tight, and the page names an Orin accent ("his Orin accent rough and heavy"). Check the accent against later Adin chapters where he is described as ancient and vague.
- "Fourth bell. Tonight." and the sun is high, so the recruiting day is the feast day. Ch1 Harmon "ran farther tonight than most grown men" says the run was the night before, check against the Ch1 time of day. Anchor "And he ran farther tonight than most grown men would dare."
- Harmon takes the seat and a cup at the bar, then another cup in the booth, then speaks of "my night off". Minor.
QUESTIONS
- Why does Harmon ask "Can I have Luca?" when Adin just said he is a special case that must not be seen? Is it Harmon's bold read of Luca or a plan Adin shares. Zac to rule.
