### STORY NOTES
IMPRESSION
This chapter changes the genre of the book for a while. It cuts from the dark city to a warm tense court and answers who coerced Drake. The war chamber scene is a strong stage of a man holding a dagger while his family is hanged across the continent. The rest is a series of plot confirmations, the Mirelle and Edric plan, the old man's warning and the missing king. The old man and the changeling beat is the best moment, because it plants Lucent without naming him.
