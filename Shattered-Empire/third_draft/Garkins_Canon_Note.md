# Garkins Canon Note and Chapter 16 Insert

## Canon
- Sir Evens Garkins of Woford was marching to the pass with new soldiers and part of the army he commands, to take over the frontier fort from Sir Jorah (Prologue). He never arrived.
- His column was hit when the pass fell. Cause of the soldiers turning on each other: the staff's influence (Ch 16), by Zac's design. Say so in the Guide.
- His chief of staff led the camp followers (cooks, farriers, healers) south so as many as possible could get away, and stayed back to cover them. He is one of the men who died when the mind control peeled off at the tree (Ch 16). Name: open, not invented.
- The smaller orcs were what was left of the force, sent to catch runners and mop up stragglers. The five-orc camp with the black-rooted tree is where they held what they caught.
- Garkins's horse was killed in the first volley and he ran. The sending stone was in the saddlebag on the dead horse and he never looked. So no one at court could reach him. Where he went is open on purpose, for Book 2.
- Aurelia's team never finds this camp. Luca's pass team finds it (Ch 15 and 16). The Guide line 'never arrived' stays true.

## Draft insert for Ch 16 (after "We stopped thinking straight.", before "That's enough.")

A gray-haired woman near the back lifted her head. Her hands still shook, but her voice had steadied.
“We weren’t traders,” she said. “That is what we told the gate. We were the column’s people. Cooks. Farriers. Two healers. Wherever the army goes, we go behind it.”
Taerel’s eyes sharpened. “Which army?”
“Sir Evens Garkins’s. Marching to take the fort.” She said the name flat, with no prayer in it. “His chief of staff had us in the rear when the cliffs lit up. He turned every wagon he could south and told us to run. He stayed back to give us the time.”
Her gaze drifted to the older man who had gone still in the snow. She held it there a long moment.
“We looked back once,” she said. “The soldiers were fighting each other. Our own men, in the road, with nobody giving orders. The smaller orcs came out of the rocks after that. They were not there to fight. They were there to collect whoever ran.”
“And Sir Garkins?” Luca asked.
“His horse went down in the first volley. He got clear of it and ran.” Her mouth twisted. “The sending stone was in his saddlebag, on the dead horse. He did not stop to look. He did not look back at all.”
Luca said nothing. Taerel let out a slow breath through his nose.
