# Premiere Pro and YouTube fact check
Date 2026-11-20. Requested by studio-director for the printable Premiere guide. Read-only web research, $0.

## Limits of this check
- Every helpx.adobe.com page I tried returned HTTP 403 (Color mode basics, Generative Extend, Generative credits FAQ, keyboard shortcuts, Generative Extend FAQ). I could not read Adobe Help directly. Adobe facts below come from search result excerpts of Adobe pages, an Adobe Learn page, Adobe staff replies in the Adobe Community, and Pro Video Coalition. Anything not seen in an Adobe or Google source is marked NOT VERIFIED or PARTLY.
- The Google YouTube Help end screen page was read in full for the quoted lines.
- Which version Zac uses is unknown. Both v26 and v27 are noted where known.
