## Notes on the people claims

- Gable and Heston and Brooks and Bea Arthur and Lee all served. The list is mostly sound, two names are not. Dietrich and Hepburn are civilians with real wartime stories. Easy fix is one caption.
- The Stewart DFC count differs by source. Marshall Foundation says DFC with oak leaf cluster, so two. Eisenhower Foundation says two clusters, so three. Wikipedia (as read) says a single DFC. The best-supported reading is two. Official record not retrieved.
- Vietnam claim at 00:46:14 (Stewart flew in Vietnam). Wikipedia says February 1966, as a non-duty observer in a B-52 on an Arc Light mission, so "flew in Vietnam" is true but he was an observer. Not in the requested list, noted for context.
