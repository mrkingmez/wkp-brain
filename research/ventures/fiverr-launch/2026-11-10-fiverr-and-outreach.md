1. Fiverr keeps 20 percent. A 20 dollar photo order pays you 16 dollars. Payment sits 14 days after the order completes before you can withdraw.
2. New sellers can have only 4 gigs. We use 2 (one per business). Paused and draft gigs count against the 4.

### Seller fee
- Fiverr takes 20 percent of every transaction, including extras and add-ons. Seller keeps 80 percent of the client's cleared payment. SEARCH-OF-FIVERR, and consistent with the earlier market file.
- One forum post claims 25 percent. That is one user's report, not Fiverr. Not used.
- Buyer-side service fees are separate, charged on top of the gig price. Amount NOT AVAILABLE this session. Your listed price is what the buyer sees before their fee, so the 20 percent comes out of your listed price.
- Math at our prices: 20 dollars pays 16. 50 pays 40. 75 pays 60. 25 pays 20. 75 pays 60. 150 pays 120.

### Payout timing and minimums
- Clearing period: 14 days after the order is completed before the money can be withdrawn. Top Rated, Seller Plus Premium, and Pro sellers get a shorter period (7 days per one Fiverr guide). You will not be any of those at launch. SEARCH-OF-FIVERR.
- Withdrawal minimums by method: PayPal 1 dollar, Payoneer account 10 dollars, bank transfer via Payoneer 20 dollars, Fiverr Revenue Card 30 dollars. SEARCH-OF-FIVERR. Fees per withdrawal vary by method (PayPal 0 dollars listed, Payoneer 3 dollars, bank transfer 1 dollar, Revenue Card 1 to 3 dollars, as read in a search excerpt of the table). Check the table on screen.
- First withdrawal and any change of payout method has a 24 hour wait. Changing the phone number has a 48 hour wait. Max 5,000 dollars per transaction. SEARCH-OF-FIVERR.
- Early payout: 1 percent of the earned amount, only for eligible sellers (Top Rated and Seller Plus Premium named). Not available to a new seller. SEARCH-OF-FIVERR.
- Real timeline for a first order: order placed day 0, delivered day 5, buyer accepts (or auto-completes after Fiverr's window, length NOT AVAILABLE), then 14 days. Plan on about 3 weeks from first order to first withdrawable dollar. INFERRED from the figures above.
