Order folder, per customer, under `D:\WKP\scratch\photo-restore\` (never the repo, customer photos are private): `<date>-<firstname>\` with subfolders `01-original` (read only, never edited), `02-work`, `03-preview`, `04-final`, plus `order-log.md`. Delete after 30 days unless the customer says keep (proposal, Zac decides).

Steps per photo:
1. Intake check (Claude, 5 min): look at the file. Grade it A (light damage), B (creases, fading, stains), C (face damage or missing area), D (too small or blurred to help). Tell Zac the grade and a yes or no. D is a polite no. C gets the honest-limits note before any work.
2. Save a copy. Original goes to `01-original` and is never opened for editing.
3. Zac restores in Photoshop from a duplicate: straighten and crop, levels, dust and scratches, repair creases and stains in non-face areas, then gentle sharpen. Color last, only if the face passes.
4. Face rule: faces get cleanup only (dust, scratch, tone). No feature is redrawn. If any restore step changes eyes, nose, mouth, hairline, or age look, back it out and use the cleaned-only version, or tell the customer the face is beyond what we can honestly fix.
5. Quality check (Claude and Zac): view original and result side by side at 100 percent on the face, and at fit-to-screen. Pass bar below.
6. Preview: Claude makes a watermarked, reduced-size preview and a side-by-side image. Zac sends it. Nothing clean goes out yet.
7. Payment (see section 3), then clean file.
8. Deliver: restored file, untouched original, and a 3 line plain note of what was repaired and what was left alone.

Quality bar (a photo ships only if every line is true):
- The face looks like the same person to someone who knew them. No smoothing to plastic, no new teeth, no changed expression.
- No visible clone-stamp repeats, smears, or halo edges at 100 percent.
- Damage that was fixed is gone, damage that was left is named in the note.
- Grain and age are kept enough that it still looks like an old photo.
- File opens on a phone, resolution at least as large as the original (never upscale-promise).
- If it fails, redo once. If it still fails, tell the customer and no charge.

What we refuse to promise (put in the offer and the replies): no guaranteed result, no face invention or "AI fills in the missing part," no turning a blurry photo sharp, no results on photos too small or too damaged, no legal claim to the photo (customer confirms it is theirs or family's), original file always returned untouched, no 48-hour guarantee, no refund-by-surprise: if we say a photo can't be fixed honestly before work starts, no charge.
