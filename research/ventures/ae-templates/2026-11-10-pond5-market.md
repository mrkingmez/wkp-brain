## Verdict (one paragraph)
Pond5 does sell AE templates and Zac can list there, but this is a slow, low-yield play for urgent money. Pond5 pays only 30 percent, sets its own prices and discounts, and the sports category is crowded (2,848 sports AE results on Pond5 against 140 for military). Military is thin on Pond5 but the comparable VideoHive military list shows modest lifetime sales (mostly tens to a few hundred, one at 512) at 18 to 38 dollars, which at a 30 percent cut is a few dollars a sale. I found NO verified seller-reported income for AE templates anywhere. Realistic read: weeks to build a pack, review takes days, first sale could be weeks or months out, and a first month of under 25 dollars (the payout minimum) is a plausible outcome. It will not fix an urgent cash problem. If Zac wants it anyway, the best angle is a small military pack (briefing and tribute titles, lower thirds, unit-style HUD) since it is the less crowded half, built with zero third-party logos or music.

## 6. Legal and licensing traps
- Pond5 rule: all media inside the ZIP must be Zac's own creation. No licensed stock, no copyrighted music, no movie or game trailers, no watermarked content (contributor portal page above). Contributor agreement section 5 and 7 require releases for any third-party trademark, logo, design or copyrighted work, and Zac warrants no infringement and indemnifies Pond5 and buyers.
- Military insignia: DoD and service marks are protected and commercial use needs written permission from the service trademark licensing office. Army regulation bars commercial articles that use designs or likenesses of decorations, medals and ribbons. DoD guidance bars logos built from service marks. Source: search results summarizing DoD Trademark Licensing Guide and service pages (SECONDARY, I could not open DoDI 5410.20 or the guide itself): https://www.trademark.marines.mil/Portals/161/Docs/DOD%20Trademark%20Licensing%20Guide-16%20July%202017_1.pdf ; https://www.hqmc.marines.mil/Agencies/Counsel-for-the-Commandant/Marine-Corps-Trademark-Licensing-Program/Commercial-Use ; https://dodcio.defense.gov/Home/PublicUseNotice/ . Implication (inference): use generic art only, no real service seals, unit patches, rank insignia copies, medals or ribbons, uniforms with real unit marks, or real service names on aircraft or vehicles. Many existing Pond5 listings may do it anyway, that does not make it safe. Ask a lawyer before shipping anything with real insignia.
- Sports team marks: NFL, NCAA, school and club names, logos, and uniforms are trademarked. Generic "football" or "hockey" art is fine; no real team names, mascots, logos, or colors that copy a real team. High school mascots belong to the school, so a recruiting reel kit should use placeholder logo slots (the buyer drops in their own) rather than shipped logos. This is general knowledge, not retrieved from a source in this run, label UNVERIFIED.
- Fonts: do not bundle fonts unless the license allows redistribution; Pond5 asks for font links instead. Use fonts with open licenses (such as SIL Open Font License) and list them. Font license terms not checked in this run.
- Music and sound: none inside the ZIP unless Zac made it. Preview may use outside music if the description says not included, but the preview then is exposed to a claim; safest is Zac's own or clearly licensed owned audio.
- Tutorial-derived projects banned (Video Copilot named).
- AI use: Pond5 has a separate Video AI terms page (https://www.pond5.com/legal/video-ai-terms-of-service). Whether AI-generated parts are allowed inside AE templates is NOT AVAILABLE. Do not include AI-made images in a template without checking with Pond5.
