# Name conflict flags: photo restoration and transcription side businesses

Run: 2026-11-10, clearance agent. Requested by Jarvis for the quick-start money ventures (photo restore, transcription).

**This is a risk flag, not a legal opinion.** Nothing here says any name is safe to use, file, or register. A web search is not a trademark clearance search. Zac makes the call, and before he spends money on a filing, signage, or a domain he plans to build on, a trademark attorney should run a full search (federal, state, and common law).

## How this was searched, and what could not be done

- **Web search (common law use):** DONE for all 10 names. The search engine does not honor exact-phrase quotes reliably, so "no hits" means "nothing surfaced", not "nothing exists".
- **USPTO Trademark Search (tmsearch.uspto.gov):** NOT AVAILABLE. This agent has no page-fetch tool, and the USPTO search is a script-driven app that web search cannot query. Substituted: third-party mirrors of USPTO data (markinton.com, trademark.justia.com, gleanmark.com, ttabvue.uspto.gov index). These are not authoritative. Every serial or registration number below must be checked in USPTO TSDR before anyone relies on it.
- **Georgia Secretary of State business search (ecorp.sos.ga.gov):** NOT AVAILABLE as a live query. Only pages already indexed by the search engine came back. No indexed GA entity matched any of the 10 names. Zac needs to run each name himself at https://ecorp.sos.ga.gov/businesssearch using "Contains".
- Nothing was contacted, filed, or registered.

## Affiliation flags (government, archive, veteran organization)

- **None of the 10 names implies a veteran organization.** If Zac markets either business as veteran-owned, that is a separate claim to keep accurate, and no name here uses VA, veteran, or military wording.
- **Grovetown Photo Repair** and **Grovetown Transcription Desk** use the city name. A city name alone does not claim affiliation, but paired with an official-looking logo or seal it could read as a City of Grovetown service. "Desk" adds to that a little, since it sounds like a clerk's office or records desk.
- **Keeper Photo Restoration** has a faint "records keeper" or archive ring to it. Low risk on the name alone. It rises if the branding leans on archive or library language.
- No name uses "archive", "national", "official", "registry", or "records", which are the words that most often cause affiliation trouble.

- Whether any name is worth filing federally, and whether a descriptive name (Grovetown, Plain Text) is worth building on at all, is an attorney and budget question, not a search question.
