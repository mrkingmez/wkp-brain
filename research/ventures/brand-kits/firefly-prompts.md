## Photo restoration (Same Face)

### P1  Fiverr gig hero background (no faces on purpose)

Prompt: Overhead photograph of a worn dark walnut table in soft window light from the left. A loose scatter of old black and white and sepia prints with white scalloped borders, all turned face down or tilted so nothing on them reads, a few black paper photo corners, a soft white cotton glove, a small soft brush. Shallow depth of field, muted warm palette of cream, brown and one rust accent. Leave the left third of the frame calm and mostly empty for headline text.

Settings: Image, Firefly Image model (Adobe's own, commercially safe line; exact model name on Zac's account NOT VERIFIED, read it off the panel). Content type Photo. Aspect 16:9 (Fiverr wants 1280x769, about 5:3, crop the extra off the top and bottom). Variations 4. Record the seed of the keeper in Advanced > Seed. Visual intensity default.

### P2  Facebook cover background strip

Prompt: Wide flat lay along one horizontal band: a row of aged black and white print corners and torn paper edges on a cream linen surface, warm light, very low contrast, all prints face down, large calm cream area on the left half for text. Soft focus, matte, no strong shadows.

Settings: Image, same model. Aspect 16:9, then crop to the central band for 851x315 (the 2x file is 1702x630). Variations 4. Keep it light, text sits on top.

### P3  Optional hero for a Facebook post, lifestyle

Prompt: A shoebox of old paper prints on a kitchen table beside a mug of coffee, afternoon light through a window, shallow depth of field, prints face down, no people. Warm, quiet, a little nostalgic, not staged-looking.

Settings: Image, same model. Aspect 1:1 for feed, 4 variations. Do not ask for faces. Real before/after images come from Zac's own photos, never generated.

## Transcription (Listen Twice)

### T1  Fiverr gig hero background

Prompt: Close photograph of a tidy desk in cool soft light. Over-ear headphones resting on a closed matte navy notebook, a blurred monitor behind glowing with abstract teal waveform shapes that read as light only, no readable text anywhere. Shallow depth of field, deep navy and teal palette with a clean white highlight. Leave the left third calm and empty for headline text.

Settings: Image, Firefly Image model (name NOT VERIFIED on Zac's account). Content type Photo. Aspect 16:9, crop to 5:3 for 1280x769. Variations 4. Record the seed of the keeper.

### T2  Facebook cover background strip

Prompt: Abstract flowing ribbons of teal and white light shaped like audio waveforms on a deep navy field, wide and horizontal, soft glow, very low detail on the left half so text can sit there, smooth gradient, no text, no symbols.

Settings: Image, same model. Content type Art. Aspect 16:9, crop to the centre band for 851x315 (1702x630 at 2x). Variations 4.

### T3  Optional post hero

Prompt: A podcast microphone on a desk next to a printed transcript page whose lines are blurred beyond reading, cool morning light, shallow depth of field, navy and teal palette, no people.

Settings: Image, same model. Aspect 1:1, 4 variations. Any text on the page must stay unreadable, add real words afterward in Express or code.
