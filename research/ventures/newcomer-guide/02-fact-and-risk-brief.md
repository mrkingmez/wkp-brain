# Newcomer Guide for the Augusta Area Fact and Risk Brief

Date 2026-11-03. Requested by the caller (venture research). Nothing is being built. Saved to the path the caller gave, under research\ventures, not research\books.

Tags used throughout. VERIFIED means read on an official or primary page by this desk on the date shown. SECONDARY means news, trade, or commercial pages. UNVERIFIED means a claim seen but not confirmed, or not found.

Tool note. Several search results below came back as summaries, then key pages were fetched directly. Where only a summary was seen, it is tagged SECONDARY at best.

## 1. Answer in plain words

1. **The base is Fort Gordon again, not Fort Eisenhower.** The Army reverted the name in June 2025 and dedicated it for Master Sgt. Gary I. Gordon on 26 September 2025. A guide titled "Fort Eisenhower" would be wrong on day one. Search traffic and Zac's audience may still use both names.
2. **Most of what a newcomer needs is already free and official** (sponsor, Newcomers Orientation, Lending Closet, ACS Relocation, school liaison). The guide's only room is the independent local layer, which depends on Zac's own knowledge.
3. **The biggest risks are legal and branding, not facts.** Army marks, VA loan and real estate affiliate money (RESPA), fair housing wording, and school-rating data licensing. All are manageable if the guide stays independent, text-only on marks, and ratings-free.
4. **Highest staleness items** are housing prices, BAH, gate rules and hours, school boundaries, and anything with a phone number or room number.

## 4. Legal and platform guardrails

None of this is legal advice. A one hour consult with a GA attorney before launch is cheap insurance. Items marked SECONDARY must be checked against the primary text.

### 4a. Army and DoD names, logos, endorsement
- Army Trademark Licensing Program (ATLP) administers Army marks and requires a license for merchandise bearing Army marks. Displaying marks outside products still means contacting ATLP. SECONDARY (search summary of army.mil/atlp). Primary page to read is [army.mil/atlp](https://www.army.mil/atlp).
- 32 CFR 507.10 allows insignia in commercial articles only with ATLP written permission. SECONDARY. [eCFR 507.10](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/subtitle-A/chapter-V/subchapter-A/part-507/subpart-B/section-507.10).
- DoD guide says non-federal logos incorporating Military Service marks are prohibited, and seals are essentially off limits. SECONDARY. [DoD guide copy](https://secure.in.gov/dva/files/DOD-Guide-About-Use-of-Seals,-Logos,-Insignia,-Service-Medals.PDF). The primary rule is DoDI 5410.20 for commercial use of DoD imagery. [DoDI 5410.20](https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodi/541020p.pdf). Not read in full, UNVERIFIED in detail.
- DoD visual information site statement bars any use that implies endorsement of a non-federal entity or product and defines advertising and marketing as commercial use. SECONDARY. [DoD site statement](https://www.jpeocbrnd.osd.mil/DOD-Site-Statement/).
- Working guardrails for the product. Use no Army star, seal, Cyber Corps or Signal Corps insignia, no DoD or Army photos from DVIDS on the cover or in marketing, no "official" or "U.S. Army" in the product name or shop name. Name the base in plain text only as a place ("a guide to the area around Fort Gordon"). Add a plain disclaimer on cover and listing, "Independent publication, not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Army, the Department of Defense or Fort Gordon." Whether plain-text place names in a title need any permission was not resolved. Treat as a question for counsel. UNVERIFIED.
- Name hazard. Putting "Eisenhower" or "Gordon" in a title also raises search and accuracy issues, not only legal. Decide naming with the 2025 reversal in mind.

### 4b. Real estate and VA loan affiliate links
- RESPA Section 8(a) bars giving or receiving anything of value for referrals of settlement services (lenders, agents, title) on federally related mortgage loans. A pay-per-lead or referral fee from a lender or agent for a guide reader is the risky pattern. SECONDARY. Primary to read is CFPB's RESPA Section 8 FAQs. [Mayer Brown summary of 2020 FAQs](https://www.mayerbrown.com/ja/insights/publications/2020/10/cfpb-replaces-cordray-era-msa-guidance-with-new-respa-section-8-faqs).
- A CFPB consent order involving a veterans organization treated endorsement payments tied to home loan marketing as a Section 8 problem. SECONDARY. [Orrick](https://www.orrick.com/en/Insights/2017/02/CFPB-Consent-Orders-Address-Wide-Range-of-Real-Estate-Referral-Practices-Under-Section-8a-of-RESPA).
- Georgia real estate licensing. Taking a fee for referring buyers or sellers to a licensed agent generally has license law implications. **No Georgia source gathered.** UNVERIFIED. Counsel question.
- Misleading VA loan ads have been a CFPB enforcement area (government affiliation claims, VA terms). SECONDARY. [CFPB blog](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/blog/the-va-doesnt-send-you-mortgage-ads). Any lender link on the guide should not imply VA or Army endorsement.
- Safest model. No lender or agent paid referral links. Pay-once flat sponsorship needs counsel before use. Neutral "how to find a lender" content only.
- FTC. Affiliate relationships need a clear and conspicuous disclosure when consumers would not expect the tie. The 2023 revision of the Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255) added affiliate guidance. SECONDARY (Manatt). [Manatt](https://www.manatt.com/insights/newsletters/advertising-law/an-in-depth-look-at-the-ftcs-updates-2). Primary to read is the FTC Endorsement Guides and FAQ. Amazon Associates links elsewhere in a PDF also need disclosure. Amazon's own program rules were not checked, UNVERIFIED.

### 4c. Fair housing language
- HUD's advertising memo says describing housing, residents or neighbors in racial or ethnic terms creates liability. SECONDARY via summary. [HUD memo](https://hud.gov/sites/documents/DOC_7784.PDF). It is old staff guidance that HUD still points to.
- Familial status is protected. "Family friendly" and "safe neighborhood" are flagged by realtor groups, not HUD text. SECONDARY. [Miami Realtors](https://www.miamirealtors.com/wp-content/uploads/Featured_Images/Services/fair-housing-advertising-guidelines.pdf).
- A guide is not a listing, but describing areas by who lives there is the same risk pattern. Working rule. Describe places by facts (distance, lot size, price band, amenities, year built, commute minutes, link to public crime data) not by people. No "good for X people," no "safe," no "the kind of families." Military status itself is not a federally protected class, but Georgia and other law was not checked. UNVERIFIED.
- Honest tension. The product's pull is "where should I live." Fact-only wording will feel flatter. This is a design cost, not a reason to skip it.

### 4d. School ratings data
- GreatSchools 1 to 10 ratings and boundary data are licensed through its enterprise data license, not the self-serve API. API terms (effective 4 Sep 2024) prohibit use beyond the order form and require attribution. SECONDARY (search summary of GreatSchools pages). [API terms](https://www.greatschools.org/gk/api-terms-of-service/), [Enterprise license](https://solutions.greatschools.org/k12-data-solutions/enterprise-data-license). The general site terms page returned 404, UNVERIFIED.
- Working rule. Do not copy ratings, scores, or maps from GreatSchools, Niche or Zillow. Link out to the Georgia Department of Education report card and district zone lookups. State and district public data is the safer base, with its own terms to check.

### 4e. Etsy
- Etsy digital items must be made or designed by the seller. VERIFIED, [Etsy digital listing help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628347-Creativity-Standards-and-Etsy-s-Policy-on-AI) (page read 2026-11-03, footer 2026). That page did not cover AI.
- AI disclosure and the "Made by / Designed by / Sourced by" creativity labels. Only seller education blogs reached, labels disagree between them. SECONDARY. Etsy's own pages block automated fetch (matches earlier WKP note in TASKS.md, Kingdom Planners AI disclosure row, 2026-09-24). Needs a logged in browser check. Reuse the disclosure approach already locked for Kingdom Planners as a starting point.
- Etsy limit of 5 files, 20MB each per listing. VERIFIED same page.
- Existing WKP rules apply. Etsy listing cap (3 per day, 8 per week, to 15 Nov 2026), and `no-ai-slop` on public copy.

### 4f. Amazon KDP
- KDP requires telling it about AI-generated text, images or translations when publishing or republishing, including cover and interior art, even after heavy edits. AI-assisted content (you wrote it, AI edited or brainstormed) need not be disclosed. VERIFIED, [KDP content guidelines](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390), page read 2026-11-03, no page date shown.
- Descriptions must not mislead about content. VERIFIED, same page.
- Fit note. A PDF newcomer guide is a digital-product fit, not a KDP fit. KDP is paperback or Kindle. Also, a time-sensitive local guide on KDP would show stale data to buyers with no way to update for them except republish. UNVERIFIED on KDP-specific update behavior.

### 4g. Other risks to flag
- Medical, legal, tax statements (VA claims, state taxes, licensure) need "not advice" language and dated sourcing.
- Photos. Do not use Army, DVIDS, Google or Zillow images. Use Zac's own.
- Opsec and privacy. Do not publish unit-level details, gate schedules as a security product, or named people. No child data (as requested).
- Name and brand. Check USPTO for any title before it is chosen. Not done.

| Spouse licensure law, MyCAA rules | Yearly legislative session (GA January to April) | Military OneSource state policy, SECO |
| Lender and VA loan limits and rules | Yearly or on rule change | VA.gov, CFPB |
| Platform rules (Etsy, KDP, FTC) | Every few months, silent changes | Re-read before every re-listing |
| Cyber CoE and unit stationing | Yearly | Army pages, DVIDS |
| Economic impact numbers | Do not publish without date | Alliance, AEDA |
| Anything dated over 2 years in this brief | Possibly stale | Gate 1 2024 item is the only one |

Practical model. A dated edition, a "last verified" date on every section, a short refresh each January and each June to August (PCS season), and no hard numbers where a link works.
