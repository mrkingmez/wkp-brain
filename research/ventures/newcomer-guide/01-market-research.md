## 2. Demand

- Fort Eisenhower size: Wikipedia (citing the Army's Fort Eisenhower page, undated) says more than 16,000 service members and 13,500 civilians assigned, and about 30,000 employees (uncited). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Eisenhower. One base-guide site gives 19,992 active duty and 20,819 family members, undated. https://pcshub.us.com/base/fort-gordon. These disagree and neither is dated. Use "16,000 or more" as the safe floor.
- Yearly incoming soldiers: NOT VERIFIED. No source gave an annual arrival number. An undated AFCEA slide listed a Signal School annual student load of 19,774, but period and scope are unknown and most students are trainees in barracks, not house-hunters.
- Army move rate: a 2001 GAO report says about one third of all military members PCS each year, average about two years between moves (https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GAOREPORTS-GAO-01-841/html/GAOREPORTS-GAO-01-841.htm). It is 25 years old and all-service. A Task and Purpose item dated 2026 says the Pentagon is looking at longer tours (two years single, three years with family); I could not confirm it. If it happens, fewer PCS moves.
- Rough illustration only, my arithmetic, not a measured figure: one third of 16,000 would be about 5,000 arrivals a year, many of them single or in training. Do not use this as a forecast.
- PCS season: the Marine Corps says about 40 percent of moves happen May 15 to August 31 (DoD-wide figures also cited around 43 percent from SDDC; one older Navy-sourced article says over 65 percent). https://www.whiteman.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/836223/peak-season/ and https://int.moaa.org/content/publications-and-media/news-articles/2019-news-articles/marines-will-activate-reservists-as-part-of-plan-to-improve-peak-pcs-season. No Army-only figure found.
- Fit to Nov to Jan launch: that is the quiet season. Most buyers are searching May to August. Winter arrivals exist (training class cycles, mid-year assignments), but I have no number. Inference: expect low volume until spring, and a guide launched now mostly pays off next summer.
- Search interest: no data. There is a PCS Pay-It-Forward claim of 127,000 families served since 2016 across 115 plus installations (self-reported, https://pcspayitforward.com/about-pcs-pay-it-forward/). That shows people look for free PCS help in volume. It does not show they pay.
- Forum questions: I could confirm a Reddit thread on r/Augusta, "PCSing to Augusta GA", dated Dec 28 2024 (title only, from a mirror; I could not read the replies reliably) and a 2016 RallyPoint thread "PCSing to Fort Gordon looking for a place to stay". https://www.rallypoint.com/answers/pscing-to-fort-gordon-looking-for-a-place-to-stay.atom. Two threads is an anecdote, not demand. The RallyPoint one is stale.

That is the only defensible product. Whether anyone pays for it: not verified.
