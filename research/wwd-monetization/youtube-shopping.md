## Bottom line

- **Finding:** Studio product tagging is a real route for a small channel, but WWD cannot use it until it is in the YouTube Partner Program, and 744 subscribers is not enough for the full YPP bar of 1,000 subscribers. Whether WWD qualifies for any lower YPP tier is NOT AVAILABLE (see section 1).

## 1. Eligibility (YouTube help pages)

Source A: Get started with Shopping on YouTube, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12257682 (OBSERVED 2026-11-05).
- Own store path: channel must be in YPP and meet the YPP subscriber threshold (or be an Official Artist Channel). Page gives no number. Audience cannot be set to Made for Kids and the channel cannot have a significant number of made-for-kids videos. No Hate Speech strikes. No creator-country list on the page for this path (viewer locations are mentioned but not listed).
- Other brands (affiliate) path: YPP plus the YPP subscriber threshold, not a music channel, not Made for Kids, no active Community Guideline strikes, and the creator must be based in one of 12 locations: United States, South Korea, Indonesia, India, Thailand, Vietnam, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore, Brazil, Taiwan, Japan. United States is in the list.

Source B: YouTube Shopping affiliate overview, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13376398 (OBSERVED).
- YPP required, must meet the YPP subscriber threshold (no number given), non-music, not Made for Kids.
- Creator countries on this page: Argentina, Brazil, Germany, India, Indonesia, Japan, Korea, Malaysia, Mexico, Netherlands, Philippines, Poland, Singapore, Taiwan, Thailand, United Kingdom, United States, Vietnam. The two pages list different countries. US appears on both. Page says criteria may differ for pilot programs.

Source C: Google blog, announced March 30, 2026, https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/platforms/earn-earlier-expanding-youtube-shopping-to-creators-with-500-subscribers/ (OBSERVED): affiliate access for YPP members with at least 500 subscribers. It does not name countries and does not mention own stores.

Source D: YPP overview, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851 (OBSERVED, but the fetch looked truncated): full path is 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months, or 1,000 subscribers plus 10 million Shorts views in 90 days. ## 2. Limits and where tags show

Source F: Tag products in your content, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10191533 (OBSERVED).
- Up to 60 products per video or Short. Livestream cap: not stated.
- Long videos: a Shopping button on the video plus tags in the description. Shorts: a product sticker for the first product in the list, bottom-left by default. Livestreams: shopping icon. Shorts tags also show at the top of Google Lens results.
- Cannot tag: Made for Kids, copyright claim, limited or ineligible for monetization, or a Creator Music revenue-share track. Viewer must be in an eligible country. Tags do not work on mobile browsers, smart TVs or game consoles.
- Picking: Products tool, search by name, drag into place. YouTube may suggest products from its affiliate catalog based on title, description, captions. Studio, Earn, Shopping, Product Recommendations helps and you can save products for later.

Source G: Auto-tagging, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/17046000 (OBSERVED): for affiliate-program creators. Skips videos with existing tags, third-party claims, Made for Kids, music outside the Shopping Sounds library, and videos with paid partnership designations including sponsored content. Shorts get a single sticker. No product maximum stated.

Note: an earlier search snippet said 30 products. The official page I opened says 60. Use 60.

## 3. Connecting a store and the affiliate program

Source E: Connect your store, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12258186 (OBSERVED).
- Steps: Studio, Earn, Shopping tab (appears only if eligible), Get Started, follow prompts. Several stores can be linked. YouTube reviews products in about a few business days.
- Supported platforms: BASE (Japan), Cafe24 (Korea, Japan), FourthWall, Marpple Shop (Korea), Shopify, SHOPLINE, Spreadshop, Spring (formerly Teespring), Suzuri (Japan), TeePublic, Wix. Sales data in Analytics for Cafe24, Spreadshop, Spring, Shopify.
- Supported retailers (merch makers): 3Blackdot, Bonfire, Chico Rei, Cotton Bureau, Creator Ink, Creator Store, Fanjoy, Killer Merch, Represent, Shelf Merch, Teemill, Threadless and about 45 others. Full list on the page.
- Printful and Printify: not mentioned on the page. NOT AVAILABLE. INFERRED route: run a Shopify or Wix store with Printify or Printful behind it. That adds a store plan cost I did not verify.
- Shopify path: install the Google and YouTube app, connect the Google account, choose the channel, accept terms.

Affiliate details (Source B, OBSERVED):
- Sellers named on the page include Target, Walmart, Sephora, Nordstrom, Home Depot, Etsy, Samsung, Ulta. Page says this is not the full list; the full list is in Studio.
- Commission: each brand sets its rate and window per product. You earn when a viewer clicks a tag and buys on the retailer site. Page says a temporary incentive passes 100 percent of commissions to creators, with 30 days notice before it ends. Rates are shown in Studio under Earn, Shopping, Explore affiliate offers. Specific rates NOT AVAILABLE here.
- Paid through AdSense, 60 to 120 days after purchase. Returns reverse commission.

Amazon (Source H, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/17105501, OBSERVED): US creators in YPP and the affiliate program, with a YouTube channel linked to an Amazon Influencer or Associates account. Subscriber or view thresholds not stated. Pick from available Amazon products in the picker or ask Creator Support to add one. Commission rates not on the page. One channel per Amazon account.
