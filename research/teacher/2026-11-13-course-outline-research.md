## 3. Platform rules for selling AI-made products

Retrieved from the platform's own page unless marked.

**Amazon KDP** (primary, retrieved): [KDP content guidelines](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672410). AI-generated content is defined as "text, images, or translations created by an AI-based tool", and still counts as AI-generated "even if you applied substantial edits afterwards." Disclosure is required when publishing a new book or republishing an edited one. "You are not required to disclose AI-assisted content" (you wrote it, AI helped edit or error-check). Page shows no revision date. Third party claim of a three books a day cap is NOT verified.

**Printify** (primary, retrieved): [Intellectual Property Policy](https://printify.com/intellectual-property-policy), last updated 2026-09-17 on the page. Seller must review AI content and be legally permitted to use it: "You are responsible for reviewing any AI-Generated Content and ensuring that You are legally permitted to use it." Seller "assume[s] sole responsibility and accept[s] all risk." Printify's own warranties about ownership do not apply to AI content it provides. So Printify allows AI art, with all risk on the seller. I did not find a separate AI-art ban.

**Etsy** (primary NOT retrieved, etsy.com/legal/creativity and help article 4511 both returned 403 for my fetch). Secondary only, treat as unverified until someone reads Etsy's page in a browser:
- [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-bans-ai-patterns/) reports (paraphrase, no Etsy quote): on 2026-10-22 Etsy banned AI-generated functional guides such as patterns, recipes and instruction manuals, and fully automated AI listings with no individual human creative input, and required most listings to carry one unedited photo of the actual item. On 2026-11-07 Etsy softened the photo rule (cropping, resizing and watermarks allowed, no AI tools, one photo per listing enough, grid-pattern exception) and said proactive enforcement is planned for early 2027.
- [Etsy's Printify help article](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/4578896595345) exists on Etsy creativity standards, but the version I fetched had no AI detail.
- Older, from a search summary only: since July 2024 Etsy's standards require labeling human involvement and AI art is allowed with transparency, and selling AI prompts is prohibited. Unverified.

Implication: the unedited-photo rule matters for a physical product, and likely for printables mockups. Planners and printables could be affected if they count as "functional guides". NEEDS verification before the sell lesson is written.

## 5. Five most important findings

1. TripleTen's AI Software Engineering bootcamp is mostly traditional web development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, MERN) with only four of 28 weeks on AI-assisted coding, taught in JavaScript and TypeScript, not Python. No prerequisites, 9,800 dollars upfront per its own page. Confidence high on the page figures, but the programs page conflicts on duration (7 months versus 6).
2. TripleTen's separate AI Automation program (4 months, from 5,950 dollars) is closer to what Zac described: use AI tools, then build automations, then connect systems. Its sprint order is a good template for Track 3.
3. Etsy's primary policy page could not be fetched (403). Secondary reporting says AI-generated patterns, recipes and instructions were banned 2026-10-22, with changes on 2026-11-07. Someone must read Etsy's page in a browser before lesson 2.8 or 2.9 is written.
4. KDP and Printify rules are confirmed from their own pages. KDP requires disclosing AI-generated text, images and translations even after heavy edits, but not AI-assisted work. Printify allows AI art but puts all legal risk on the seller.
5. None of the beginner courses is clearly open for copying. Anthropic's course repo is CC BY-NC 4.0 (noncommercial, attribution), the others state no license. Link only, write original lessons. The Anthropic tutorial also needs a paid API key for most exercises.

Not verified overall: Microsoft course outline, Claude and Canva free-tier limits, free image tool terms, Clipchamp availability, and whether any TripleTen price is current.
