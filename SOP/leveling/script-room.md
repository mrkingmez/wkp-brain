## Step 1: Craft Brief (`memory\agents\briefs\script-room-craft-brief.md`)
Retrieve and quote (URL, retrieved date, verbatim quote). The list below is a starting set written without fetching anything: confirm each page resolves, drop dead ones, add better ones found. Write NOT RETRIEVED for any page that cannot be fetched. Never fill from memory.
- Published craft pieces by the Writers Guild or Film Independent with dates, on structure and dialogue
- Dated masterclass transcripts or interviews by named screenwriters (for example Gilroy, Sorkin, Mazin) on structure and dialogue
- Scriptnotes transcripts (johnaugust.com) on specific craft topics
- Documentary and video essay narration craft: a named practitioner with a dated breakdown (for Den Files and What If)
- YouTube Help or Creator Academy on hooks and retention
- WGA script format reference for formatting rules
Target 5 to 10 retrieved sources, at most 2 community. Local material to read as input, not as proof: `logs\script-room.md`, `.claude\agents\script-room.md`, `memory\agents\script-room.md`, pass logs in `reports\`.

## Step 2: Gap audit (`memory\agents\briefs\script-room-gap-audit.md`)
Audit the Ian trailer Draft 3.3 two-pass log (`logs\script-room.md`, clearance for Ford) and the Den Files EP 1 Fire in the Sky script. Which structural or dialogue notes would a retrieved source have forced earlier?
Finish with the 3 gaps that cost the most.

## Step 3: Drill (write the pass bar BEFORE running; file `SOP\leveling\drills\script-room-drill-1.md`)
Free drill: a 90 s teaser script from a short brief, then a graded second pass. Criteria written before the run: (1) hook in the first 5 s without giving the answer (teasers, not verdicts), (2) one clear spine, (3) voices differentiated (Matt verdict-first, Zac warm connective), (4) clearance checked and named, (5) both passes logged with what changed between them, (6) no em dashes and slop_check exit 0, (7) each craft claim tied to a retrieved source. Pass: 6 of 7, with 1, 5 and 6 CRITICAL.
Graded by qa-director with devils-advocate as second reader. Result saved beside the drill with date and grader.
