For WWD reviews (not FrostCast), never start a transcript of the raw recording. Wait for the transcript of Zac's edited cut.

**Why:** Zac, 2026-11-23, after I started a transcript of the raw Percy Jackson S3 recording and he stopped it: "That has a lot of cut time in there because I was having technical difficulties." The raw is full of dead time and retakes, so timings and content are wrong for run sheets.

**How to apply:** After a review is recorded, do the initial picture and clip pull only (transcript not needed), then wait for Zac's edited-cut transcript from Premiere before run sheet, stringout, beat-matched pictures or fact checks. FrostCast keeps its own chain (transcribe the downloaded episode). See [[feedback-take-initiative]], [[feedback-run-guide-not-straight-cuts]].
