## 1. What world-class looks like in this craft
1. A music supervisor shows options "in the same zone", not one answer, so we keep 3 ranked picks (safe, bolder, outside) per request. [Berklee]
2. A licence claim is only as good as the page it came from. Pixabay's own summary says "only the full Content License is legally binding," so a summary page is not the proof. [Pixabay summary]
3. The licence is not the same as no claim. A free-licence track can still be registered with Content ID, and the match gets a claim that blocks, monetizes or tracks the video. [YouTube Content ID help, Pixabay track pages]
4. Only one library is known to YouTube to be safe from claims: its own Audio Library. [YouTube Audio Library help]
5. AI-made tracks are labelled on Pixabay by rule, so reading each track page for the label is a hard gate, and the album or search page is not enough (our own 2026-11-20 FMA miss). [Pixabay terms 7B, lessons file]
6. Generated music rights depend on the plan at the time of creation. Suno: free is non-commercial, Pro or Premier downloads are assigned and perpetual. ElevenLabs: free is non-commercial, paid plans commercial, but the per-plan table adds "For Individual Use Only" that is undefined. [Suno ToS, ElevenLabs]
7. Cut to the cue, not to the beat. "never cut your film too closely to the beat" and an important shot can land on "a big change in your music cue". [PremiumBeat, community]
8. A sting under 6 s has to be measured, not assumed: a track page length is not a cut point.

## 4. What this changes in our work
- Every pick card quotes the track page licence line plus the date, and cites the full terms page, not the summary (Sources 1, 2).
- Gate: open the individual track page and read the AI label and the Content ID line. An "Ai Generated" tag is a REJECT for a WWD or client pick (Source 2, our 2026-11-20 lesson).
- Content ID is stated as one of block, monetize or track, with the Pixabay "Content ID Registered" line as the evidence (Source 4).
- For WWD uploads on YouTube, the only claim-safe route is the Audio Library inside YouTube Studio. Offer it as the safe pick when Zac can open Studio, and note it cannot be retrieved or receipted by an agent (Source 3).
- Suno spec line corrected: lapsed plan blocks only new tracks, old Pro tracks downloaded on a paid plan are perpetual (Source 6). Needs proof of plan date.
- Keep the ElevenLabs Individual Use question open, no output used for Ian or clients (Source 5). Free plan needs credit on distribution.
- CC BY tracks need a credit line, written into the pick card (Source 11).
- Keep a swap track on the same cue times in case a claim or takedown lands (Pixabay terms 11).
- Cut points on a downloaded file are measured with ffmpeg, never taken from the page length (own rule, PROPOSED as a craft rule, supported by PremiumBeat quote on cue points).
