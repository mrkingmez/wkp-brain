Zac 2026-11-26, on the Percy Jackson S3 cut: way too much solo cam. Solo cams are only for hitting a big point or every now and then, not every time someone talks.

**Why:** The pictures take up a lot of screen space, and the sound effects and fun transitions hit harder when the base is the two-shot.

**How to apply:** Default to the two-shot (about 85 to 90 percent of runtime). Solo cut roughly once every 45 to 75 s, held 4 to 10 s, on a punchline or key claim. Never follow the speaker or ping-pong. Pictures, clips, whooshes and transitions are the main visual rhythm.
