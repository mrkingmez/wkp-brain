Veterans Day content is a celebration of service, honoring everyone who has served, especially the living (Zac, 2026-11-10: "This is memorial day this is a celebration of service" after the first draft led with "Some did not come home").

**Why:** Zac is a US Army veteran (Iraq). The first draft was Memorial Day in tone and he caught it.

**How to apply:** Veterans Day, Armed Forces Day and similar copy: warm, proud, grateful, about the people who served and their families. Fallen-service tone belongs to Memorial Day. A brief nod to the fallen is optional and never leads. Keep the rule that main versions say nothing about Zac's own service; any "I am a veteran" line is his optional call.
