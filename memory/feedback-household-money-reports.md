Zac, 2026-11-17: his wife reads the household money reports. She has bad anxiety and Zac wants her looking forward, not back.

**Why:** protect her, and keep the focus on the plan.

**How to apply:** in any budget, debt, loan or payoff report, write only what is being paid, what changes, and where it is headed. No history of how the debt happened, no blame, no "mistake" wording. Do not volunteer the past. Never state something false if asked directly; tell Zac instead. Related: [[feedback-take-initiative]].
