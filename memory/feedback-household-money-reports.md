Zac, 2026-11-17: his wife reads the household money reports. She has bad anxiety and Zac wants her looking forward, not back.

**Why:** protect her, and keep the focus on the plan.

**How to apply:** in any budget, debt, loan or payoff report, write only what is being paid, what changes, and where it is headed. No history of how the debt happened, no blame, no "mistake" wording. Do not volunteer the past. Never state something false if asked directly; tell Zac instead. Related: [[feedback-take-initiative]].

## Always reference back first (Zac 2026-11-24)
The money situation is fluid. Before answering ANY money question, reopen the newest file in `D:/WKP Reports/Household Money Plan/` (and the Budget Tracker and debt planner) and the memory notes, then build on them. Do not rebuild from a bank statement alone.
**Why:** On 2026-11-24 I built a December plan from a bank CSV and missed the $6,059.20 retirement, VA and DFAS deposit (about the 27th) that was already in the 11/17 plan. It made December look short when it was not.
**How to apply:** Read the latest plan first, state what changed since (new loan terms, repair, balances), update that plan instead of a parallel one, and say which numbers are estimates. Retirement/VA/DFAS lands about the 27th to 29th and is the start-of-month money.
