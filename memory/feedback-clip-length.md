Zac, 2026-11-19: "when you are getting clips for the video they have to be longer, we can go for a whole 6 to 8 secs, sometime we can get away with 12 sec."

**Why:** 4 second clips were too short to use in the edit. He decides how much footage a review needs.
**How to apply:** clip-scout cuts 6 to 8 s, 12 s only when the shot needs it, inside one shot, muted, on a spoken line. No total cap was given: report total seconds, wwd-rights flags the exposure (longer clips carry more fair use risk). Specs updated in wwd-clip-scout, wwd-rights, wwd-run-sheet.
