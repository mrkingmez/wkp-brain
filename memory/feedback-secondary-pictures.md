When pulling secondary (gap-fill) pictures for a WWD review, pull pictures from the title being reviewed that we do not yet have a picture for. Do NOT pull pictures from other shows or movies unless that other title is a major feature point of the discussion (Zac 2026-11-10).

**Why:** Zac says the pulls are not working right. He expects to use only about two of the extras (the ones around USE 11 to 20), and what he wants from them is coverage of scenes, characters and moments from the reviewed title that have no picture yet. Other-show pictures were padding.

**Refined same day (Zac):** the point of the pictures is to cover characters, main plot points, and other cool things we can get on screen. In the Vision Quest pull he liked the Sonic picture (funny, and it fits the line) and did not like Commando (does not fit). So an other-title picture is fine when it is a real joke or reference the hosts make and it matches that exact line; it is padding when it only loosely connects.

**How to apply:** In Step 8 gap fill, first list every beat in the transcript about the reviewed title with no picture, and fill those from that title's stills (IMDb per-name galleries, TMDb backdrops, official press). Pull from another title only when the hosts spend real time on it as a main topic (a named comparison that carries a segment, a remake source), and say so in the report. Small passing mentions (an actor's other role named in one line) stay unpictured and are listed as gaps. Keep the no-repeat, 7 s max and no-actor-tags rules. See [[feedback-no-actor-tags]].
