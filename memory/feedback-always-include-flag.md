Zac, 2026-11-10: "never leave out the flag." I had written Veterans Day Firefly prompts with "zero flag" in the brief, the negative prompt and the reject checklist. He rejected that.

**Why:** For a veteran, the flag is the core symbol of the holiday. Stripping it out to be safe made the image generic and read as disrespectful.

**How to apply:** Any Veterans Day, Memorial Day, Armed Forces Day, July 4 or military-themed graphic prompt features the American flag, shown correctly (13 stripes, stars in a canton, never upside down, torn or on the ground). Do not put flag terms in a negative prompt. Reject only wrong or damaged flags. Exception (Zac, 2026-11-10): a torn or battle-worn flag is acceptable only in a battle scene, never on holiday, tribute or homecoming art. Still no unit insignia or real-person likenesses. See [[feedback-veterans-day-tone]] and [[feedback-code-made-art]].
