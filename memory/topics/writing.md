## No em dashes, nothing generic — Zac's general writing rule, not just fiction [2026-09-14]
Generalized the Shattered Empire "no em dashes" rule (above) to everything,
across every venture, plus a second rule: nothing generic. Zac's exact
words: "Remember my writing rule no emdashes and nothing generic, lets run
any thing going public goes through the no ai slop skill." Built the
`no-ai-slop` skill same day (`D:\WKP\.claude\skills\no-ai-slop\`) and
locked it into root CLAUDE.md's standing rules — every public-facing piece
of copy (social posts, sponsor reads, video descriptions, Etsy listings,
pinned comments, sponsor emails) runs through it before shipping.

**Why:** This isn't a style nitpick - it's what makes WKP content read as
written by actual people (Zac, Matt, the shop voice) instead of generic
AI-brand-voice output. Directly triggered by catching em dashes in two
just-drafted public deliverables (Surfshark sponsor reads, the weekly
social calendar) that hadn't shipped yet.

**How to apply:** Applies everywhere public-facing, including my own
chat responses to Zac when drafting or quoting copy. Internal files
(TASKS.md, memory, logs, DECISIONS.md) are exempt - this is about what a
stranger reads, not working documents. See [[marketing-sponsors]] for the
Surfshark-specific fix this triggered.

## Blender serves two different mesh standards [2026-07]
Cinematic mesh tolerates non-manifold geometry and arbitrary scale. Print-ready
mesh must be manifold, in real-world millimeter scale, with minimum wall
thickness, exported to STL. Confirm which one is wanted before advising.
