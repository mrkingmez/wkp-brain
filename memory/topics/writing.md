# Writing and Content Formats

## Watershed and Hidden Hinge never mix [2026-07-24]
The two What If formats are locked and distinct. Format A (Watershed) is a
nine-step predictive branch methodology. Format B (Hidden Hinge) is a causal
chain correction format. Mixing them is a hard error, not a style choice.

## Shattered Empire editorial rules [2026-07-24]
No em dashes. No colons in suggested lines. "God" stays. The author has final
say on every suggestion. Book 1 is *The Patience of the Dark*, draft 2 complete
and with alpha readers. Book 2 runs a three-faction structure.

## Zac writes by discovery, not outline [2026-07-24]
Character-driven and non-outlined — he finds the story as he goes. The
Ribbon/Nessa decision is the illustrating case. Do not push outline structure
onto fiction work unless he asks for it.

## Blender serves two different mesh standards [2026-07]
Cinematic mesh tolerates non-manifold geometry and arbitrary scale. Print-ready
mesh must be manifold, in real-world millimeter scale, with minimum wall
thickness, exported to STL. Confirm which one is wanted before advising.
