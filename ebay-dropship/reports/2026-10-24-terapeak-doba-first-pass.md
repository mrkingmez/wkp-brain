## Read
Generic Doba organizers cost too close to the eBay sold price, so most fail the 20 percent floor. The squeeze is the known dropship problem, not a tool problem.
Prices on Doba cards marked "From ... Prepaid Label" are the warehouse pick-up price, the real cost is the higher number on the product page. Always read the product page.
