# eBay: best profit margin picks, US shipping only (2026-10-19)

Zac's ask: find the best profit margin and show the products, US-based shipping only.

## Read this first
No real product list can be proven yet. No supplier price sheets, no Terapeak or Seller Hub sold data, and no supplier has been confirmed to accept a sole proprietor without a resale certificate. Product costs below are ASSUMED. The two searches run today found vendor blogs only, with no verified US-warehouse supplier names or margin data. Treat this as a ranked shortlist to verify, not a list of winners.

## What is verified
- eBay fee: 13.6 percent of the sale, plus 0.40 per order over 10 dollars (eBay fee page, checked 2026-10-10).
- Our rules: 20 percent net margin floor, 5 dollar minimum profit, 10 percent supplier tax buffer (sole proprietor, no certificate).
- That means wholesale cost plus shipping must be about 50 to 55 percent of the selling price or less. Anything selling under about 20 dollars cannot clear the 5 dollar floor.

## Ranked shortlist (US-only, no brands, no electronics)
