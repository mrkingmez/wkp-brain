    schtasks /Create /TN "WKP-System-Check" /TR "cmd.exe /c D:\WKP\scripts\nightly-system-check.bat" /SC DAILY /ST 23:30

To disable the retired daily brief task: `schtasks /Change /TN "WKP-daily-brief" /DISABLE`

To add the Saturday wrap at 5:30 AM: `schtasks /Create /TN "WKP-Saturday-Wrap" /TR "cmd.exe /c cd /d D:\WKP && claude -p \"run wkp-saturday-wrap\"" /SC WEEKLY /D SAT /ST 05:30`
