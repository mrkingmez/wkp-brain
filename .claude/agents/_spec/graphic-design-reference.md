## 2026-10-30: THE DEN AFTER DARK character sheet prompts
Source: WWD\reports\Comic-Series-Art-Prompts.md. Nothing generated yet, so nothing here is confirmed to work.

Design rules adopted (untested, confirm once Zac hands results back):
- One locked style line pasted at the front of every prompt, plus a shared negative line.
- Text and lettering added afterward in Express, never trusted to the image model.
- 4:5 Instagram panels are generated at portrait 3:4 and cropped, subject kept to center 80 percent. Whether Firefly offers 4:5 directly: not verified.
- Real people are never named. Reference photos are attached instead, and prompts describe from the bible text only.
- Firefly image model name on Zac's account: not verified. Partner models (Nano Banana Pro) for multi-reference: not verified.
