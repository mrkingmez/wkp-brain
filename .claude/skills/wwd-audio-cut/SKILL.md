# FrostCast Audio Cut



Input: a diarized, elapsed-timestamped transcript.

Output: a cut document. Not audio. A document Zac edits from.



## Where to save it



Save to:

L:\\Winter Wolfs Den review show\\Audio\\podcast Frostcast audio files\\



Filename: FrostCast ep ### YYYY-MM-DD.md

### is the episode number from the transcript or filename.

YYYY-MM-DD is today's date, the date the document is created.

Example: FrostCast ep 107 2026-08-25.md



If the episode number cannot be determined, STOP and ask. Do not

guess a number and do not save the file without one.

If the L: drive path is unreachable, save to D:\\WKP\\WWD\\audio\\ and

flag it clearly in the report.



Target finished runtime: 55 to 70 minutes. Heavy cuts are expected.



## THE HARD RULE



Cuts may only REMOVE. Never reorder. Never splice two separated

statements into one. Never cut mid-thought.

If removing a section makes a later reference unintelligible, cut

both or neither.

The finished episode must be an honest record of what was said.

Violating this is worse than shipping nothing.



## Output format



\*\*Target runtime:\*\* \[figure]

\*\*Source runtime:\*\* \[figure]

\*\*Cold open:\*\* \[timecode in] to \[timecode out] - why this hooks



\*\*KEEP\*\*

\[in] - \[out] | \[what it is] | \[why it earns its place]

...ascending order...



\*\*DROP\*\*

\[in] - \[out] | \[what it is] | \[why it goes]

...ascending order...



\*\*Continuity check:\*\* anything in KEEP that references something in

DROP. Name it. Say whether to restore the DROP or also cut the KEEP.



## Choosing the cold open



Pull from anywhere in the episode. It must stand alone with no setup,

be under 45 seconds, and make someone want the next hour. A verdict,

a disagreement, or a reveal beats a scene-setter.



## What to cut first



Dead air, restarts, technical talk, in-jokes needing video, anything

referencing something on screen, tangents that do not return, and

repeated points.



## What never gets cut



The headline story's spine. Any moment where Matt and Zac disagree.

The close and tease.



## Escalation



No timestamps or not diarized? Say so and stop. Do not estimate timecodes.



Report the full path of the saved file. Never return the cut document 
in chat only. If the save fails, say so plainly.
