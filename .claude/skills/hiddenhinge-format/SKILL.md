# Hidden Hinge - Format B

Worksheet"

## What defines this format
Backward-looking causal-chain correction. "You were taught battle X
mattered because of outcome Y. Battle Z, same era, actually drove more
of Y. Here is the dependency chain."

Military events specifically. Not open to non-battle subjects.

## NEVER MIX WITH WHAT IF
No branch probabilities. No percentage verdicts. This chain is already
resolved. It needs a causal-dependency case.

## Screen the candidate first - cheap
Run mil-research's causation-only variant against the famous event
before committing research time. If the causal chain collapses under
an adversarial pass, the idea is dead for the cost of one prompt.
This catches a failure mode the five-axis score misses.

## The eight questions - answer all, sourced, before scripting

1 The famous battle X - what outcome is it credited with?
2 The overlooked battle Z - what actually happened?
3 What factors drove Z's outcome?
4 How does Z causally feed the conditions that produced X?
5 If Z had gone differently - does X still happen, happen
  differently, or never happen?
6 What does popular history get wrong about X's significance?
7 What do multiple countries' historiographies say about X and Z?
8 Historical Plausibility Score 1-10 for the causal chain, and why.

Answering all eight IS the devil's advocate pass. It is built into the
structure. Do not skip to scripting with six answered.
