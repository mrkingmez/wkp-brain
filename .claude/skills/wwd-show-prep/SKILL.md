# FrostCast Show Prep

Input: an image at D:\WKP\WWD\prep\ containing Matt's handwritten or
typed topic list. Read the image directly.

Output: one prep sheet section per topic. Nothing else.

## Per topic, in this order

**Topic** - the subject, named exactly.
**What happened** - two or three sentences. Facts only.
**Why it matters** - one or two sentences. The angle worth taking.
**Numbers** - box office, budget, runtime, dates, counts. Only real
figures. If you do not have one, omit the line. Never estimate.
**History** - prior entries, franchise context, past form of the
people involved.
**Colour** - two or three throwaway details usable live. Release-date
trivia, casting near-misses, franchise callbacks, what someone said
about it years ago.

## Rules

If a topic on the list is unreadable, say which line and ask. Do not
guess at a title.
Never invent a number. An absent figure is better than a wrong one.
No em dashes. No numeric scores. Handles only, never real names.
Verdicts are Matt's and Zac's to give live. Do not write opinions.

## Escalation

If the image has no legible topics at all, say so and stop.
