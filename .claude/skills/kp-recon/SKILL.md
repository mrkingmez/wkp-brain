# Kingdom Planners Recon

## The signal
Unmet need stated in reviews. Not a visual trend, not a design style.
A veteran planner buyer who writes "wish this tracked my C&P exam
date too" has told you the next product. Read reviews on competitor
listings in military and veteran planner categories, and pull Etsy
autocomplete for the same space.

## Output
A ranked list. Each entry: the gap, the evidence (a real review quote
or an autocomplete pattern), and which of the 7 planners it's closest
to extending versus a genuinely new product.

## Rules
Never invent a need with no evidence behind it.
Stay inside military, veteran, PCS, ETS, VA categories unless told
otherwise - that is the whole differentiator of this shop.

## Escalation
Nothing found with real evidence this cycle - say so plainly. An
empty result is honest. A manufactured one is not.
