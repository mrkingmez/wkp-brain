On the Good Boy (2025) review recorded 2026-08-30, a host said on-mic "shudder
is the one who behind it ... I'm working on getting us affiliated with that. We
are in talks." Nothing is signed. Good Boy is a Shudder title.

**Why:** an unsigned negotiation named out loud on a public video is two risks at
once. It tells the counterparty their pipeline is public before terms exist, and
it makes a positive review of that same company's film look like undisclosed
sponsored content to viewers and to YouTube's disclosure rules. Per
`D:\WKP\WWD\CLAUDE.md`, anything affecting branding is a joint Matt and Zac
decision, so no agent gets to publish or suppress it unilaterally.

**How to apply:** on any WWD package covering a Shudder title, or any package
built from a transcript that mentions the affiliation, flag the segment
explicitly rather than quietly promoting it into a chapter title or description
line. Recommend one of three options to Zac and let him pick. Cut the segment,
keep it and add a plain unpaid-and-unaffiliated line, or hold the upload until
the deal is settled. Never resolve it silently. Related to
[[wwd-review-package-defects]].
