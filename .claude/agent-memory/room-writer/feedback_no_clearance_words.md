Do not use clearance-sounding words as claims in beat sheets, production packages or copy: no "SAFE" as a version name, no "LOCKED" for beats/lines, no "clean", "cleared", "none found", "none of its elements appear". Say what was checked and against what (for example "checked only against the typed summary; nobody here has the book"). The no-quote fallback version is called "base cut" (Ian trailer, 2026-11-05); SLOT keeps its name.

**Why:** the 2026-11-05 third read on the Ian Griffin trailer (clearance bench) flagged these as false clearance claims; the writing room standard says clearance never clears anything, and these labels travel to clients.

**How to apply:** any GENERATE or revision run. Also keep anything that names a book's secret outright in `work\repo-only-notes.md`, never in files that leave the repo. Brand rule covers every text surface (prompts, post copy, captions, pinned comments, alt text, file names, hashtags), not only prompts.
