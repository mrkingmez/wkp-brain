## 3. Parody vs satire

Not parody, and should not be marketed as parody. COLD TOW comments on genre conventions and typecasting in general through invented wards. That is genre commentary or satire, not parody of one work. It does not need a parody defense because it borrows no specific expression. If a later ward borrows a specific film to mock it, that issue's parody-or-satire status becomes live and gets its own read.

## 4. Commentary and review content

## 5. Ownership and AI protectability (L299 to L300)

- **AI art.** US Copyright Office Part 2 report (29 January 2025): prompts alone are not enough human control for authorship; human selection, coordination and arrangement can be protected. D.C. Circuit in Thaler v. Perlmutter (2025) upheld the human-authorship requirement; the Supreme Court denied review on 2 March 2026, so that ruling stands. Practical exposure: someone could lift the panel art and WKP may have little to enforce.
- **The script.** Written by the writing room (AI), rebuilt this pass from AI reviews' quotes (L5). Same human-authorship issue applies to the words until Zac, Matt or Gabby materially rewrite them. How much rewriting is enough is attorney work.
- **Ownership.** The Callback, Dale, Truck Nine, Marrowgate, the wards, Dustcover, Tanner Hill drive-in are new. KingZ, Winter Wolf and Oracle are existing personas. No written answer yet on WWD partnership versus WKP versus the individual. Gabby is not a partner as far as the repo shows; her persona is hers. Close before Issue 1 posts.
- **Tool terms.** Firefly's commercial terms and any indemnity cover Adobe's own models only. Partner models inside Firefly (Veo, Kling, Runway, Luma) and any other tool carry their own terms. Log which model made each panel.

## 6. Meta labeling (Instagram and Facebook)

Secondary sources only. No official Meta help-center page retrieved this pass. Verify on Meta's own pages before posting.
- Meta's "AI info" label (renamed from "Made with AI" in July 2024) is applied when a creator toggles it, and automatically when C2PA metadata is detected. Firefly embeds Content Credentials, so expect auto-labeling.
- Mandatory self-disclosure covers photorealistic video and realistic audio. A stylized comic carousel is not plainly in that category. The "AI info" toggle at upload is the cheap control; WWD packages already carry an AI disclosure box.
- One report (single source, undated) says Instagram is testing an account-level "AI creator" badge that could sit on the whole WWD profile. Not confirmed. If true, it is a channel-wide branding effect, a Zac and Matt call.
- No established reach penalty for a correct label in the sources found; not confirmed by Meta.
