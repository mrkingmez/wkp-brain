## 5. Judgment calls

**J9 (NEW). Line 45, the second-person suspicion beat.** "So now you're one of the six who came back without him, and your whole story is a light in the trees." This replaced the sheriff claim. It states no fact about any official and makes no accusation; it asks the viewer to imagine the crew's position. "Your whole story is a light in the trees" does characterize the crew's account as thin, which leans slightly toward doubt. Described, not concluded: this sits well on the opinion and framing side, much further from the line than d01 line 95 did, and it attaches to the group, not a named man.

**J10 (NEW). Line 82, the closing question about the crew.** "Forget the light. Why'd that truck turn around?" The writer's note (line 94) says it has two open readings. One is loyalty (they went back for their friend, which line 38 sets up as the episode's core). The other, for a viewer primed to doubt, is that the return was part of a story. The line does not say which, and Matt explicitly refuses a verdict (lines 76 to 82). Described, not concluded: this is the residual of J1. It is an open question rather than an either/or naming a lie, which is a real reduction, and how it reads in the end depends on what the full episode argues.
**What would reduce the open question:** the same fact-research pass J1 asked for (each crew member's current public position), logged before recording.

**J11 (NEW, accuracy). Line 62, "Ninety-three, they make the movie."** "They" follows straight on from the crew's polygraphs (line 49), so a listener can hear it as the crew made the movie. The film was a studio adaptation, so that reading would be a small inaccurate claim about identifiable people. Low exposure. Flagged for the room as an accuracy point; not a script fix this bench supplies.

**J2 (OPEN).** Line 69, see section 1. The film-versus-account claim now sits in one line instead of three, which is less weight, but the claim is the same and still unresearched.

**J5 (OPEN).** Line 87, title use. Unchanged from pass 1.
