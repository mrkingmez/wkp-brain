# THE WRENCH TURNER, book trailer v2, draft-01

Mode: GENERATE. Room-writer first draft, not yet read by the benches.
Sources used: Ian's typed summary and back cover (scratch\ian\wrench-turner-summary.txt) and the Amazon listing facts. Nobody here has read the book. Anything not in those sources is marked ASK IAN.

**BLUF:** This version tells the book's story out loud, in order: a boy under a car with his dad, the Army, Iraq, a wound, two years of rehab with his father beside him, and a man who comes home and goes back to the garage. It stops there. No secret, no ending.

---

## A. Pitch

Brandon Turner learned everything that mattered lying on a garage floor next to his dad, handing up the wrench. Then he went to war. He lost his squad leader in Iraq, took the man's place, and got wounded. He spent two years in rehab with his father in the chair beside him the whole time. The trailer walks a stranger through that life in plain words and real places, then lands where the back cover says the book really starts: Brandon home, broken, standing in the garage in front of the old car he and his dad worked on. It ends on the book's own promise, "even something broken can be fixed," and sends the viewer to Amazon to find out how.
