### 2c. Medical and military imagery (new this pass)

- **Red Cross emblem.** Medevac cabin (line 30, item 9) and hospital corridor (line 31, item 10). Stock and generated medical scenes often carry a red cross on white, on stretchers, aircraft walls, or signs. The emblem is protected by statute and treaty and is not a generic medical symbol; use in commercial work is restricted. The draft bars unit patches and Army logos (line 115) but does not name this. Frame-check for it.
- **Aircraft markings.** A military medevac interior can carry Air Force or Army markings, tail numbers, or stenciled unit text. Same frame-check as insignia. Nothing in the draft implies Army or DoD endorsement; keep it that way.
- Helmet "no insignia" (line 29), no rank, name tapes, or logos (line 115). Unchanged from pass 1.
- Real places: BRAGG, GERMANY, IRAQ (lines 28, 52, 65). Places only, shown neutrally, no business depicted. Landstuhl is out. Not a flag.

- Realistic war and hospital scenes likely fall under YouTube's synthetic-content disclosure and Meta and TikTok AI labels. Whose account posts decides who ticks the box.
- No faces anywhere (lines 30, 34, 115) lowers lookalike risk. Does not remove it.
