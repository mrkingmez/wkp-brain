- YouTube description: 1 to 3 short lines, then exactly 5 hashtags on the final line. Hashtags are high-volume discovery tags for the clip's topic. Never #FrostCast or any channel-branded tag. No non-ASCII.
- Instagram: 1 to 3 short lines, up to 5 hashtags. Facebook: 1 to 3 short lines, 0 to 3 hashtags. YT, IG and FB copy must differ from each other.

- Opinions stay opinions: write "the hosts say / think / call it", never state a host opinion as fact. Rumors stay rumors.
- Do not state facts the hosts did not say. Do not add box office numbers, dates, or names beyond the list under each clip.
- Spoiler clips (spoiler:true): the YT, IG and FB copy must open with a spoiler warning for Vision Quest episode 7, and must NOT reveal the reveal.
