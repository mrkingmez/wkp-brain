## Character bible for the art team (one page, visual description only)

Palette: black and blood red base. Cold blue is Winter Wolf's eyes. Red glow is KingZ. Violet and cold white is Oracle. Wax red and white is the villain. Torch amber lives inside the Den only. The city outside is red, black and rain gray. Gritty noir with a little humor in the faces. Match the EP094 page for hosts.

**KingZ.** Masked face, red eyes. Crown cap. Hoodie with KINGZ in dripping red lettering. Always holding something (mug, game console, case). Posture open: arms wide, leaning toward the reader, pointing. Takes the most room in a panel.

**Winter Wolf.** A wolf. Black hooded jacket, WINTER WOLF across the back, fur collar, blue eyes, the only cold blue in the frame besides Oracle. Posture still: arms crossed, a clawed hand flat on things, leaning back. Smaller gestures than KingZ.

**Oracle (look is open, Zac and Matt decide).**
- Option 1: a glowing, faceless circle carved into the Den table. Pale cold-white light on deep violet. The carved lines brighten when she speaks. No body, no face.
- Option 2: a hanging lantern of old frosted glass with a violet flame inside that flickers when she speaks. Hangs over the table. No face, no figure.
Balloons: round, tailless, thin clean serif, pale text on violet. Never a face, never a silhouette that suggests a real person.

**The Den.** A hideout in a brick stairwell hut on a rooftop above a rainy city. Heavy door with a torch-lit gap under it. Inside: one long wooden table, mugs, mics, scattered notes, torches on brick, WINTER WOLF'S DEN banner on the wall, rain on the windows. Warm amber inside, cold red and gray outside.

**The Last Word (villain).** Tall, narrow, slow. Long oilskin courier's coat, flat brimmed cap, a satchel of sealed black envelopes, gloves that drip hot red wax. His head is a cast-iron and brass wax-seal stamp, blank and worn, no screen, no glow, no eyes. Wherever he touches, red wax pools and sets. He never speaks. His captions are white ragged-edge boxes. He is never drawn winning and never opens an envelope.

Never draw: any other company's characters or logos, real newspaper mastheads, Oracle's real name, a recognizable console shape, a screen-headed figure.
