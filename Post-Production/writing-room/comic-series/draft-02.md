## Villain rebuild: THE LAST WORD

Pass 1 problem: "villain who spoils endings" (Henry Danger Spoiler, Simpsons Spoiler Boy) and a screen for a face (Skibidi TV Man, Rorschach silhouette).

New motive: he does not tell anyone how anything ends. He wants the talking to stop. He hates the debate. He believes every argument should be closed with one official answer, and he seals shut whatever keeps people arguing: doors, mics, mouths. The Den is the loudest room in the city, so it is his target. This is the show's own line turned into a plot: "Tell the truth. Have the debate."
- Nothing is ever spoiled. The envelope holds a "final word" nobody ever reads. Stamp stays ENDING ENCLOSED (ending of the argument). If originality dislikes that stamp, fallback stamp is FINAL WORD.
- Look: tall, narrow, long oilskin courier's coat and a flat brimmed cap, a satchel of sealed envelopes. His head is a cast-iron wax-seal stamp, a heavy brass-and-iron signet with a blank worn face pressed into it, no screen, no glow. Where he touches, hot red wax pools and sets. Wax drips from his gloves. He is slow, walks like a mail carrier at the end of a long route.
- Rules kept: never speaks (his "voice" is the thud of the stamp and a wax-white caption box with a ragged edge), never wins, no envelope ever opens.
- Name: THE LAST WORD. Pages say it in his caption box only.
