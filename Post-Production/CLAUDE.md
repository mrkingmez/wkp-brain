## Pricing
Historical model: $40/hour, first hour billed flat (not prorated), every hour after is prorated. Zac is open to restructuring — e.g., hourly for editing (unpredictable scope), package/flat rates for sound design tiers, per-project for color grading (scope usually known upfront) — but the actual working method is **budget-first negotiation**: quote based on where the client's budget sits, then negotiate from there. Flexibility matters more to Zac than a rigid rate card, as long as time spent doesn't lose money.
