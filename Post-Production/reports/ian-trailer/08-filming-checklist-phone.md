# Wrench Turner: filming checklist for your phone

Practical, night-friendly shots only. Source: package Part 3, section G(1). The script fixes from the third read change captions and one cutdown, not what you film. Daylight shots (beat 9 under the old car, beat 6, beat 8b) wait for a weekend morning.

## Set up first
- On the PC make D:\Ian Trailer\ with folders 01 Phone, 02 Firefly, 03 Audio, 04 Stills, 05 Premiere, 06 Exports.
- Phone camera, Video mode. 4K at 30 fps, horizontal. Flash off. Wipe the lens.
- Lock focus and exposure: press and hold on the ball until it says locked.
- Phone on its side on a folded towel on the floor. Lens 2 to 4 inches up, propped with a paperback.
- Lamp low and to one side. Tape over any badge or plate.
- Use a worn plastic ball. The real ball spec comes from Ian, so this is a stand-in.

## Every shot
- Press record, wait 3 seconds, do the action, wait 3 seconds, stop.
- 3 takes each. Say the shot number out loud at the start of each clip.

## The shots
1. Shot 1: drop the ball so it bounces once and wobbles under the bumper edge. 6 takes.
2. Shot 2-i: phone beside the creeper or a blanket, your hand on the ratchet. About 3 seconds in, stop turning and reach left for the wrench. No child's hand in frame.
3. Shot 3: ball over the garage door lip into dark grass, in a lamp pool.
4. Shot 5a: boots, a tote with cards on top, roll the ball between the boots.
5. Shot 7a: clipboard with plain paper. Photograph the blank page, then fill the lines in stages with 3 pens (scribble signatures only, no readable names), photographing after each stage.
6. Shot 7b: a chair, your folded hands in the lap, ball under the chair.
7. Shot 8a: bench, ratchet, a taped log with a line that stops mid-sentence and a pen across it. No readable name. Dust lightly with flour or talc, brush off extra. Cool white lamp bounced off a white sheet.

## Wild sound, 10 seconds each
- Ball bounce, ball roll, ratchet clicking slowly, pen scratch, cards slap, tote lid, kettle ticking.

## File names (rename at the PC)
- Video: IAN_01_PH_t3.mp4 (shot number, take number).
- Sound: IAN_SFX_ballbounce.m4a.

## After
- Copy the phone folder to 01 Phone, then a second copy on another drive.
- Do not delete from the phone until both copies play.

## Do not do
- No faces, no readable text, no Ford logo or pony, no uniform marks, no Army patches or rank.
- Do not generate anything in Firefly tonight without reading the credit number first.
