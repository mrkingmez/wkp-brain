L165 says every word on screen is set by us in edit. The two logs break that: they are handwritten text inside the image.

If it reads as a first and last name, either it is the father's name from the book (needs Ian, the summary never gives it) or it is invented (an invented book fact, and possibly someone's real name). Real maintenance logs name brands (Ford, Motorcraft, oil and parts brands, a parts store), mileage, a plate number or a VIN. A readable year dates the story and can contradict Ian's timeline (see J4).
