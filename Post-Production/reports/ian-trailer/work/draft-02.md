## 1. Concept

The book opens and closes on the same small event: a ball rolls under a car where a man is working. The trailer is built on that object. We stay at floor level the whole time, the height of somebody lying on a creeper. The ball is the only thing that moves between chapters of Brandon Turner's life, and each time it rolls out of one place it rolls into the next. It passes his dad's hands, a party where everyone learns his girlfriend has moved on at the same moment he does, a barracks floor, a street in Germany where he is the one leaving, and the sand, where it stops. After that it is a hospital and a house, and the ball sits still under things. Then grown Brandon is back on his back under the old car on a bright ordinary morning, garage door open somewhere off frame, birds outside. The ball rolls in out of the daylight and taps his shoulder. His ratchet stops. He does not turn. He breathes out, the way you do when something you forgot comes back to you. The trailer ends on that feeling, something coming back, and then Ian's own back-cover words. It does not stage a mystery about a person.

### Recording spec for Ian (plain words, phone)

1. **What to read:** L1, L2, L3, L4 in order. If we are using your book line, read L1a, then your line, then L2 to L4. Then read L4-cut. Then read each line again on its own, three times, with a short pause between.
2. **Room:** the smallest, softest room you have. A walk-in closet full of clothes is perfect. A bedroom with the door shut is fine. Not the kitchen or bathroom (hard walls echo). Turn off the AC, fans, TV and anything humming. Put the dog out.
3. **Phone:** the voice memo or recorder app. Airplane mode so no call or buzz cuts in. Set it on a pillow or a stack of books, not in your hand.
4. **Distance:** about one hand-width, 6 to 8 inches, from your mouth, with the phone a little off to the side so your breath does not hit it straight on.
5. **Pace:** slower than feels natural. If it feels a bit too slow to you, it is probably right. The time windows already allow for that.
6. **Takes:** three full read-throughs, plus the three-times-each pass on every line. If you stumble, stop, wait two seconds, and start that line again. Do not stop the recording. We cut the stumbles.
7. **Before you start:** record 10 seconds of the room with nobody talking. We use that to clean the noise.
8. **Send it:** email or text the original file. Do not trim it, do not send it through Facebook or WhatsApp (they squash the sound).
