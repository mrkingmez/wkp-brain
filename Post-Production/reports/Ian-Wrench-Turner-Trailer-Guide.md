# The Wrench Turner Book Trailer: Working Guide
Client: Ian Griffin (author, retired Army). Producer: WKP Post-Production, editor Zac. Prepared 2026-10-23 by the writing room (script-room) and marketing-director. PLAN ONLY. Nothing has been posted, spent or promised.

## 1. The short version
- **Recommended concept: A, "The Ball."** One ball rolls under a red Mustang and keeps rolling through a deployment, a heartbreak, a war, a hospital and a funeral, until it taps a grown man's shoulder under the same car. Ian narrates. We never see who rolled it.
- **Why:** the ball is the book's own bookend, it reads with the sound off in one second, the hero shots are cheap, and Ian's own voice is the one thing a template trailer cannot copy.
- **Package (Better tier):** 45 to 60 second vertical master, 15 s and 6 s cutdowns, silent captioned version, 16:9 and 4:5 cuts, cover still, end card, clean no-text file, .srt, and a one-page posting sheet.
- **WKP cash cost:** about 0 to 25 dollars. The tools are owned. Zac's time is the real cost, about 23 hours.
- **Suggested price:** Good 250, Better 550, Best 950 dollars. Friend rate on Better: 350 dollars with a testimonial. Or free as a portfolio piece, with a written scope.
- **Timeline:** about 3.5 to 4 weeks from files received. Veterans Day (11 Nov 2026) is 19 days out, so a Veterans Day cut only works if Ian's answers and files arrive this week.
- **Hard rule from Ian:** the plot secret (who Logan is) never appears. Not in the video, captions or hashtags.

- 0:43 to 0:50 Grown Brandon alone under the Mustang in the cover pose. The ball taps his shoulder. He does not turn. We never see who rolled it.

- 0:55 to 1:00 End card: cover, IAN GRIFFIN, where to buy, iangriffinbooks.com, handles, the dragon crest in the corner.

### Required changes from the writing room (originality pass)
- Cut the zipped body bag and the folded flag. The flag also reads as killed in action, and his father died at home.
- Restage the wound beat without rotor noise or white-out.
- No solo acoustic guitar. Use garage ambience, then one sustained instrument or silence into the tap.
- Keep grown Brandon in the cover pose, with no Gran Torino posture.
- Replace the stock "That was the problem" sample line.
- Get the father's exact line from Ian. It is what makes him a person and not a truck-ad dad.
- The grieving-veteran, classic-car, neighbor-kid setup sits close to Gran Torino and a few holiday car ads. We break it with execution, and the secret, which cannot be shown, is the only unique premise element.

## 4. What we need
**From Ian:** original high-res cover (ideally the art without the type), a clean author portrait with written permission (the banner file is a photo of a banner), a clean QR or just the URL, buy links and release date, award names and years, review quotes with names (we never invent quotes), his voice recorded on a phone in a quiet room, his tone and "do not" list, written confirmation the secret stays out, his deadline, and who posts.

**From WKP:** edit, sound design, mix, cover animation (parallax, headlight glint, dust), animated type (every word on screen is set by us, since Firefly garbles text), captions, end card, cutdowns, reframes and the delivery sheet.

**What we generate:** garage plates, hands on a wrench, a small hand holding a wrench (hand only), the ball rolling, back-of-head or silhouette shots. No logos, no readable plates, no child faces, no likeness of Ian beyond his supplied photo. Firefly image-to-video from a start frame, 2 to 3 takes per beat, 8 to 12 usable clips.

**Practical shots Zac can film in one afternoon:** a ball, a creeper, a wrench and car inserts.

## 7. Risks and what blocks us
**Stop items (clearance, nothing is cleared yet):**
1. Quoting the book text word for word (the father's line, the back-cover sentence, Ian's banner quote) needs Ian's written confirmation that he holds the text rights.
2. Ford marks: the FORD hood letters, the pony and the Mustang plate show on the cover. In Concept B they sit under the title for the whole runtime, which is why B is dropped. In A it is a judgment call. Options: de-badge or crop the car in the present-day shot, or accept the exposure after an attorney answers. No readable plates on any real car Zac films, and the car owner's permission is needed.

**Open items:**
- **Cover art origin:** unknown (AI, stock or commissioned). Animating it needs its license.
- **Dragon crest with a 7:** no matching Army unit found. Ask where it comes from. The CSM chevrons should show without "Ret." only with his OK.
- **AI labels:** YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram and Facebook each have AI-content label rules, reported by third-party sources. Check each live page before upload. Keep a list of which shots are AI.
- **AI soldiers:** they invent fake Army tapes and patches, so check every frame.
- **Children:** no generated child faces. A real child model needs the release scope checked.
- **Music:** the loose mp3s on P: have unknown origin. TikTok business accounts are limited to its commercial library. Meta's library is Meta-only.
- **Ian's portrait:** the photographer's license is unknown.
- **Claims:** "multi-award-winning" and "Available now on Amazon" must be true and current.
- **Platform specs:** all came from third-party blogs and conflict. Confirm on official pages and by private test uploads. 4:5 and 1:1 specs are NOT verified.
- **Paid-promo disclosure:** only if the trailer posts on WKP, WWD or other Zac-controlled channels.
- **Attorney:** the starred exposures (Army marks, text rights, Ford) need an attorney or an Army trademark licensing contact. This guide is not legal advice.

## 8. Questions for Ian (send this week)
**The secret**
1. Confirm in writing that nothing about Holly, the pregnancy, the daughter, Logan's identity or the accident appears anywhere, captions and hashtags included. Is mentioning a neighbor kid at all off limits? (Our default is yes, off limits.)
2. Is the back-cover line the only tease, word for word?
3. May the ball roll to grown Brandon at the end, as long as no child or second person appears with it?

**Story and book**
4. The exact wording of the father's "wrench turner" line.
5. Is the cover car the book's car? Year, color, model.
6. Year and place of the boyhood garage scene.
7. Year and season of Brandon's return, his branch, job and unit type (we will not invent one).
8. Can the trailer show combat, the wound and the medevac, or keep war off screen? How should PTSD appear, if at all?
9. Is any character or event, especially the fallen squad leader, based on a real person?

**Rights and assets**
10. Who holds rights to the book text and back-cover copy? Any publisher, audio or media deal?
11. Who made the cover, was it AI, what is the license, and does it allow animation? Do you have a layered version without type?
12. Where does the dragon crest with the 7 come from? May we show it and the CSM chevrons?
13. Who shot your banner portrait and what does that license cover? Can you send the original file?
14. Is the Ford and Mustang use on the cover cleared, and do you have a note on showing the car?

**Production and selling**
15. Will you narrate? Can you record 20 to 30 seconds on a phone in a quiet room? If not, are you OK with a hired or synthetic voice, which we would disclose?
16. Comfort with AI images, separately an AI voice, and do you want a disclosure on the post?
17. Tone: quiet and emotional or heavy and cinematic? Songs or composers you like, and anything you do not want?
18. Where do people buy (Amazon, your site, both), in which formats, and is the book out now or is there a launch date?
19. Which awards and years may we name? Any reviews we may quote, with the reviewer's name?
20. Which platform matters most, who posts, and is any of it boosted as paid ads? Can you send three screenshots of your top posts?
21. Deadline, and do you want a Veterans Day (11 Nov 2026) version?
22. Have you had a trailer before, and what did you like or hate? Who approves, and are two revision rounds OK?
