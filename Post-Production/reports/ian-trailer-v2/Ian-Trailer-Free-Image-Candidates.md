## Read this first

- Licenses below were read on Wikimedia Commons file pages or from Commons file metadata (the same license field the page shows). They were not read on the original DVIDS or Army.mil pages.

- Library of Congress item pages returned 403, so LOC items are listed from the LOC "Free to Use and Reuse" set pages only. LOC says "rights-free" and "free to use and reuse" on those set pages, but the item-level rights line was NOT VERIFIED.
- Video clips: not searched this pass. Gap.
- DVIDS own policy (read at dvidshub.net/about/copyright): DoD imagery by government employees is not copyright eligible, but some may be owned by others "regardless of whether the VI is marked." Publicity and privacy rights of people shown are not waived. For commercial use show the non-endorsement line "The appearance of U.S. Department of War (DoW) visual information does not imply or constitute DoW endorsement." and obscure distinctive military markings. Add that line to the trailer credits for every military item.
- Public domain for a US government photo covers copyright only. It does not clear a person's likeness, a trademark, or a unit insignia.

Not allowed: use "especially Content which features recognisable people" in an immoral or illegal way, use as a trademark or business name, standalone resale, misleading use. Client-work limits, plainly: these are free licenses, not public domain, and none of them gives a model or property release. For a paid client trailer, use only images with no recognizable person, no readable brand or logo, and no readable text. A person who is recognizable needs a signed release, which these sites do not supply.
