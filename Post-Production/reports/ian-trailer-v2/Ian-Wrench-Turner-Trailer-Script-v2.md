# THE WRENCH TURNER, book trailer v2, final script

For: Ian Griffin. From: Warrior King Productions.
Sources: Ian's typed summary, the back cover, and the Amazon listing. Nobody on our side has read the full book, so anything we could not check is in the ASK IAN list.

**BLUF:** One spine in three lengths (60, 30, 15 seconds). A boy under a car handing his dad the wrench, the Army, leave, Bragg to Germany to Iraq, a wound, two years of rehab with his dad in the chair beside him, a hard homecoming, and the garage. The voiceover is paced at about two words a second and tells a stranger this is a novel. It ends on a question, and a back-cover card answers it. Three things need Ian's written OK before anything airs: the two quote cards and the back-cover wording in one voiceover line.

---

## A. Pitch

Brandon Turner learned everything that mattered lying on a garage floor next to his dad, handing up the wrench. He joined the Army, came home on leave to find his girl had moved on, then went Bragg to Germany to Iraq. He lost his squad leader, took the man's place, and got hit. Two years of rehab, and his dad never left the chair beside him. The trailer walks a stranger through that life in plain words and leaves him in the garage with the old car and the same wrench in his hand, not sure where to start. It closes on a back-cover fragment, "even something broken can be fixed," and tells the viewer this is a novel on Amazon.

Voice: plain, unhurried, one man talking across a kitchen table. Best read is Ian's, at his own pace, not a slowed trailer drawl. No "narrated by" anywhere unless Ian asks. Say it the way he would say it. A short beat between blocks, not after every sentence.
