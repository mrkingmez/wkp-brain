# THE WRENCH TURNER, Firefly prompts for the trailer stills and clips

For Zac. Source: Ian-Wrench-Turner-Trailer-Script-v2.md (Section B master table, Section F footage list). Nothing has been generated and no credits spent. Zac clicks generate.

**Plan in one line:** make one approved still per shot, then animate each still into a 4 to 6 second clip. Text, quote cards, place cards and the end card are built in the editor, never in Firefly.

---

## 1. Look bible (6 lines)

1. **Film look, every shot:** 35mm film, visible grain, shallow depth of field, muted palette, slightly soft highlights, no glossy shine.
2. **Light:** dim warm work-light (amber, one bare bulb, deep shadow) for the boyhood garage, kitchen scenes and the final garage. Flat gray daylight, low contrast, for the hospital, the desert and the medevac cabin. Night field and dusk porch use cool blue with a few warm practical lights.
3. **Frame:** generate 16:9 master. For 9:16, the crop keeps only the center third of the width, so put every subject in the middle third.
4. **THE WRENCH (one prop, always the same):** worn steel combination wrench, about 10 inches, dull gray, scuffed, open jaw on one end and a box ring on the other, smooth shaft with no stamping, no brand, no numbers.
5. **THE PEGBOARD:** gray painted pegboard with white painted outlines of tools, outlines only, no labels. Almost every hook is empty. The wrench hangs on its own outline, center.
6. **THE CAR (generic classic American coupe):** two-door coupe, long hood, short rear deck, rounded fenders, faded red paint, dull and dusty. Smooth bare body: no chrome trim, no badges, no grille emblem, no hood ornament, no license plate, no model-identifying trim. Covered version: faded gray-tan canvas dust cover. **THE DUFFEL:** plain dark olive canvas duffel with a rope handle, no stencil, no patch, no name tape.

## 2. Hard rules (apply to every prompt below)

- No readable faces. Children and the father are shown from behind, as hands, or as lower legs only.
- Never name a car make, model or brand in a prompt. Use "classic American coupe" only.
- No Army unit insignia, name tapes, rank, or flags with readable text. The helmet has no insignia.
- No Red Cross emblem or aircraft markings, tail numbers or stencils in the medevac and hospital shots.
- No real person likeness. No text in any generated image.
- Every image prompt ends with the negative line: **No text, no letters, no logos, no badges, no readable markings, no faces.**
- Video settings for all clips: audio off (sound is built in the edit), 1080p, 24 fps, 16:9, start frame = the approved still from the same shot.

**Models.** Images: **Firefly Image** (Adobe's own, commercially safe), 16:9. Video: **Veo 3.1** for realism and physics, **Kling 3.0** when the clip must hold the start frame exactly (hands and tools). Menu is from Zac's account (memory, 2026-10-25). Not verified: which durations and end-frame options each model offers, check the panel.

## 4. Consistency tips

- Generate Shot 3 first (the pegboard). Once one of its 4 options is approved, upload it as the style and composition reference for every later still.
- Approve the car once (Shot 12 or Shot 14), then use that image as the reference for any shot with the car.
- Reuse the same seed where the panel offers one (Advanced > Seed for video; check the image panel). Write the seed down per shot.
- Generate 4 variations per shot, pick one, and run the video from that exact still as the start frame.
- Fix wrong hands or text by re-running the generation, not by editing the image. Re-run costs another generation, so budget it (below).
- Keep the wrench, pegboard, car and duffel wording word for word from the look bible. Do not paraphrase.

## 5. Plan B for shots Firefly will likely fumble

| Shot | Problem | Fallback |
|---|---|---|
| 2, hands passing the wrench | Finger count, the wrench morphing | Generate only the child's hand with the wrench and the adult open palm as two separate stills and cut between them on the clink. Or use one still of the adult palm, flat, empty, then a close-up of the wrench in a child's fist. |
| 1, child's lower legs | Legs fuse or the child gets a torso | Show only the sneakers (feet on the concrete, cut above the ankle). |
| 8, medevac interior | Red Cross or markings appear; a glow; faces | Drop to a close on the gloved hand, IV line and a gray blanket only, no cabin in frame. If markings still appear twice in a row, use plain owned or licensed stock of a generic cabin. |
| 12, garage door rolling up | Doors rarely roll right | Skip the roll. Start on the cover being pulled, and cover the door with its sound in the edit. |
| Quote cards, place cards (BRAGG, GERMANY, IRAQ), end card | Firefly garbles text | Built in the editor over Shots 3, 14 and T. Never generated. |
| 6, party crowd | Faces turn to camera | Replace with a wide of string lights over the tailgates, no people. |
