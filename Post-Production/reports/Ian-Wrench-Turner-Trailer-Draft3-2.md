## 3. Firefly and Veo plan for two ratios

### 3a. The rule for each shot

For every shot there are three ways to get a 16:9 version.

1. **TRUE 16:9.** A new Veo generation in 16:9 from a 16:9 start frame. Costs one or more Veo runs.
2. **BLUR-SIDES FRAME.** The 9:16 clip sits at full height in the middle of the 16:9 frame, sharp, no stretch, no crop. A second copy of the same clip is scaled up behind it, blurred and dimmed, so the sides are the clip itself and not black bars. Costs zero runs. The sharp picture is 607 pixels wide on a 1920 wide screen, so it is a narrow picture. Fine for a 3 to 4 second shot. Not fine for the whole trailer.
3. **CENTER CROP.** Cutting a 16:9 band out of a 9:16 clip. NOT USED anywhere, because it throws away about two thirds of the picture and the upscale is soft. Premiere's Auto Reframe is also NOT USED (it crops).
