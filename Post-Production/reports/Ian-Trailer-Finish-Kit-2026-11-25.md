### How to do the pieces
**Set a still's length.** Drag the still to the timeline. Right-click it, Speed/Duration, type the length as timecode (for 3 seconds 12 frames type 3:12). Or drag the right edge. NOT VERIFIED wording.

**Fit a 16:9 still to the frame.** Right-click it, Set to Frame Size. If it does not fill the frame exactly, pick Scale to Frame Size instead. Never crop a still.

**Push a still (Ken Burns).** Click the still. Open Effect Controls (Window menu if it is hidden). Open Motion. Put the playhead on the first frame of the clip. Click the stopwatch beside **Scale**. Type the start number. Move the playhead to the last frame of the clip. Type the end number. Play it. It should move slowly.

**Cross Dissolve.** Effects panel, search **Cross Dissolve**, drag it onto the cut between two clips. Double-click it on the timeline. Type the duration. Alignment: **Center at Cut**. NOT VERIFIED dialog.

**The hand-off (beats 3 and 4).** Put R3 and R3b side by side on V1 as in the table, then one Cross Dissolve of **2 seconds** between them. Its midpoint is 10:06 (10.25 s). Pause there. You should see ONE wrench and ONE palm. If you see two wrenches, R3b has drifted a few pixels. Click R3b, Effect Controls, Motion, **Position**. Change it by 1 or 2 pixels at a time until the wrench and palm sit on top of R3's. Then **Scale** in steps of 0.5. Only the giving hand is supposed to change. Never use a video model for this move.

**The rip (beat 8).** Drag **W1-rip-v3-paper.mp4** to V1 so its first frame sits at **25:00** and its last frame at **27:23**. Do not trim it and do not stretch it. No transition on either side. Look at the full frame once for readable truck badges. If you can read one, tell us.
